# 2026 product-video scripts: dosing discrepancies with labels

As of 2026-10-06.

Nine of the 2026 website product-video scripts give dosing that differs from
the label directions on the current product pages. For each row below, please
confirm which is correct: change the script, or tell us the label is due to
change.

LeanMeal is the one that looks like a mistake in the script. The others look
like updated advice.

## Discrepancies

| Product | Label directions | Script says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LeanMeal | 2 scoops per serving | "Mix one scoop", though the script's own 170 calories and 21 g protein match the 2-scoop serving |
| GlutamineComplex | 1 scoop after the workout, plus 1 before bed | 5 g three times a day (15 g total), up to 0.1 g per lb of body weight |
| Advanced Brain Health | Ages 45–55: 1 serving a day; over 55: 2 servings | Ages 56–65: 2 servings; over 65: 3 servings |
| CollagenComplex | Skin: 2 capsules a day | Skin alone: 1 capsule a day |
| Creatine Monohydrate | Under 175 lb: 1 scoop a day; over 175 lb: 2 scoops; no loading phase | 5-day loading phase at 4 scoops a day, then 2 scoops a day (1 under 175 lb) |
| AminoFormula | Over 150 lb: ¾ scoop before and after; also gives dosing for non-exercisers | 150–200 lb: ¾ scoop; over 200 lb: 1 scoop; no dosing for non-exercisers |
| FirstString | 2 scoops before and 2 after training | 1.5, 2 or 3 scoops by body weight |
| Omega3 | 1 softgel a day | 1–2 softgels a day |
| ThermAccel | Do not take within 5 hours of bedtime | "Within several hours of bedtime" |

  recommendations. The current label directions say only one softgel daily

- **ActiveMV, Women'sMV, Over50MV:** the labels still say "use daily with
  dotFIT Super Calcium+", the old name for CalciumComplex. The scripts already
  use CalciumComplex.
