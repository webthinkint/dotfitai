D-mannose is a simple sugar closely related to glucose that is naturally produced by your body and found in fruits like cranberries and oranges. It is widely used as a dietary supplement to naturally prevent and manage urinary tract infections (UTIs) by preventing harmful bacteria from sticking to the urinary tract. [1, 2]
How it Works for UTIs
Most UTIs are caused by E. coli bacteria. These bacteria have hair-like structures (pili) with sticky tips that allow them to cling to the walls of your urinary tract. [1, 2, 3, 4]
The Decoy Effect: When you take D-mannose in supplement form, it is quickly absorbed into your bloodstream, filtered by the kidneys, and sent to your bladder.
Flushing the Bacteria: Because the bacteria are naturally attracted to D-mannose, they bind to the sugar molecules instead of your bladder walls. The bacteria are then harmlessly flushed out of your body when you urinate. [1, 2, 3]
Benefits & Uses
UTI Prevention and Management: Clinical studies show D-mannose is a highly effective, natural supplement for preventing recurrent UTIs and managing the symptoms of active, uncomplicated bladder infections without relying on antibiotics.
Gut Health: It can act as a prebiotic, feeding beneficial bacteria in your digestive tract and helping to reduce gut inflammation.
Rare Genetic Conditions: It is medically used to treat an inherited disorder called Carbohydrate-Deficient Glycoprotein Syndrome Type 1b. [1, 2, 3, 4]
Dosage & Side Effects
How to take it: It is available in powder or capsule form. For UTI prevention, common dosages range from 1.5 to 3 grams daily.
Safety: D-mannose is naturally processed and is generally safe, even for long-term use. It is processed differently than glucose, meaning it has a minimal impact on blood sugar levels.
Potential Side Effects: Because it is a simple sugar, consuming high doses can occasionally cause mild gastrointestinal symptoms like bloating, gas, or loose stools. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
