At 250 lbs, you need 250 grams of protein daily as your starting point. Now ideally we'd work off your lean body mass specifically, but until we know your exact body fat percentage, that's your target number.

Step 2: Body Composition is the Real Goal
Here's what I want you to understand - we're not just chasing weight loss. We want fat loss while preserving and building muscle. Your body weight on the scale matters less than what that weight is made of.
Measure your body fat every two weeks to make sure it's moving in the right direction.

Your body composition is simply a product of your intake AND your output. You've already got the output covered with your training.
