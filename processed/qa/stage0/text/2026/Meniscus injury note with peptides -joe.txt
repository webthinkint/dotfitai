Note: BPC-157 and TB-500 are not used to "heal" a torn meniscus, especially if the tear calls for surgery (large, displaced, etc.)
The rationale for using them before surgery is that they may help:
Reduce pain and inflammation 
Improve healing of surrounding soft tissues (capsule, ligaments, tendon attachments) 
Potentially enhance healing in the outer ("red zone") portion of the meniscus where blood supply exists 
Improve function and allow better participation in physical therapy
Potentially reduce the need for surgery in some minor tears

For your knee, BPC-157 is a reasonable starting option to discuss with your physician, while TB-500 may be considered/added for more significant tissue/tendon/ligament involvement.
