Vegetable stearin (often used interchangeably with vegetable stearate or magnesium stearate) acts primarily as a lubricant and flow agent in supplement tablets and capsules. It prevents ingredients from sticking to manufacturing machinery and ensures uniform, high-quality tablets by keeping ingredients from clumping. 
Healthline +3
Key functions and details include:
Lubricant: It stops tablets from sticking to equipment during high-speed production.
Binder: It helps bind ingredients together to maintain tablet integrity.
Source: It is derived from plant oils, such as palm oil or cottonseed oil.
Safety: It is generally considered safe and non-toxic, with note that the amounts used in supplements are far below dangerous levels, as outlined on this Dr. Axe page.
