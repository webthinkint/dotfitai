FTC: AdvoCare business model was pyramid scheme
Consumer Advice | Federal Trade Commission (.gov)
https://consumer.ftc.gov › consumer-alerts › 2019/10 › f...

Jul 5, 2022 — Today, the FTC announced a case against Texas-based AdvoCare International, a multi-level marketer of energy drinks, shakes, and supplements .Read more


AdvoCare is no longer considered a multi-level marketing (MLM) company, having shifted to a single-level direct selling model in July 2019. Following discussions with the FTC, they stopped compensating for recruiting and now only pay distributors based on product sales to consumers. 
Key details regarding this change include:
Pre-July 2019: AdvoCare operated as a traditional MLM structure.
Post-July 2019: The company restructured, eliminating the multi-level compensation plan.
