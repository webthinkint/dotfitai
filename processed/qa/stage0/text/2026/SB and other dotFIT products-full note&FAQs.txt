FAQs and more information
Q: If I order the Alln1 Super Blend, does that replace the MV over 50, Super Omega and Super Calcium? 
A: You do not need to take your Over50 multivitamin and mineral formula (MVM) when using the Alln1 SuperBlend™ (SB). Although the SB contains omega-3s, it is plant source, thus acts slightly different in the body than a marine source, such as the SuperOmega-3. Therefore, you can continue using your SuperOmega if you do not consume fatty fish regularly and your SuperCalcium if not getting 1200mg/day from diet.

Q: If someone is using the dotFIT health supplements and wants to switch to the Alln1 SuperBlendTM (SB) what will they no longer need?
A: They will no longer need the Multivitamin, SuperiorAntioxidant, UltraProbiotic, Digestive Enzymes because those formulas are in the SB. They may add these additional dF health products as needed: SuperOmega (SB has a plant Omega-3 -see below FAQ) and extra V-D (SB contains 1000Ius but sometimes more is needed to reach >30ng/ml). If recommended, you can add BrainHealth, JointSkinCollagen+, and SuperCalcium, as these are purposely not in the SB because not everyone of all ages would necessarily need these. All other dotFIT products (e.g., performance, bodyfat/weight loss, proteins, bars, etc.,) can be added as usual.

Q: I am taking the Alln1 SuperBlendTM (SB) daily, is there any need for me to keep taking the SuperOmega-3 fish oil? 
A: If you are not eating at least 2svgs/week of fatty fish, then yes, you should continue to take the dotFIT Super Omga-3 because it is marine Omega-3, which has complementary actions to vegan/plant omega-3 sources. Although the SB contains omega-3s, it is from flaxseed to keep the product vegan friendly for pure vegan clients, giving others the option of adding a marine source. Again, for basic health protection, if you are eating ≥8oz of fish weekly, you do not need to add the SuperOmega-3 as you will have a healthy dose of all omega-3 sources. For extended athletic purposes (e.g., recovery, performance, body composition, etc.,) the dosage of a marine source (Super Omega-3) is generally 3-6gm/d, which would likely require additional supplementation based on fish intake (EX: if eating less than 8oz/wk and using SB, add 3-4 SuperOmega-3s).
