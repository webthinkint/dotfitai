IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 Insulin-Like Growth Factor-1) is a synthetic analog of IGF-1 designed to have a much longer half-life and greater biological activity than natural IGF-1. It has an added 13-amino acid extension and a modification at the 3rd amino acid position, which reduces binding to IGF-binding proteins and allows it to remain active in circulation far longer. 
Its primary mechanism of action is direct activation of the IGF-1 receptor on muscle and other tissues, stimulating anabolic signaling pathways such as PI3K/Akt/mTOR and MAPK. These pathways increase protein synthesis, satellite cell activation, muscle cell proliferation, glucose uptake, tissue repair, and recovery while also reducing protein breakdown. 
The primary uses of IGF-1 LR3 are typically related to:
Supporting lean muscle growth and recovery 
Enhancing tissue repair after intense training or injury 
Improving nutrient partitioning and body composition 
Research into muscle regeneration, metabolism, and anabolic signaling 
Because IGF-1 signaling also influences cellular growth broadly throughout the body, there are important safety considerations, including potential effects on blood glucose regulation and theoretical concerns regarding abnormal cell proliferation. Most legitimate clinical discussions emphasize medical supervision and careful monitoring. 
Dosage
There is no medically established or FDA-approved dosing protocol for IGF-1 LR3 in healthy individuals for muscle growth or performance enhancement. Most dosing information comes from experimental use, peptide clinics, and bodybuilding communities rather than large controlled human trials. 
That said, the most commonly referenced “performance-support” dosing ranges are:
20–50 mcg daily as a conservative/common range 
50–100 mcg daily in more aggressive experimental protocols 
Typical cycle lengths discussed are 4–6 weeks, followed by time off to reduce receptor desensitization risk. 
The rationale behind these doses is to activate IGF-1 receptors enough to increase:
muscle protein synthesis, 
satellite cell activation, 
recovery, 
and nutrient uptake, 
without excessively increasing side effects such as hypoglycemia, edema, nerve compression symptoms, or unwanted tissue growth.
