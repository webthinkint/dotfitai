Tesamorelin is FDA-approved for lipodystrophy and commonly used safely. Other off-label uses are based on emerging research and clinical experience rather than FDA-approved indications.
While Human data is limited for off-label usage, (e.g. reduction of visceral fat in non-HIV individuals; improvement of body composition; support during age-related declines in GH secretion; potential preservation of lean mass during fat-loss phases, etc.,) empirical (practical human experience) is strong favoring safe results
Because peptides mimic natural substances, when they are metabolized in the body, their constituent AAs can be recycled with little to no stress on organs or metabolism unlike most drugs
