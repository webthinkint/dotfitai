BPC-157
Since you are not treating a specific injury, the BPC-157 can be systemically dosed with the goal of overall body tissue repair and used for 5weeks followed by a 2-week tapering off period. Injections have superior results compared to oral administration. The latter only exists because it’s unusually stable in gastric juice.
Systemic Injection (non-site-specific) is common when injuries/pain are spread out such as in your case, age-related slower intense exercise recovery.
Goal: Systemic connective-tissue support and general whole-body recovery enhancement through:
Nitric oxide signaling modulation
Angiogenesis
Growth-factor pathway interaction
Gut–brain–immune axis support
Dose
First 5 weeks: 500mcg per day
Week 6: 250mcg per day; week 7: 250mcg 2 times evenly spread, such as Monday & Thursday
Week 8 off until further notice
Recommended injection site: subcutaneous abdomen; thigh or glute – your choice and may alter
Tesamorelin (your dosing is to be discussed if you choose to use)
Growth Hormone (GH) secretagogue a step above the popular sermorelin because it is longer acting and more robust in increasing GH pulses and raising insulin growth factor-1 (IGF-1), which carries out what GH initiates (e. g., building muscle, burning more fat, etc.). This will be administered like the BPC-157 (systemically injected) and used for a total of 20weeks followed by a 4-week tapering period and subsequent bloodwork.   
Systemic Injection (non-site-specific) for your goal of building muscle and reducing visceral (belly) fat
Goal: increase natural GH release to optimize its axis including build muscle/lose fat through:
Stimulating the pituitary gland (PG) to release GH (more pulsing) and subsequently raising lipolysis (fat burning) and IGF-1 levels and sleep-related GH pulse enhancement

Growth-factor pathway interaction including supporting LBM

Notes

BPC-157 is not FDA-approved for human therapeutic use but commonly used safely
Tesamorelin is FDA-approved, but only for specific lipodystrophy (abnormal distribution of fat)
Human data are limited; most mechanistic evidence comes from animal models
Because peptides mimic natural substances, when they are metabolized in the body, their constituent AAs can be recycled with little to no stress on organs or metabolism unlike most drugs.
