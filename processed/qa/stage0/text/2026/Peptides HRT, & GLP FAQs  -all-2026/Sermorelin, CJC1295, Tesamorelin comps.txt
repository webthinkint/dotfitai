Simple Client Script (30–45 sec)
“Sermorelin helps your body produce more of its own growth hormone—the hormone responsible for recovery, muscle maintenance, and fat metabolism. Instead of injecting hormones directly, it turns your system back on, which is why it’s often used for healthy aging and staying strong over time.”

High-Level Positioning Summary (Trainer-Friendly)
Sermorelin → “Gentle, physiologic GH support for recovery, sleep, and aging.”
Tesamorelin → “Stronger GH signaling with unique visceral fat benefits.”
CJC-1295 → “Performance-focused GH pulse amplification.”
GH → “Hormone replacement—not stimulation—with higher risk and medical necessity.”

Practical Trainer / Educator Takeaway
GH peptides stimulate—GH replaces.
Peptides depend on a functioning pituitary and preserve natural hormone rhythms, while injected GH bypasses regulation and carries higher metabolic risk.
Decision Tree for clients and trainers
“Which Peptide Fits Which Client Goal?”
Decision Flow (Trainer / Educator Use Only)




START → Clarify the Primary Goal (not the symptom)
1️⃣ Is the client’s main goal sleep, recovery, or healthy aging?
Reports poor sleep quality
Slower recovery from training
Wants to preserve lean mass with age
No expectation of rapid body recomposition
➡ Best Educational Fit: Sermorelin
Why: Gentle, physiologic GH stimulation that supports natural nightly GH pulses
Trainer framing: “Supports recovery and aging processes—subtle, long-term.”

2️⃣ Is the primary goal visceral fat reduction or metabolic health?
Central / abdominal fat is the concern
Blood markers trending unfavorably
Already resistance training and eating well
Interested in clinically validated outcomes
➡ Best Educational Fit: Tesamorelin
Why: Strong GH signaling with unique evidence for visceral fat reduction
Trainer framing: “Metabolic-focused GH support with specific fat distribution benefits.”

3️⃣ Is the primary goal performance, physique, or training output?
Already lean and training hard
Focused on recovery between sessions
Wants enhanced anabolic signaling
Accepts that effects are adjunctive, not magic
➡ Best Educational Fit: CJC-1295
Why: Amplifies GH pulse amplitude and duration, often discussed in performance settings
Trainer framing: “Performance-oriented GH pulse enhancement—not beginner friendly.”

4️⃣ Is the client seeking fast, dramatic, or guaranteed results?
Wants rapid fat loss or muscle gain
Asks for “the strongest option”
Less interested in lifestyle fundamentals
➡ Correct Response: No peptide discussion yet
Why: Expectations misaligned; peptides will disappoint or create risk
Trainer framing: “We need to earn advanced tools by mastering the basics first.”

5️⃣ Does the client have diagnosed GH deficiency?
Endocrinologist involved
Lab-confirmed deficiency
Medical replacement indicated
➡ Appropriate Category: Growth Hormone (GH)
Important distinction:
GH = medical hormone replacement
Not a fitness or longevity supplement
Requires physician management
Trainer framing: “That’s medical therapy, not a performance or wellness peptide.”

Quick Decision Summary (One-Look Box)
| Client Goal | Educational Fit |
| Sleep, recovery, aging | Sermorelin |
| Visceral fat, metabolic health | Tesamorelin |
| Performance, physique | CJC-1295 |
| Acute injury repair | ❌ None of these |
| Rapid transformation | ❌ Reset expectations |
| GH deficiency | GH (medical only) |

Trainer Guardrails (Critical)
Peptides are experimental adjuncts
They do not replace:
Progressive resistance training
Adequate protein / EAAs
Micronutrient sufficiency
Sleep & recovery hygiene
Trainers do not prescribe, dose, or administer

Trainer-Friendly Close
