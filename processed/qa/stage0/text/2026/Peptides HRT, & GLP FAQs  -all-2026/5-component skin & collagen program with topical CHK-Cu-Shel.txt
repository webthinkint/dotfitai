Skin health and youthful appearance goal-- 5-compponent program
For the specific goal of maximizing collagen production, preserving existing collagen, and improving the appearance and quality of aging skin a multiple ingredient intervention may look like the below. The important point is that they work through different mechanisms, so this isn't simply five versions of the same treatment. Mindful, this should approved, assembled and monitored by a dermatologist (the will tell you tips and tricks for applying Tretinoin).
5-component skin & collagen program
| Component | Practical use | Primary purpose | Evidence |
| 1. Tretinoin / retinoid | Tretinoin 0.025–0.05% at night, beginning 2–3 nights/week and gradually progressing toward nightly use as tolerated | Stimulates new collagen, improves collagen organization, reduces collagen degradation, wrinkles and photodamage | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 2. GHK-Cu topical | Apply once daily, potentially progressing to twice daily depending on formulation/tolerance | Signals fibroblasts and supports collagen/elastin production, extracellular-matrix remodeling and repair | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| 3. Vitamin C topical | ~10–20% L-ascorbic acid in the morning | Antioxidant protection + essential cofactor for collagen synthesis; helps photodamage and pigmentation | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 4. BioCell Collagen peptides  oral | ~2–4, 1000mg caps daily | Collagen peptides, hyaluronic acid & chondroitin sulfate to improve joint comfort, mobility, & recovery; enhancing skin elasticity, hydration, and reducing wrinkles and fine lines | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 5. Sunscreen | Broad-spectrum SPF ≥30 every morning, preferably SPF 50 with significant outdoor exposure | Prevents UV-induced collagen breakdown and protects the gains from everything else | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
Tretinoin deserves the central position. A systematic review of randomized trials found that topical tretinoin improves photoaging and induces new collagen formation, with treatment periods extending from several months to two years. 
Morning
Cleanse → Vitamin C → GHK-Cu → moisturizer if needed → SPF 30–50
Vitamin C is particularly attractive because it isn't merely cosmetic. In a controlled facial study, topical vitamin C produced visible improvement after 12 weeks, and biopsies demonstrated increased collagen and increased type-I collagen gene expression. A systematic review likewise found improvement in wrinkled/photoaged skin, although the literature isn't strong enough to establish one universally optimal concentration. 
Evening
Cleanse → tretinoin → moisturizer
Keep GHK-Cu primarily in the morning so you don't need to pile multiple active ingredients on the skin simultaneously.
For someone new to tretinoin, 0.025% 2–3 nights per week is a reasonable conservative starting approach under a dermatologist's guidance. Increase frequency gradually as the skin adapts. More irritation isn't synonymous with more collagen production.
If prescription tretinoin isn't appropriate, an OTC retinol/retinal product is an alternative, although tretinoin has the stronger clinical evidence.
Oral collagen: BioCell peptides have been shown in multiple clinical trials to improve joint and skin health, including reducing lines, wrinkles and improving skin hydration and circulation: Use 2-caps in AM and 2 in PM for first 60days then can reduce to 1-cap twice daily.

Note on GHK-Cu
GHK-Cu has a compelling mechanistic rationale for fibroblast activity, collagen/elastin production, tissue remodeling and repair, but the clinical evidence ranks behind tretinoin and UV protection. Therefore, it would be considered an adjunct that potentially makes an already strong collagen program better, rather than the foundation of the program.
For now I would avoid injectable GHK-Cu simply because the goal is maximum collagen. Currently no good human evidence demonstrating that injecting 1–2 mg/day, for example, produces greater dermal collagen accumulation than topical GHK-Cu.

Strategy Summary:
Tretinoin → stimulates remodeling and new collagen
GHK-Cu → provides additional repair/remodeling signaling
Vitamin C → supports collagen synthesis + antioxidant protection
Collagen BioCell peptides (and daily protein) → supplies nutritional substrate/signaling
Sunscreen → prevents UV from destroying the collagen you're trying to build – don’t ignore the sunscreen!
Practical 12-month approach: run all five consistently, evaluate photographs and skin quality at baseline, 3 months, 6 months and 12 months, and don't judge the program after a few weeks. Collagen remodeling is slow. Oral collagen trials generally show measurable changes after 8–12 weeks, while retinoid-driven dermal remodeling can continue for many months. 
For a serious anti-aging skin program, think of it this way: tretinoin + daily UV protection the non-negotiable foundation, with vitamin C, GHK-Cu and collagen peptides layered on top.
