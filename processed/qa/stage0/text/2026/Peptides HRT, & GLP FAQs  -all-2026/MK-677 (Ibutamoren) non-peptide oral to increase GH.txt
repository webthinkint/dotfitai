Ibutamoren (MK-677) Oral non-peptide* GH stimulant
Primary use: to increase the body’s natural growth hormone and IGF-1 levels to support muscle growth, recovery, and overall anabolic function without injections
Mechanism: mimics ghrelin (“hunger hormone”) to activate ghrelin receptors (GHS-R1a), which signals the hypothalamus & pituitary to increase natural GH pulse release; liver then produces more IGF-1 leading to an increase in protein synthesis and reduction in muscle breakdown
Typical dosing used in research and practice is ~10–25 mg once daily, usually taken in the evening (or split), with many starting at ~10 mg to assess tolerance and adjusting based on response

Notes: MK-677 is an oral compound that increases your body’s growth hormone by mimicking a hunger signal. That can help with recovery, muscle maintenance, and sleep—but it also makes you hungrier and can affect blood sugar, so it’s not a shortcut unless everything else is dialed in and human results vary and largely based on anecdotes. MK-677 is not FDA Approved and classified as a research compound.
  *1 Ibutamoren (MK-677) is a small-molecule, non-peptide compound, specifically an orally active ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a) agonist. It’s a synthetic, drug-like molecule (not a chain of amino acids like peptides designed to bind the ghrelin receptor and stimulate GH release. Its small-molecule structure is what allows it to be taken orally and remain stable in the body. Therefore MK-677 works like a drug, not a peptide—so you can take it orally and it still activates the GH pathway effectively

🧠 Mechanism of Action (Simple + Trainer-Friendly)
🔹 Core Action
MK-677 is a ghrelin receptor agonist
👉 It mimics the hormone ghrelin (“hunger hormone”)

⚙️ Step-by-Step Mechanism
Activates ghrelin receptors (GHS-R1a)
Signals the hypothalamus & pituitary
Increases natural GH pulse release
Liver produces more IGF-1
Leads to:
Increased protein synthesis
Reduced muscle breakdown
Enhanced recovery

🔁 Key Difference vs Peptides
| MK-677 | Sermorelin / GHRPs |
| Oral (pill) | Injection |
| Continuous GH elevation | Pulsatile GH release |
| Strong appetite increase | Minimal appetite effect |
| Longer half-life (~24h) | Short acting |
