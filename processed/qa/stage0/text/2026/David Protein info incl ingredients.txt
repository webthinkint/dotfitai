Ingredients That Have Generated Discussion
The two ingredients most frequently discussed by nutrition professionals and in the recent lawsuit are:
EPG (esterified propoxylated glycerol) – a modified fat replacement that provides texture and mouthfeel with substantially fewer metabolizable calories than conventional fat. 
Maltitol – a sugar alcohol used to help achieve the 0 g sugar claim. Some individuals experience gastrointestinal distress when consuming larger amounts. 
Trainer/Nutrition Professional Takeaway
From a formulation standpoint, David is essentially a highly engineered protein-delivery system designed to maximize protein density (28 g protein for 150 calories) using isolated dairy proteins, collagen peptides, EPG, fiber, and sugar alcohols. It is not a whole-food style bar like an RXBAR. The primary advantage is its exceptionally high protein-to-calorie ratio; the tradeoff is greater reliance on specialty ingredients and sweeteners
