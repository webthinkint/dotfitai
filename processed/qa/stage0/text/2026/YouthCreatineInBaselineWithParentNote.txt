Youth/Athlete Growth, Development, Performance, Recovery & Health Dietary Support Plan
Keep in mind all programs we create not only reduce the risk of injury, speed recovery, improve training/exercise induced results but also contribute to better overall health while maximizing growth (including height) and development potential. Proper nutrition makes our children and athletes better “on the field” and in the classroom.
The FirstString Protein mix/shake is a 3rd party tested and lactose free (as all our products) food product that we have the youth athletes (any active youth) we work with use for a pre and post activity shake for energy and recovery, and/or weight/muscle gain. Below is more information including incorporating FirstString into daily planning along with proper micronutrient supplementation to also aid recovery and fill important nutrient gaps common to youngsters. CreatineMonohydrate Loading & Maintenance* Strategy 
5-day Loading: Mix 1-scoop with 4-8oz of favorite fluid/shake and take 4 times daily with a protein and/or carbohydrate containing meal/drink for first 5 days. Spread doses as evenly as possible
Maintenance: <175LBS take 1-scoop daily; >175LBS take 2scoops daily
Training days: 1scoop after workout with meals/shake. May mix with your post training formula; >175LBS take 1scoop before and 1 after workout with meal/shake
Non-training days take recommended dose(s) anytime with a meal (if using 2 scoops/ take 1 with AM and 1 with PM meal)
