Other than for clinical reasons, no one needs this information. Therefore, if the product is being used for a clinical application, then the clinicians may need to know the makeup of the compounds found in a specific whey concentrate (or an isolate), because of a particular dairy allergy in the treatment group. As an example, B-Lactoglobulin is one of the primary proteins found in bovine whey and some people may be allergic.
