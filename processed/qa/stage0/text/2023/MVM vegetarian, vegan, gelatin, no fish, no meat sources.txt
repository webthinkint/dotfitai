Neither the ActiveMV, nor the WomensMV (nor any other multivitamin that we offer) contain gelatin or fish/meat ingredients.  The products are all considered vegetarian but not vegan due to the vitamin D-3 that is used is derived from lanolin (sheep’s wool exposed to UV light).  Since this is a “biproduct” of an animal but not the actual animal, it is considered vegetarian but not vegan.  

We also offer a fully vegan multivitamin called VeganMV (https://www.dotfit.com/VeganMV) which uses a vegan sourced vitamin D-2 rather than vitamin D-3, if that is of interest to you.
