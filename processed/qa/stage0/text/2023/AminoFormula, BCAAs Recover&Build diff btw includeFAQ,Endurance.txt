Q: I've been using AminoFormula to help with adding muscle; would "Recover and Build" do the same thing?
A: Yes, Recover & Build (branched-chain amino acids [BCAA]) work for recovery but not as complete AminoFormula, (AF) since AF has the BCAAs and the other 5 amino acids that trigger muscle protein synthesis (MPS). However, endurance athletes use the Recover & Build (BCAA only) exclusively to delay fatigue and also help recover. See below for more information.

BCAA supplementation during strenuous endurance-type activities may help reduce fatigue and improve multiple choice reaction times (MCRT) through its ability to contribute as an energy source during prolonged training, potential to block serotonin production and reduce lactate levels.
Note
The dotFIT Recover&Build (RB) is a tablet form of isolated BCAAs allowing the user a convenient, low calorie and economical alternative to the dotFIT AminoFormula (AF), which also contains a very high dose of BCAAs (6 g in one 12 g serving). While there may be clinical or other potential conditions where BCAAs supplementation alone may be warranted, the dotFIT R&D team positions the use of Recover&Build (RB) only for the goals described above, which are similar to the actions of AF supplementation for recovery. In other words, if you are using AF for recovery, we have no rationale to recommend RB other than in relatively high doses for the primary goal of delaying fatigue during strenuous endurance activities or clinical reasons as recommended by a qualified practitioner.
