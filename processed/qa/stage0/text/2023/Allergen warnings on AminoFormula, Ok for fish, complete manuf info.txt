Derek, I received the answers that were needed. 


The Lemonade and BlueRaspberry AminoFormula are produced in a facility that is free of shellfish (in fact, they are free of fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts and wheat).  This is a recent facility change that incorporated both the Lemonade and BlueRaspberry AminoFormula but was done after our labels were changed and that is why the allergen warning is stated as such.  New label printings going forward will contain an allergen warning of:  “Contains:  Milk, Soy (from soy lecithin) and Egg.”

So, those 2 flavors of AminoFormula would be safe for you to use.



For the Mixed Berry and Tropical Colada (Vegan) AminoFormula products, we used a different manufacturer for those and this is what the manufacturer provided as to information about this topic:

“We have a HAACP Plan that involves stringent cleaning to keep cross contamination from happening between products.  As there isn’t a shellfish allergen present in the actual raw materials comprising the AminoFormula, there shouldn’t be any concern about him having an allergic reaction to the product from shellfish.”

With these 2 flavors, I will leave that up to you if you want to take their advice to use those products but at least you know you can use the Lemonade and BlueRaspberry flavors for sure.

All AminoFormula products going forward will be produced at the first facility that is free of fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts and wheat.
