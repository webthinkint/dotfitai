Sucralose
Sucralose is also a NNS, and is made from sucrose by a process that substitutes three chloride atoms for three hydroxyl groups on the sucrose molecule., Sucralose is a very versatile NNS that is 450–650 times sweeter than sucrose, possesses a pleasant sweet taste and a quality and time intensity profile that is close to that of sucrose making a popular NNS.414, Sucralose has been extensively studied with more than 110 safety studies reviewed by the FDA in approving the use of sucralose as a general purpose sweetener for food.427,428,446, A primary advantage of sucralose for consumers is its exceptional stability. It retains its sweetness over a wide range of temperature and storage conditions and in solutions over time. This stability allows manufactures to create greater tasting foods and beverages and maintain the fresh flavor. Like Ace-K, sucralose is heat stable, meaning that it stays sweet even when used at high temperatures during baking, making it a common sugar substitute in baked goods.448,
