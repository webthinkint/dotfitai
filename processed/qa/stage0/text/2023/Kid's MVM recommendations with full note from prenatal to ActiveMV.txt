Kid’s MVM Supplementation
Dietary support to maximize your child’s structural and functional growth and development  - carry on from the prenatal
From the womb thru breastfeeding continue your prenatal MVM and Omega-3
Post breastfeeding till teeth (generally age 2) incorporate vitamin and mineral fortified baby formulas/foods
Ages 2-4yrs take 1 dotFIT COMPLETE Kid’s MVM (includes high D, C and now choline) and 1-omega if not feeding your child fish at least 2Xs/wk
Ages 5-11yrs take 2 Kid’s, proper protein amounts and add Omega-3 and calcium as needed
Ages 12-17yrs take 1-ActiveMV, proper protein amounts and add Omega-3 and calcium as needed
