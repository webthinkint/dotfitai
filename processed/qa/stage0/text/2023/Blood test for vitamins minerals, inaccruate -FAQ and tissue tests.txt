Blood tests for levels of vitamins and minerals (VM) do not accurately assess insufficiency/deficiencies because it only measures the vitamin or mineral in the blood, not in the tissues where they are part of their respective enzyme systems.
Everyone falls short of the recommended (RDAs of VM) as every study shows from diet alone, therefore take a Multivitamin and Mineral Formula (MVM), regardless of what your blood test shows. Further, no blood test can test for all VMs in one or two tests. It would take about 20 different test and still only tell you blood levels, not functional tissue/cellular activity. See below for more information.

More info
Blood test for minerals may be flawed (plasma, serum, etc.) because those levels may not be the same in tissues especially during systemic inflammation response (measured by levels of c-reactive protein), which significantly lowers plasma levels but not a true deficiency*. Also, blood plasma levels are not signs of tissue saturation especially Vit-C.
Measuring vitamin status with tissues samples or blood can be problematic because of vitamin instability (e.g., light oxygen, heat, etc.) – why you don’t do it – not reliable*. 291 for ref). Also circulating blood levels only tell you what’s there, not in storage
Blood mineral status is not representative of tissue or cell status. Serum and plasma differ in content. (Page 313)* 

FAQ
Q: To the best of your knowledge, are there any ways to test for micronutrient deficiencies other than blood tests? 
Thank you!
A: Blood test don’t do a good job. They only tell you what’s in the blood (blood mineral status is not representative of tissue or cell status. Serum and plasma differ in content). You need to get related enzyme activity tested, which is impractical for all VMs, but not if you are looking for a specific deficiency or insufficiency such as iron, vitamin K or D, etc. All this said, diet analysis (recall) will at least give you what is being ingested but tissue measurement activity is the final call if you suspect an deficiency.
