A pre-workout supplement is usually a multi-ingredient blend that includes stimulants such as NO7Rage, and used by more serious/experienced exercisers/athletes. A protein powder is just that, protein with generally some carbohydrates (CHO) and fats. A protein powder, depending on the other macronutrient contents (fats and CHOs) can be used as a meal replacement and/or a pre-workout protein and CHO supplement, or simply to economically supplement your daily protein needs of 1gm/LB of lean body mass (LBM) daily. As of note, we have the Pre & Post Workout Formula and Meal Replacement (PPWMR), which is commonly used for both a pre-workout protein and CHO shake, and as a meal replacement (hence the name). You can go to the PPWMR  recipe section to get many ways of using the formula to make foods/shakes.
