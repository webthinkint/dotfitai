Q: Is your Over50MV NSF-CS?
A: Thanks for contacting us. All our products, including the Over50 Multivitamin and Mineral Formula (MVM), are 3rd party tested (click here for all facilities and testing) and all are manufactured in NSF certified facilities. The majority are also NSF-Certified for Sport (NSF-CS) because they are the ones that require that additional specialized certification, such as the college, pro and Olympic teams we support. See below for more information but as an example, the Over50 is not sold into our specialized channel requiring the extra NSF-CS (4th party testing if you will) certification and this also helps keep the cost down. The ActiveMV is the only MVM our team purchase because it is made especially for this population.

More info
All our products are 3rd party tested after completion, and most also go through the NSF Certified for Sport (NSF-CS) process (4th party test if you will), which is a significant extra cost per lot (and a test that goes beyond the standard NSF certification testing). Therefore, products that are not in high demand, such as some of the MVM and fat loss products including LeanMR, or Weight Loss & Liver Support (WLLS), by our pro and college teams and other organization that require NSF-CS (drug tested athletes), we don’t send it through the additional NSF-CS process so we can keep these products affordable for everyone. Again, All products are 3rd party tested, manufactured in NSF certified facilities, and go through the same manufacturing rigor and processes, so the only reason for an extra NSF-CS (4th party if you will) is for the customers that require it.
