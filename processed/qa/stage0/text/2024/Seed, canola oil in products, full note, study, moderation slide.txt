The miniscule amount of canola or other seed oils that a few of our product’s use is not just good for the product production but also can be part of a healthy diet.
Like anything else in your diet, seed oils are fine in moderation (see true science slide below). They should be part of an overall balanced eating plan that includes lots of vegetables, fruits, wholegrains, and lean protein options.
Of course, you can get too much of a good thing. The overuse of seed oils is the most problematic when they are an ingredient in ultra-processed foods. For example, if you eat a lot of deep-fried foods, pastries, crunchy fried snacks (such as chips) and donuts, you can get too much oil. These ultra-processed foods are also high in sugar, salt, and/or food additives, which are also partly to blame for the poor health outcomes.
