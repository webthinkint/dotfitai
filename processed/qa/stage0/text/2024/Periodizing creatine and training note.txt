Yes, just like competitive athletes periodize their training, you want to taper down, rest and restart. Therefore, she can stay on all her health supplements and protein as needed and go off the Creatine. She can still train but should reduce the intensity to what we like to call “active rest”. Meaning, you can still exercise but don’t go to failure on reps and lighten the load and maybe take a week of resistance training off completely. After a week or so of that, start back slowly to where after 3 weeks of building training intensity, you can restart the creatine and get into full  training mode again.
