While magnesium insufficiency may be a contributor to high blood pressure (BP), magnesium supplementation would not be a treatment for high BP unless your attending physician prescribed it, in which case, something was identified as a deficiency that also may be an inability to absorb magnesium from foods. However, make sure you get the proper magnesium intake from food and supplements (RDA: 320-420 mg) Upper limit [UL]: 350 mg**) as necessary, such as from your Multivitamin and Mineral Formula (MVM) and any other supplements containing magnesium (e.g., most calcium supplements contain magnesium like our SuperCalcium that has 125mg/tab). The dotFIT Women’s MV has 100mgs; Active MV 200mg, Over50 150mg.
** Upper limit refers to supplements intake only (don’t have to add your food magnesium content to the total), therefore, you should not take more than 350mg/day from supplements without a doctor’s approval.
Basically, what we are suggesting is to make sure your magnesium (and all other vitamin and mineral daily intake) intake is correct, and if your BP remains high, ask your doctor.
