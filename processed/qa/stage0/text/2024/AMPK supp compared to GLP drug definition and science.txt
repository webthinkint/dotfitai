AMP-Activated Protein Kinase (AMPK)
AMP-activated protein kinase plays a role in cellular energy homeostasis, mostly to activate glucose & fatty acid uptake and oxidation when cellular energy is low. Net effect of AMPK activation is stimulation of hepatic fatty acid oxidation, ketogenesis, stimulation of skeletal muscle fatty acid oxidation and glucose uptake, inhibition of cholesterol synthesis, lipogenesis,  and triglyceride synthesis, inhibition of adipocyte lipogenesis, inhibition of adipocyte lipolysis, and modulation of insulin secretion by pancreatic β-cells.
