Thanks for contacting us. We are not sure what you are asking here, other than there is no evidence that chromium supplementation at a proper daily amount* is toxic anywhere in the body. If you are referring to higher doses (we can’t tell a dose you are referring to from what you wrote), the answer is that any metal/mineral can be toxic at high levels - i.e., daily ingestion is regularly above the Tolerable Upper Limit Levels (UL) and chromium would be no exception. That said chromium up to 1000 mcg daily is likely safe for short-term use, and doses of 200-1000 mcg daily have been used safely for up to 2 years. And there is no established Tolerable Upper Limit Levels for chromium at this time.
