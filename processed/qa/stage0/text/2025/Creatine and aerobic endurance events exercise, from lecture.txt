It is not uncommon for endurance athletes to add CreatineMonohydrate (CM) to their supplement regime to help with recovery including greater glycogen restoration (see below from our creatine lectures. Add CM to the supps you are using to give a try to see if it improves your training numbers.

Creatine mechanisms of action to potentially effect aerobic activity
Enhance Event Performance
CS changes energy substrate utilization, at least during the early phase of aerobic activity that may help increase time to exhaustion (15,32,62,80,81,84)
CS can attenuate cardiovascular & thermoregulatory responses during prolonged exercise in heat (improves how the body handles heat) (82,83)
Acute decreases in strength following aerobic exercise may be offset by CS -may be of use to cross-training athletes (85)
Enhance Recovery to Potentially Benefit Ongoing Events
CS augments muscle glycogen stores post exercise to potentially enhance the next bout of endurance activities (15,87-89)
Magnitude of glycogen re-synthesis during the first 24 hours of supplementation was ~82% greater in the CS groups 
CS may reduce muscle glycogen and protein breakdown in aerobic and anaerobic activity (91)
CS can decrease p53 levels & increase in IGF-1 levels, inferring a protective effect on apoptosis (promotes cell survival), suggesting a recovery benefit for endurance athletes (93)
Takeaway: better energy usage and overall recovery including glycogen restoration
