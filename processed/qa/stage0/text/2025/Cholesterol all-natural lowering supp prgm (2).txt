SUPPLEMENT PLAN (Lowering Cholesterol, ApoB, & Triglycerides)
Goals
Lower LDL cholesterol
Reduce ApoB (the number of cholesterol-carrying particles)
Bring triglycerides down toward the healthy range
Support heart, liver, and metabolic health
Core supplements (Take Daily) beyond the Alln1 SuperBlend™ 
Most effective, safe, and well-researched supplements for improving your numbers.
1. Omega-3 Fish Oil (EPA + DHA) dotFIT
 Dose: 2,000–3,000 mg per day (split into two doses 2caps in AM and 2 PM) 
Benefits: Lowers triglycerides; Supports heart health; Reduces inflammation
2. Red Yeast Rice (NOW Foods Red Yeast Rice 1200 mg) (citrinin-free, third-party tested)
Dose: 600 mg twice daily OR 1,200 mg once daily with dinner (4-5mg of monacolin K in 1200mg)
Benefits: Lowers LDL cholesterol; Lowers ApoB; Helps maintain healthy cholesterol production
3. Berberine (BIO-Vitals Berberine)
 Dose: take 2 capsules once daily with a meal
Benefits: Helps lower LDL and triglycerides; Supports healthy blood sugar and liver function; Reduces liver production of cholesterol particles
4. Psyllium Fiber (Soluble Fiber) (Metamucil Premium Brand)
 Dose: 1 tablespoon in water before 1–2 meals per day
Benefits: Lowers LDL cholesterol; Helps the body remove cholesterol naturally; Supports digestion

 Morning: Fish oil (2caps); Berberine; Psyllium
