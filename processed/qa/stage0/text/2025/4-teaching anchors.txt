4-Teaching anchors
First law of thermodynamics: eliminates any basic alternate reasons for a plateau, specifically spontaneous energy storage, while answering all questions why someone loses body fat no matter what method (other than surgery) this constitutes the bottom line in the energy change in ay system and ties to all other anchors.
Societies increasing fat storage is due to lack of energy expenditure compared to energy intake (eat too much and move to little). This severely restricts the macronutrient-related arguments as well as fad diets being a general solution.
We are not prisoners of our genes. People do lose weight and keep it off. There is probably a set point range for most individuals that may set by genetics. Where you end up on the range is determined by lifestyle (physical activity, eating habits, etc.,). These genes control appetite, satiety, basal and exercise metabolism, and the point at which excess energy intake is converted to lean tissue or bodyfat. Ultimately we can control energy in and out. In the final analysis it eliminates the genetic argument while still admitting that there is an increased difficulty for some because of genetic inheritance.
Fat loss is not limited like hypertrophy. This eliminates the argument that one cannot lose anymore weight/fat. We can always alter mass/composition by manipulating energy intake and expenditure (it is a matter of what one is willing or must do to get there). Additionally, this fact eliminates the “point of diminishing returns” excuse for a plateau.
