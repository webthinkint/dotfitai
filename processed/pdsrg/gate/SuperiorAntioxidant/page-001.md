Rationale

supplied through diet.9,10 Carotenoids (e.g., lycopene, zeaxanthin, lutein, astaxanthin, etc.) are health promoting bio-
actives which are synthesized in plants/algae, bacteria, yeast and fungi, and are acquired by humans by consuming
these organisms as foods.11,12
