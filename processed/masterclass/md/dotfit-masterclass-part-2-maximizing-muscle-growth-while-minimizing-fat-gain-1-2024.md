## MUSCLE GAINZ MACRONUTRIENT REQUIREMENTS (p. 17)

Protein 25-35% (greater if fat loss is part of goal) • Tissue repair & building • Generally, 1gm/LB of LBM or body weight if not overweight (Ex 175lb=150-175g) • Divided 4-6Xs/d including pre/post workout •Min/meal (muscle full effect): ~.2g/lb/LBM (175LBM=35g) (.18/lb/BM) Ref Carbohydrate (CHO) 40/50-70% (less if fat loss is part of goal) • Fills the primary energy system that needs constant refueling for maximum performance –some within every meal • Generally, ≥2:1 CHO:P ratio unless prolonged caloric restriction • The greater/longer training need, the higher the CHO requirement •~1.4-2.3 g/lb of body weight – more personal • Fats: 20-35% (primarily unsaturated or naturally in Proteins) • Generally, remaining calories after CHO & protein needs are met
