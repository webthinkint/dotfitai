LEVEL 1: ESSENTIALS (Foundation for Brain Structure, Mood & Stress Resilience)
What are the evidence-based formulas
Essentials include a high-quality multivitamin/mineral (or All-in-one SuperBlend), often extra Vitamin D (beyond the MVM), and Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA)—the core nutrients most people are insufficient/deficient in, but required daily for proper brain structure and function.
What do they do
These nutrients provide the biochemical foundation of the brain—supporting neurotransmitter production, brain cell structure, inflammation control, and protection against oxidative stress. Together, they help improve mood stability, stress response, cognitive function, and long-term brain health, especially when correcting common nutrient gaps.
Who would use them
Everyone. Designed for all individuals—especially those with inconsistent diet quality, high stress, high training volume, poor sleep, or aging-related nutrient decline. It is the baseline for both general health and mental performance.
How to use them
Daily MVM or SuperBlend with meals; extra Vitamin D as necessary to achieve ~40–70 ng/mL blood levels
Omega-3s: ~1,000 mg/day for general health or 1,500–3,000 mg/day EPA/DHA for brain, mood, and performance support.Consistency is key—this level works through daily correction of deficiencies/insufficiencies.

LEVEL 2: NEXT-LEVEL (Targeted Brain Energy, Aging & Mood Support)
What are the products
This level adds Creatine Monohydrate and targeted brain nutrients such as phosphatidylserine, acetyl-L-carnitine, alpha-lipoic acid, and vitamin B12—nutrients that specifically support brain energy, aging, and neurological function.
What do they do
These compounds go beyond baseline nutrition to enhance brain energy (ATP production), protect neurons, support memory and focus, and help regulate mood under stress or aging conditions. Creatine supports brain energy during stress (sleep loss, fatigue, concussion), while the other nutrients help maintain youthful brain function and protect against cognitive decline.
Who would use them
Best suited for individuals who are:
Experiencing high stress, mental fatigue, or mood challenges
Athletes or high performers needing cognitive resilience
Those wanting preventative brain-aging support before symptoms begin
45+ (aging-related decline in brain nutrients)
How to use them
Creatine: ~5 g/day (higher doses may be used in specific conditions)
Brain Health nutrients: taken daily (e.g., 4 tablets for 45–55, 8 for 56+)
Used in addition to—not instead of—the EssentialsThis level is about targeted support layered on top of a strong foundation.
