How to use it?
Take NO7 PREWORKOUT ~10–30 minutes before training or competition. Dosage is body-weight based:
Under 150 lbs: 1.5 scoops
150–200 lbs: 2 scoops
Over 200 lbs: 2.5 scoops (do not exceed 3 scoops daily)
On non-training days, take your total daily serving at any time to help maintain creatine and beta-alanine levels. If sensitive to caffeine or beta-alanine tingling, divide the serving into half-scoops spaced 90 minutes apart, ensuring the full dose is consumed before or during training
