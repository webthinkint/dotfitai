"ThermAccel is made to do three main things:
Boost your daily calorie burn,
Increase fat burning, and
Help control your appetite.

"First up is caffeine, a well-known stimulant that increases your metabolism. It activates your nervous system, helping you burn more calories—even at rest. 3. EGCG – The Green Tea Extract Advantage
"Next is EGCG, a powerful antioxidant found in green tea. It works differently than caffeine—it blocks the breakdown of fat-burning hormones like norepinephrine and activates AMPK, your body’s internal fat-burning switch. Together with caffeine, EGCG boosts calorie burning and fat loss. It also helps regulate blood sugar and may even improve gut health."

4. Capsaicin – Turning On Brown Fat
"ThermAccel also contains capsaicin, the compound that gives chili peppers their heat. It activates a type of fat in your body called brown fat. Brown fat burns calories to produce heat, and capsaicin turns it on. It also helps reduce appetite, making it easier to stick to your nutrition plan."
5. Sinetrol – Plant-Based Fat Loss Support
"Another key ingredient is Sinetrol, a non-stimulant blend of citrus polyphenols. It increases the breakdown of fat by keeping fat-burning enzymes active and enhances energy partitioning—meaning your body uses more fat for energy instead of storing it. Clinical studies show it helps reduce waist and abdominal fat specifically."
6. Caralluma Fimbriata – Appetite Control
"One unique ingredient is Caralluma Fimbriata, a plant used traditionally to suppress hunger. It works on your brain’s appetite centers and also helps regulate hormones like ghrelin and neuropeptide-Y, which are linked to hunger. It gives extra support on those tougher days when cravings hit."
7. L-Theanine – Smooth Energy Without the Jitters
"To balance out the stimulant effect of caffeine, ThermAccel includes L-Theanine, the calming amino acid found in green tea. It reduces mental stress, sharpens focus, and helps prevent the overstimulation some people feel with caffeine alone. The result? You stay energized, focused, and calm."

"ThermAccel is a great fit for:✔ People who are already dieting and exercising but need an extra push✔ Those struggling with plateaus or low energy✔ Clients needing help controlling appetite during a calorie deficit
