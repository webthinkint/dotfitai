## Slide 162

Part 1 & 2 Leading to Part 3
Part 1 – the start of your personal “Health Cabinet“ – establishes the non-negotiable foundation for lifelong health, performance, and aging well by correcting “silent hunger” through complete daily nutrition (MVM or Alln1 SuperBlend™, adequate protein, omega-3s; add extra vitamin D and calcium as needed), which powers metabolism and enables the other two cornerstones—activity and body composition—to function properly;
Part 2 – expands your “Health Cabinet” – builds on this foundation by optimizing recovery, performance, and resilience across each decade of life through targeted additions (antioxidants, probiotics, collagen, brain health, creatine, amino acids, glutamine, and other goal-based supports), creating a comprehensive “Health & Performance Cabinet” that enhances daily function, delays age-related decline, and extends Playspan® to better match lifespan
Together, Parts 1 and 2 maximize natural physiology first, which sets the stage for Part 3—where advanced interventions like HRT, peptides, including GLP-1 therapies are only considered if truly needed, based on a well-nourished, optimized system and a clear risk-versus-benefit evaluation.
Takeaway:
Part 1 builds the body.Part 2 upgrades how it performs.Part 3 decides if anything else is even necessary.
