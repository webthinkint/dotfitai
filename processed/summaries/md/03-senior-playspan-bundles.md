WHAT ARE THE PRODUCTS?
The Senior Health, Performance & Longevity Bundles are three simplified supplement stacks that allow adults—especially those 50+—to choose the level of nutritional support that best fits their budget, lifestyle and goals. The foundation is built around filling nutritional gaps with Over50MV or Alln1 SuperBlend™, adequate high-quality protein, omega-3s, and calcium/vitamin D when needed. More comprehensive levels add targeted products such as CreatineMonohydrate, AminoFormula to further support muscle, recovery, joints, cognition and continued physical performance. The concept is progressive rather than all-or-nothing: start with the essential nutritional foundation and add additional support as needs, activity and budget increase.
WHAT DO THEY DO?
Together, the bundles are designed to address two realities of aging: nutritional needs remain critically important while the body's ability to maintain muscle, strength, recovery and performance becomes increasingly challenged. The foundational products help close everyday nutrient gaps from diet and aging to provide the protein and essential nutrients required for normal structure and function. The more advanced products then help support preservation of muscle and strength, exercise performance and recovery, bone and joint health, brain function and overall resilience. You can progress by moving from “Foundational—Essential for structural & functional integrity,” to “Optimizing Daily Performance,” and finally “Beyond Protein to Recover & Build better/faster/longer.”

WHO WOULD USE EACH BUNDLE?
Bundle 1 — Foundation / Essentials: Best for the person who wants the simplest, most affordable starting point for healthy aging. This includes people over 40 who may or may not exercise regularly, but particularly adults 50+ who want to protect their nutritional foundation, muscle, bones and general health without taking a large number of products.
Bundle 2 — Health + Performance: Best for the active older adult who wants everything in the foundation but is also concerned about maintaining muscle, strength, energy, exercise capacity and recovery. This is where creatine and/or AminoFormula become particularly valuable additions as age-related resistance to protein and exercise becomes more important. This bundle is especially important for older exercisers seeking to maintain performance and muscle.
Bundle 3 — Longevity / Lifelong Performance: Best for the person who says, “I want to do everything reasonably possible to stay strong, sharp, mobile and active as I age.” It builds upon the first two levels with more targeted support for joints, connective tissue, brain health and other age- or goal-specific needs. It is particularly appropriate for highly active seniors, lifelong exercisers and people whose priority is not simply lifespan, but preserving independence and physical and cognitive performance for as long as possible. After 60, priorities increasingly shift toward general health, longevity/independence, strength and fall prevention – this bundle is your best natural solution

HOW TO USE THEM
The goal is to make the program easy enough to become a lifelong habit. Take the foundational multivitamin/mineral or Alln1 SuperBlend™ daily; use protein as necessary to achieve the day's protein target; use SuperOmega-3 according to fatty-fish intake; and add Calcium Complex and vitamin D according to dietary intake and individual need. At the performance level, CreatineMonohydrate is generally used daily, with the presentation using approximately 5 g/day as the standard maintenance amount, while AminoFormula can be used around exercise or to fortify lower-protein meals. The protein strategy simple: use food plus supplemental protein as necessary to reach the individual's daily target, distributed throughout the day.
TO ALL POTENTIAL USERS
“The goal isn't simply to live longer—it's to stay strong, sharp, mobile and independent while you do it. As we get older, good nutrition and exercise become more important, not less. These bundles make it simple by giving you the nutritional foundation your body needs every day and then allowing you to add support for muscle, strength, recovery, joints and brain health based on your goals and budget. You don't have to take everything. Start with the essentials and build from there. Think of it as investing a little every day in the body you want to be living in 10, 20, 30 or 40, etc., years from now.”
The “Health Cabinet” rather than reactive “Medicine Cabinet” approach and its emphasis on maximizing natural physiology through nutrition and activity first, defines the factual statement that “an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure”
