## Slide 249

Top Youth Nutrition Takeaways (Parents & Trainers Must Know)
Kids only get one growth window. You cannot “go back later” and fix missed nutrition during childhood and adolescence — including height, bone density, brain development, and metabolic programming.
Most youth do NOT meet daily nutrient needs through food alone. Picky eating, busy schedules, and limited fruits/vegetables lead to vitamin, mineral, and Omega-3 insufficiencies that impact behavior, cognition, immunity, and development.
Protein is the #1 growth macronutrient. Children and youth athletes should target ~1 g of protein per pound of body weight per day, spaced across all feedings, especially around training.
Proper daily calories are essential. To maximize growth and athletic development, kids need calories above maintenance, with roughly 2:1 carbs-to-protein, fueling muscle, energy, and training recovery.
  Try to meet the 5 servings of fruits and veggies/day – use a protein smoothie as necessary
Meal timing matters. Youth should eat at least 3 times/day and especially fuel pre- and post-training with protein + carbohydrates for growth, recovery, and performance.
