## Slide 96

dotFIT Collagen Complex Summary
Mobility + Beauty Support in One Formula

What is the Product
Collagen Complex is a clinically tested supplement that combines hydrolyzed type-II collagen peptides, hyaluronic acid, and chondroitin sulfate in a unique, patented matrix that mimics the natural composition of human joint cartilage and skin extracellular matrix. This low molecular weight form (BioCell Collagen®) ensures high absorption and bioavailability, delivering targeted building blocks directly to connective tissues such as joints, ligaments, tendons, cartilage, and skin
What Does It Do
Collagen Complex supports the structure, function, and longevity of joints and skin by restoring collagen and glycosaminoglycan (HA and CS) levels that naturally decline with age and activity. Clinical trials show it improves joint comfort, mobility, and recovery from stress or injury while also enhancing skin elasticity, hydration, and reducing wrinkles and fine lines. It protects against age-related collagen loss, photoaging, and connective tissue breakdown—making it a “two-in-one” product for both joint health and youthful skin.
Who Would Use It
This product is ideal for:
Adults over 35 looking to counter age-related joint stiffness and skin aging.
Athletes or active individuals who place heavy stress on connective tissues and need faster recovery.
People with overuse-related joint discomfort (e.g., runners, lifters, or physically demanding jobs).
Those seeking anti-aging skin support, including improved hydration, elasticity, and reduced wrinkles.
Anyone wanting a safe, non-drug alternative to support joint comfort and skin vitality
