Your family's multivitamin and mineral formula (MVM) solutions

The dotFIT Multivitamin & Mineral (MVM) formulas were designed to complement—not replace—a healthy diet by filling the well-documented vitamin and mineral gaps that commonly remain even in health-conscious individuals.
Unlike one-size-fits-all retail products, dotFIT formulas are professionally integrated into a complete health, fitness, and nutrition program and are recommended based primarily on age, body size, activity level, and specific life-stage needs.
Each formula is designed to work synergistically with other dotFIT supplements, helping users achieve recommended nutrient intakes while remaining within established safe and effective ranges.
The formulations utilize evidence-based nutrient levels, highly functional vitamin and mineral forms, controlled-release delivery technology, and rigorous third-party testing for purity and label accuracy.
Rather than competing on price, flavor, or marketing claims, dotFIT focuses on providing comprehensive, science-based foundational nutrition support designed to help individuals achieve and maintain optimal nutrient status throughout life.
For those seeking a completely plant-based option, the Alln1 SuperBlend™ provides a vegan-friendly nutritional foundation built on the same philosophy of filling dietary gaps and supporting long-term health.
