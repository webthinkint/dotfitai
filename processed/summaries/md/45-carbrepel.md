## Slide 183

CarbRepel Product Summary
CarbRepel™ — Enjoy the Carbs. Block the Calories

What Is the Product?
CarbRepel™ is a non-stimulant carbohydrate-blocking formula built around Phase 2®, a clinically studied white-bean (Phaseolus vulgaris) extract shown to inhibit alpha-amylase—the enzyme responsible for breaking down dietary starches for absorption. By partially blocking carbohydrate digestion, the product reduces the number of carbohydrate calories that enter the body. Additional natural cofactors such as citrus pectin and pomegranate leaf extract serve as synergistic supporting natural delivery agents. CarbRepel™ is designed to be used within a structured diet and exercise plan to support body-fat reduction and weight-maintenance goals.
What Does It Do?
CarbRepel™ reduces the absorption of carbohydrate calories, allowing users to consume a normal or slightly higher-carbohydrate diet while still maintaining or accelerating fat loss. Research on Phase 2® demonstrates meaningful reductions in body weight, body fat, waist measurements, and appetite-related hunger signals. It also helps blunt post-meal glycemic responses, which can reduce cravings tied to rapid blood-sugar fluctuations. By lowering available carbohydrate calories without affecting the central nervous system, CarbRepel™ helps individuals manage appetite, maintain lean body mass during weight loss, and avoid the typical “carb-craving cycle.”

Who Would Use It?
CarbRepel™ is ideal for individuals who tend to crave or overconsume carbohydrates or sweets, those seeking a non-stimulant fat-loss aid, and anyone wanting to enhance the results of a weight-reduction plan without relying on appetite-suppressing drugs. It is also valuable for people who experience episodic high-carbohydrate meals and want help keeping calorie intake under control. Users following the dotFIT 90-Day Weight Loss Solution (LeanPak90) may incorporate CarbRepel™ to strengthen progress, while individuals with difficulty maintaining weight loss can use it during higher-intake “risk periods.”
How to Use It
The standard protocol is to take two tablets, twice daily, 30 minutes before the largest carbohydrate-containing meals or snacks, with at least 8 oz of fluid. CarbRepel™ may be discontinued once weight-loss goals are reached or when lifestyle habits are sufficient to maintain progress. It is generally well-tolerated, though individuals using diabetes medications should monitor blood glucose closely, and use is not recommended during pregnancy or lactation due to insufficient data.
