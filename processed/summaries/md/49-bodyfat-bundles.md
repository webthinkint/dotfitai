## Slide 200

Bodyfat/Weight Loss Bundles that Protect lean body mass (LBM)

Whether your goal is a steady transformation or rapid fat loss, dotFIT’s 3-Level Body Fat Loss Bundles give you the right tools for your goal, budget, and experience level—built on essentials that support your metabolism, protect lean mass, and help you lose body fat the smart way. Start with the foundation, level up as needed, and watch your results accelerate.

What Are the Products
The dotFIT Body Fat Loss Supplement Bundles are structured into three levels, designed to match a user’s goal intensity, experience, and budget. Each bundle layers evidence-based products to support targeted fat loss while preserving lean body mass (LBM). At the core of every bundle are the foundation essentials:
Active Multivitamin & Mineral (MVM) — a 4-in-1 NSF-Certified formula providing full nutritional coverage and metabolic support.
LeanMeal — high-protein and fiber meal replacement that improves satiety, calorie control, and helps “feed muscle while starving bodyfat.”
From there, higher bundle levels integrate specialized support like Weight Loss & Liver Support (WLLS- liver health/appetite control), ThermAccel or CarbRepel (metabolic boosters or carbohydrate absorption inhibitors), and AminoFormula (EAA blend for advanced muscle preservation and recovery) to accelerate fat loss and prevent plateaus
What They Do
These bundles work synergistically to optimize fat loss physiology without compromising lean mass. The MVM ensures that all cellular “spark plugs” are firing, maximizing energy metabolism and recovery. LeanMeal integrates seamlessly into daily meal planning to control hunger, provide precise calorie intake, and support higher protein distribution—key for preserving LBM during weight loss. Add-on products like WLLS, ThermAccel or CarbRepel further increase daily calorie burn, enhance fat oxidation, improve liver health, reduce cravings, or blunt carb absorption, helping users break through adaptive plateaus that typically slow or stop fat loss. Finally, AminoFormula supports muscle building, energy, and recovery, giving users the ability to change their body composition more efficiently

## Slide 201

Bodyfat/Weight Loss Bundles that Protect lean body mass (LBM)

Who Would Use Each Bundle
Level 1 (Essentials): Ideal for beginners or budget-conscious individuals who want to start losing fat safely and healthfully while protecting lean mass. Focuses on MVM + LeanMR to create the nutritional foundation. Generally, includes Weight Loss & Liver Support (WLLS) to improve liver fat metabolism, especially important for users with >22% (men) or >32% (women) body fat, with slowing metabolic functions.
Level 2 (Accelerated): Best for intermediate users or those seeking faster results. adding either ThermAccel or CarbRepel to speed calorie burning, reduce carb absorption, and improve overall metabolism.
Level 3 (Comprehensive): Built for advanced users, competitive athletes, or those with aggressive goals. Incorporates AminoFormula for enhanced muscle protection, workout recovery, and plateau prevention alongside all Level 1 & 2 products. This is the fastest route to fat loss while maintaining or building LBM (can be added to any level).
How to Use Them
All levels are designed to integrate easily into daily routines.
MVM is taken daily with meals to fill nutritional gaps and support metabolic processes.
LeanMeal replaces 1–2 meals per day within a 4–5 meal structure, helping control calories and maintain protein intake (~1 g per lb of lean body mass).
Weight Loss & Liver Support* is taken 3-times daily to support overall fat metabolism and control appetite.
ThermAccel or CarbRepel* are used as directed (e.g., ThermAccel 2 tabs twice daily) to boost metabolic rate or CarbRepel to reduce carb calories.
AminoFormula is used pre-, during, and/or post-workout to deliver EAAs that protect and enhance lean tissue. Together, these usage patterns ensure a calorie-controlled, nutrient-dense, high-protein plan that promotes steady fat loss while protecting muscle
* ThermAccel, CarbRepel, Weight Loss and Liver Support are discontinued when goals are achieved
