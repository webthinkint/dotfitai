What the Product Is
dotFIT Brain Health is a targeted brain-nutrition supplement designed to complement a foundational health program (such as a multivitamin, omega-3s, and protein) by supplying specific nutrients known to support brain structure and cognitive function as we age. The formula contains clinically studied ingredients—including phosphatidylserine, acetyl-L-carnitine, alpha-lipoic acid, and vitamin B-12—that help maintain brain cell integrity, energy production, and neurological signaling. The product is designed to help maintain youthful levels of key brain nutrients that naturally decline with aging and typical dietary patterns.
What the Product Does
Helps support healthy cognitive function by nourishing brain cells, supporting neurotransmitter activity, and protecting neural structures from age-related decline. Key ingredients help maintain neuronal membrane integrity, improve mitochondrial energy production in brain cells, reduce oxidative stress, and support healthy neurotransmitter activity involved in memory, mood, and focus. Together, these nutrients help maintain mental performance, cognitive clarity, and overall brain health as individuals age.
Who Would Use It
Brain Health is designed primarily for adults entering midlife and beyond—generally beginning around age 45—when natural production or absorption of certain brain nutrients begins to decline. It is ideal for individuals who want to support long-term cognitive health, maintain mental sharpness, protect against age-related cognitive decline, or enhance brain performance for demanding careers, training, and active lifestyles. It is especially appropriate for people focused on healthy aging and maintaining high levels of mental function throughout their lifetime.
