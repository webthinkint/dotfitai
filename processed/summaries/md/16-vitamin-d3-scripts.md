## Slide 73

Vitamin D3 – The Total Health Hormone
(More than a vitamin – it’s essential for bone, muscle, brain, immunity, and heart health.)

45–60 Second Trainer Script
"Most people don’t realize that Vitamin D isn’t just about bones—it’s about your whole body. In fact, it’s more like a hormone than a vitamin, controlling over 2,000 genes that affect your heart, muscles, brain, and immune system. The problem is, almost no one gets enough from food or safe sun exposure. That’s why supplementation is critical. Taking a daily dose of Vitamin D3 helps keep your levels in the optimal range, which has been shown to reduce risk of fractures, boost strength and recovery, support immunity, and contribute to overall healthy aging. For most people, a minimum of 1,000 to 2,000 IU per day is needed, but some may require more depending on lifestyle and health status. The bottom line—if you’re serious about health, performance, or longevity, Vitamin D3 is one of the simplest and most powerful daily habits you can start right now.“ And always pair with your daily MVM since all vitamins work synergistically, especially D with Vitamins K1 & K2); and daily intake of ~1,200 mg calcium for optimal bone health.
