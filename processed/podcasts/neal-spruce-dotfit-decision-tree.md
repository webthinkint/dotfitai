# dotFIT Product Decision Tree (Neal Spruce's logic)

This is a short version of [neal-spruce-supplement-decision-tree.md](neal-spruce-supplement-decision-tree.md). It uses only the dotFIT products that Neal's logic calls for, taken from `data/Product Data/products.json`. The source document explains the reasoning and cites the podcast timestamps. This document turns that reasoning into a pick list of dotFIT products. Part numbers are in brackets. Flavors of the same product share one entry.

**Doses follow the product label.** Where Neal recommends more for highly active people, the higher amount is shown as an "up to" value for athletes.

---

## 1. Rules that apply to every recommendation

1. **Baseline first, then the goal, then extras.** Keep the person on the baseline for 60–90 days before adding anything optional.
2. **Everyone gets a complete multivitamin/mineral.**
3. **Recommend one product per job.** Use one multi and one creatine source, then count the overlaps (§6).
4. **Protein target: 1 g per lb of lean body mass per day.** A protein powder closes the gap.
5. **Screen first.** Some people are limited to a multi plus protein. This covers anyone with liver disease, a past cardiovascular event, or medications that may interact. It also covers anyone pregnant, nursing, or trying to conceive. Refer them to their doctor.
6. **If stimulants bother someone, give them no caffeine products.** That means no NO7 PreWorkout or ThermAccel.
7. **Under 18: no creatine, glutamine, pre-workouts, or weight-loss products.** See §2 for what teens can use.

---

## 2. Screening

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Liver disease, cardiovascular event, interacting medications | Use only a multi (§3.1) and protein (§3.4). Tell the doctor. |
| Pregnant, nursing, or trying to conceive | dotFIT has **no prenatal product**. Refer to a physician. Several labels say not to use during pregnancy: CollagenComplex, Creatine Monohydrate, Creatine Complex, GlutamineComplex, NO7 PreWorkout, WeightLoss & LiverSupport, ThermAccel. |
| Age 12–17 | Active MV [1005], 1 tablet a day, **or** SuperBlend [8001/8016], 1 scoop a day. Protein powder is fine. Nothing from the under-18 list in §1.7. |
| Under 12 | dotFIT has no product for this age. Refer out. |
| Stimulant-sensitive | Flag it. Use the non-stim options in §4. |

## 3. Tier 1: the baseline (everyone)

### 3.1 Multivitamin/mineral: pick exactly one

```
Can't or won't swallow pills, vegan, or wants one product that does the most?
  → Alln1 SuperBlend [8001/8016]
Otherwise:
  Athlete or trains hard ........................ → Active MV [1005]
  Woman aged 18–50 ............................. → Women's MV [1007]
  Aged 50+ ...................................... → Over 50 MV [1009]
  Adult who doesn't exercise regularly .......... → Active MV [1005], 1 tablet a day
                                                   (or the Women's / Over 50 version if it fits better)
```

| Product | Dose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Active MV** [1005] | 1 tablet a day with a main meal. **Athletes: up to 2** (1 tablet twice a day with meals). | 600 IU vitamin D and 5 mg iron per tablet |
| **Women's MV** [1007] | 1 tablet a day with a main meal | 1,000 IU vitamin D, 10 mg iron, 400 mcg folic acid |
| **Over 50 MV** [1009] | 2 tablets a day with a main meal | 1,000 IU vitamin D, 50 mcg B12, **no iron** |
| **Alln1 SuperBlend** [8001, 8016] | 2 scoops a day. Can be split into 1 scoop morning and 1 scoop evening. Ages 12–17: 1 scoop. | A complete multi **plus** the full Antioxidant formula except astaxanthin, a 10-billion-CFU probiotic, 6 g fiber, flax omega-3 (330 mg), ashwagandha, turmeric, reishi and chaga mushrooms, and 2 vegetable servings. Vegan. This is Neal's own base product. |

Take the multi **with food**. Expect to notice effects after about 45 days. **Never pair SuperBlend with a second multi.**

### 3.2 Omega-3: only if the person eats fewer than 3–4 servings of fatty fish a week

- **Omega-3 Fish Oil** [1020]: 600 mg EPA+DHA per softgel. 1 softgel a day with a meal. **Athletes: up to 5–6 a day.**
- SuperBlend's flax omega-3 does **not** replace this.
- The product is not vegan (fish oil in a gelatin softgel).

### 3.3 Calcium: only if food supplies less than about 1,000–1,200 mg a day (roughly fewer than 3–4 dairy servings)

- **Calcium Complex** [1004]: each tablet has 500 mg calcium, vitamins K1 and K2, 125 mg magnesium, 200 IU vitamin D, and boron. Neal insists calcium come with K2, magnesium, and vitamin D, and this product has all three.
- Dose, with meals:
  - **Women:** 1–2 tablets a day. With 2, take 1 at the morning meal and 1 at the evening meal.
  - **Men:** no more than 1 tablet a day.
- It fits especially well for people who avoid dairy and people who take acid reducers daily.

### 3.4 Protein powder: to reach 1 g per lb of lean body mass

| Situation | Product | Protein per scoop |
|---|---|---|
| Default | **WheySmooth** [1369/1370/1399], or **BULK WheySmooth** [1391/1392] for heavy users | 25 g |
| Wants natural sweeteners (no sucralose or maltodextrin) | **All Natural WheySmooth** [1374/1375] | 25 g |
| Vegan or dairy-free | **Plant Protein** [1300/1301] | 21 g (also contains 3.5 g glutamine and 5 mg iron) |

- Dose: 1 scoop a shake. **Up to 2 scoops (40–50 g protein)** for people with high protein targets.
- None of these powders adds vitamins, so they don't double up on the multi.

## 4. Goal branches (after the baseline)

### 4.1 Muscle gain

```
Novice who is still making progress? → baseline only; add creatine once progress stalls
Otherwise, in this order:
  1. Creatine: pick ONE source
       Tolerates stimulants and wants a pre-workout → NO7 PreWorkout [1214/1215/1217]
       Stimulant-sensitive, or wants a non-stim pre-workout → Creatine Complex [1207]
       Just creatine ............................. → Creatine Monohydrate [1200 flavored / 1227 unflavored]
  2. Essential amino acids around workouts ...... → AminoFormula [1213/1216/1220]
  3. Glutamine: intense or ultra-endurance athletes, or contest prep only → GlutamineComplex [1205]
```

| Product | What's in it | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| **Creatine Monohydrate** [1200, 1227] | 5 g creatine per scoop | Under 175 lb: 1 scoop a day. Over 175 lb: 2 scoops a day. On training days take 1 scoop 30 minutes before the workout. It absorbs better with carbs or protein, so it mixes well into a protein shake. Optional loading phase: 4 scoops a day for 5 days. |
| **Creatine Complex** [1207] | Per scoop: 2.5 g creatine, 3.5 g glutamine, 1.6 g beta-alanine | 2 scoops a day. On training days take 1 before and 1 after training. On rest days take 1 with the morning meal and 1 with the evening meal. **Up to 3 scoops** for people over 150 lb training for strength, speed, or power. This is Neal's non-stim pre-workout (beta-alanine, creatine, glutamine). |
| **NO7 PreWorkout** [1214, 1215, 1217] | Per scoop: 2.5 g creatine, 2 g beta-alanine, 150 mg caffeine, plus citrulline, glycerol, taurine, and Nitrosigine | Under 150 lb: 1.5 scoops. 150–200 lb: 2. Over 200 lb: 2.5. **Never more than 3.** Take 30 minutes before training. On rest days take the same amount at least 5 hours before sleep. Keep this up for at least 28 days. This is Neal's "big three" (caffeine, creatine, beta-alanine), and **it counts as the creatine source**. |
| **AminoFormula** [1213, 1216, 1220] | 8 essential amino acids with **no tryptophan** (4.2 g leucine). About 10 kcal. | Under 150 lb: ½ scoop 10 minutes before and during training, then repeat right after. Over 150 lb: ¾ scoop on the same schedule. |
| **GlutamineComplex** [1205] | 5 g glutamine per scoop, plus 34 mg magnesium and 150 mcg chromium | 1 scoop within an hour after training, plus 1 before bed. **Intense athletes and contest prep: up to 4 scoops (~20 g) a day**, split into 3 doses and started about 6 weeks before a competition. |

### 4.2 Fat loss

```
1. Baseline (multi, omega-3 and calcium if needed, protein)
2. High-protein meal replacement ........................ → LeanMeal [1333/1334]
3. Very overweight → liver support (Neal: "always start there") → WeightLoss & LiverSupport [1100]
4. Tolerates stimulants → thermogenic ....................... → ThermAccel [1102]
5. Optional: AminoFormula [1213] for muscle-building signal with very few calories
6. Later, if budget allows: the Tier 2 health products (§4.4)
```

| Product | What's in it | Dose / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **LeanMeal** [1333, 1334] | 180 kcal, 21 g whey isolate protein, 7 g fiber | 2 scoops in place of a meal or snack. The label says not to replace more than ⅓ of daily calories. Not dairy-free. |
| **WeightLoss & LiverSupport** [1100] | NAC, milk thistle, choline, green tea extract, African mango. **No stimulants** (only 2 mg of natural caffeine). | 1 tablet 3 times a day, 30 minutes before meals. These are Neal's liver-support "lipotropic" ingredients. |
| **ThermAccel** [1102] | 200 mg caffeine per 2 tablets, plus green tea, cayenne, and Sinetrol | 2 tablets before breakfast and 2 before lunch. Caffeine-sensitive people start with 1 tablet twice a day. Nothing within 5 hours of bedtime. This is Neal's thermogenic formula (caffeine, green tea, capsaicin). |

For people who have cycled their weight for years and are considering GLP-1 drugs, Neal says the baseline and protein are **still mandatory** to protect muscle.

### 4.3 Athletes

Start with everything in §4.1, then add:
- **Active MV** [1005], 2 tablets a day
- **Antioxidant** [1000] from day one. Skip it if they already take a full SuperBlend serving (see §6).
- **CollagenComplex** [1003] from their 20s, and right away after any injury or surgery
- **Omega-3 Fish Oil** [1020], up to 5–6 softgels a day
- **Vitamin D-3** [1018] to reach a blood level of up to 70 ng/mL (§5)
- **GlutamineComplex** [1205], only for ultra-endurance events or contest prep
- Where a sport requires certification, pick NSF Certified for Sport products:
  - Antioxidant
  - CollagenComplex
  - Calcium Complex
  - Active MV
  - Vitamin D-3
  - Creatine Monohydrate
  - AminoFormula
  - SuperBlend

### 4.4 General health / longevity (Tier 2, if budget allows), in Neal's order

| # | Need | Product | Dose | Skip if… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Non-vitamin antioxidants (lutein, zeaxanthin, astaxanthin, lycopene, CoQ10, ALA) | **Antioxidant** [1000] | 2 capsules a day with a main meal | They take a full SuperBlend serving, which already has everything except astaxanthin. |
| 6 | Probiotic (+ prebiotic) | **Probiotics** [1017]: 35 billion CFU, 8 strains, with a prebiotic | 1 capsule a day with a meal | — (it can be added on top of SuperBlend's 10 billion CFU) |
| 7 | CoQ10 + ALA (from the late 20s or 30s on; especially for **statin users**) | Covered by **Antioxidant** [1000] or **SuperBlend** | — | — |
| 8 | Collagen (from **age 40**, or earlier for athletes or after an injury) | **CollagenComplex** [1003]: BioCell collagen, hyaluronic acid, chondroitin | Skin: 2 capsules a day, 1 in the morning and 1 at night. **Joints: 2 in the morning and 2 at night.** Take before meals. | Pregnant or nursing. Not vegan. |
| 9 | Brain health (from **age 45**) | **Brain Health** [1001]: acetyl-L-carnitine, phosphatidylserine, ALA, B12 | Ages 45–55: 1 serving (4 tablets) a day. Ages 56–65: 2 servings. Ages 66+: 3 servings. Take each serving with 8 oz of water, and split multiple servings between morning and evening. | Under 45. Contains soy lecithin. |

### 4.5 Aging adults, by age

| Age | Add |
|---|---|
| Any age | Baseline. **Over 50 MV** [1009] once past 50, or SuperBlend at any age. |
| About 30+ | CoQ10 + ALA through **Antioxidant** [1000] or SuperBlend |
| 40+ | **CollagenComplex** [1003]. **Creatine Monohydrate** [1227] (Neal: "you're on creatine"). **AminoFormula** [1213] to counter age-related loss of muscle response to protein. For non-exercisers over 30: ½ scoop 3 times a day between meals, with 1 dose at bedtime. |
| 45–55 | **Brain Health** [1001], 1 serving |
| 56–65 | **Brain Health**, 2 servings |
| 66+ | **Brain Health**, 3 servings |
| Statin user | Make sure CoQ10 is covered (**Antioxidant** or SuperBlend) |

## 5. Optional add-ons (only after 60–90 days on the baseline)

| Need | Product | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Vitamin D blood level below target | **Vitamin D-3** [1018], 1,000 IU per softgel | Take it on top of the multi. After at least a month on the multi, test 25(OH)D. If the level is under 30 ng/mL, add 1 softgel a day and raise the dose slowly until the level is at least 30 ng/mL. **Athletes: up to 70 ng/mL.** |
| Chronic stress | Ashwagandha, which dotFIT sells **only inside SuperBlend** (500 mg) | Try it for 6 weeks. If the person takes pills for their multi, suggest switching to SuperBlend. |
| Joint discomfort or inflammation | CollagenComplex [1003] at the joint dose. Turmeric is available only inside SuperBlend. | — |
| Cold season or weak immunity | **GlutamineComplex** [1205] | 1 scoop a day, plus 1 before bed |

**Needs dotFIT doesn't cover.** Say so plainly instead of substituting a product.
- Prenatal multi
- Multi for children under 12
- Standalone iron: Neal says never take it without a ferritin test.
- Standalone zinc or vitamin C
- BCAAs for ultra-endurance athletes
- Algae (vegan) omega-3
- Standalone turmeric or mushroom extracts

## 6. Overlap check (run on every finished program)

| Combination | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| SuperBlend + another MV | Two multis | Drop one |
| SuperBlend (full serving) + Antioxidant | The antioxidant doses are identical, so they double | Drop Antioxidant. If the person takes only ½ SuperBlend, **1** Antioxidant capsule fills the gap. |
| NO7 + Creatine Complex + Creatine Monohydrate | Creatine piles up | Use one creatine source at its label dose |
| NO7 + ThermAccel | Caffeine from both (NO7: 150 mg a scoop; ThermAccel: 100 mg a tablet) | Use one caffeine product at its label dose. Nothing within 5 hours of bedtime. |
| **Magnesium** from SuperBlend (160 mg) + Calcium Complex (125 mg a tablet) + Active MV (100 mg a tablet) + GlutamineComplex (34 mg a scoop) | Supplemental magnesium is capped at **350 mg a day** | Add it up. For example, SuperBlend + 2 Calcium Complex = 410 mg, which is over the cap. |
| Glutamine from Creatine Complex (3.5 g a scoop) + Plant Protein (3.5 g) + GlutamineComplex (5 g) | Adds up across products | Count it toward the athlete maximum of ~20 g a day |
| Iron from Women's MV (10 mg) + SuperBlend (8 mg) + Plant Protein (5 mg a scoop) + Active MV (5 mg a tablet) | Total iron should stay near 18 mg a day, well under the 45 mg limit | Count the total. Never add a standalone iron supplement. |
| Vitamin D from the multi + Calcium Complex (200 IU a tablet) + Vitamin D-3 (1,000 IU a softgel) | Dose against a blood test | Adjust to the 25(OH)D result |
| Any vegan program | Fish Oil, CollagenComplex, and Vitamin D-3 use gelatin. Whey products and LeanMeal contain milk. | Use SuperBlend, Plant Protein, and the other capsule/tablet products only |

## 7. Example programs

|---|---|
| **25-year-old office worker.** Rarely eats fish, drinks milk daily. Goal: health. Small budget. | Active MV [1005], 1 a day · Omega-3 [1020], 1 a day · WheySmooth [1369]. No calcium. Later, if budget allows: Antioxidant [1000], then Probiotics [1017]. |

| **35-year-old experienced lifter at a plateau.** Tolerates caffeine. | Active MV, 2 a day · Omega-3 · WheySmooth · **NO7 PreWorkout** [1214] (this is the creatine) · AminoFormula around training. Add GlutamineComplex only for contest prep. |

Tell the doctor about everything. |

| **Ultra-marathoner** | Active MV, 2 a day · Antioxidant · Omega-3, up to 5–6 a day · Vitamin D-3, up to a blood level of 70 ng/mL · **GlutamineComplex, up to ~20 g a day split into 3 doses** · CollagenComplex · Creatine Monohydrate. BCAAs aren't available from dotFIT. |
