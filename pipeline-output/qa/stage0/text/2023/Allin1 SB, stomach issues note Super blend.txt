As a note on stomach issues, it doesn’t cause anymore stomach issues than people in certain sub-populations using a protein powder or a pre-workout supplement for the first time might, especially if you follow directions (starting with ½ scoop). However, there is always people that never adjust (or giver up early) to powdered formulas just like people with adverse reactions/allergies to certain foods, which is generally about 10% of users. All this said, it is a great idea to have both started kits!
