Thanks for contacting us. There is no harm eating past 8PM at night unless you are eating too much, or it effects your sleep. Whatever total calories you are consuming should be conducive to your desired body composition and spread throughout the day at the times that work for you. Ideally you would ingest most of your calories during the day while you are active, with dinner being a lighter meal since you would not generally be active too much longer. But again, it doesn’t matter if you are staying within your caloric allotment. See below for ideal eating times for athletes and exercisers.

Staying within the calories conducive to your body composition goal, below are the ideal mealtimes to maximize performance
Meal Timings 
As possible eat every 4-hours
Larger pre-training/event meal 2-3Hr before training
Larger post meal ~30-60min after last post workout supplement
Early morning training
Eat a large pre-training type meal the night before
Consume only the pre-workout snack/shake before training & follow workout day supplement schedule as described
Tournament play (multiple games)
<1.5Hr break: bars and hydration/electrolyte recovery drink
1.5-2.5Hr: small pre-training-type meal
>2.5Hr: normal pre-training meal
Fluid Recommendations* 
16 oz 2Hr before activity (extra 8-16 oz 1hr before on hot days)
4-8 oz every 20 minutes during activity
20 oz for every pound of weight loss post-activity
*Use electrolyte formula (e.g., Gatorade) and water as directed
