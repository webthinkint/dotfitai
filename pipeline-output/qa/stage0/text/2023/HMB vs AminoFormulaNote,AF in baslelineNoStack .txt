Thanks for contacting us. Beta‐hydroxy‐beta‐methylbutyrate (HMB) is a metabolite of the essential amino acid leucine and fairly “old-school” as a dietary supplement for muscle building. The more modern version, ingredients contained in a product called AminoFormula (AF), far surpasses HMB’s potential to aid in recovery and support/build lean body mass (LBM). For these reasons AF is commonly used throughout the sport and fitness industry to improve/maintain size and performance, and especially by older adults to help build muscle or stave off the inevitable. Further it is recommended to all non-exercisers starting in the 4th decade of life to attenuate natural aging muscle loss.
