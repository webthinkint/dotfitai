Consumer message
The newly named AminoFormula is exactly the same active ingredient formula as the former AminoXXXL and therefore same results. The changes are the flavoring package and source of leucine, which now makes all formulas environmentally (and vegan) friendly and just as effective. Further, the minor difference in published calories is because of the new (2022) FDA ruling on amino acids and calories. Below is another message we sent to all that you may have missed. 
Flavor Note:
We have made our popular lemonade AminoFormula (and future other flavors) environmentally (including vegan) friendly by incorporating a different leucine source so we could get away from the duck feather extractions the industry commonly uses. The taste of the new lemonade is naturally less tart, causing some users to ask if the formula was changed. The formula is identical in active ingredients, thus efficacy, but the flavoring is slightly altered by the new leucine source and flavor package. The milder taste makes it more compatible with other flavored fluids (e.g., Gatorade, fruit juices, etc.) or powders (e.g., MuscleDefender, CreatineMonohydrate, ExtremeCreatineXXXL, NO7Rage, etc.). On the next manufacturing run we will tweak the lemonade flavoring towards the previous versions while keeping it environmentally friendly. 
As always, mix it with your favorite fluid – and ours is always natural orange juice.
