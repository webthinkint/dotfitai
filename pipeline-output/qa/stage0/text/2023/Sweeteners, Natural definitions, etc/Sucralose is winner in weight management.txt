Sucralose consumption did not significantly alter body weight
from baseline, but consumers’ weights were trending downward
and were significantly lower than all other treatments at week
12. Sucralose is the most commonly consumed LCS within
the United States (48); yet, to our knowledge, only 1 trial has
examined the effect of sucralose consumption as an exclusive
LCS on body weight (49). The authors concluded that 12 wk of
consumption of ≤500 mg of sucralose/d did not lead to a change
of body weight. The trial was designed to measure sucralose
tolerance (not body weight), the dose was ∼5 times the estimated
daily intake, and was delivered as a 35-g/L solution. The extent
to which sucralose consumption facilitates weight loss requires
additional documentation.
