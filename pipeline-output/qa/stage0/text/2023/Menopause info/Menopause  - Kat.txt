I looked up the latest research on this topic and the answer is yes, Creatine Monohydrate at 5 grams/day in combination with resistance training can help postmenopausal women build muscular strength, functional strength, and muscle mass. Here’s the article for more details https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7998865/

The mid-section weight gain is normal as the body redistributes body fat to the midsection as a protective mechanism since fat excretes estrogen and estrogen levels decline post menopause. Some have referred to this as nature’s life preserver.

Focusing on creating and maintaining a calorie deficit will help reduce overall body fat – when it leaves the midsection depends on many factors.
