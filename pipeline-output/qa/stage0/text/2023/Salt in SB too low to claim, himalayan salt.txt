Sodium is not listed because it’s not in a high enough amount that it needs to be listed*, which is why you see it only in “other ingredients” and listed last (lowest level of anything in the product). This also allows the “no salt” note/claim at the bottom. The Pink Himalayan Salt shown is only part of the flavoring package and exists in an insignificant amount (also contains other minerals/trace elements). 
Hope all is well my friend – keep up the good work!

* According to FDA regulations, a product can contain less than 5 milligrams of sodium per serving and be labeled as "sodium-free" or "salt-free," meaning it does not need to list the sodium content on the label; this is the maximum amount of salt a product can have without needing to list sodium.
