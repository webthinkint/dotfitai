To keep from raising prices, we will use Eurofins (bigger than NSF) for 3rd party testing for products not requesting NSF-CS testing only
NSF and Eurofins normal ingredient 3rd party testing are the same, such as label accuracy, heavy metal content, etc. The added NSF-CS test is testing for banned substances that are on the WADA prohibited list for certain competitive athletes that are randomly drug tested.
Because our manufacturing facilities are all NSF certified, and do not produce pharmaceuticals, none of the products could contain banned substances, making the 2nd more expensive test unnecessary except for teams that only accept NSF-CS for purchase based on legacy contracts or training staff insistence. In other words product contents, purity, efficacy and safety are identical
