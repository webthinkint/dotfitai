Q: Which dotFIT products contain citrus acid? -I’m allergic to it.
None of the dotFIT pills have citric acid and neither do the protein powders, bars or unflavored powders such as unflavored CreatineMonohydrate and Glutamine Complex. The products that might have citric acid will generally be a fruit-flavored powder such as the AminoFormulas, flavored creatine formulas (e.g., Extreme, flavored Creatine, NO7), Alln1 SuperBlend™ (because it’s all natural). The good news is that a dotFIT product contains any citric acid, it will always be listed on the label, generally under “other ingredients”, because they are in minute amounts. Therefore, if you are allergic to citric acids, always check the labels.
