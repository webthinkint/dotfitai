Q: Just looking for a clarification on the digestive enzymes. It states its gluten free, but when I received it the bottle states it's manufactured in a place that may make other products with wheat. So, is this product gluten free safe for celiac? Usually, I do not take anything labeled this why due to concerns of cross contamination. Unless I reach out to the manufacture.  Please advise. Thank you. 

A: The simple answer is to read all labels. While none of our products, except for a few nutrition bars, contain gluten or related ingredients, you only need to read all labels carefully if you believe that any possible/unlikely cross contamination from facilities that may make products with gluten/wheat, may affect your celiac. All our labels have the correct legal warnings when necessary. See below. If you do not see this warning related to your sensitivity, you are good to go. And while the Digestive Enzymes have that warning, although it contains no gluten ingredients, by law you must list if the product is made in a facility that may also use wheat for other products – i.e., in case of some unlikely cross-contamination you may fear based on your sensitivity.

“Allergen Warning: This product was produced in a facility that may also process ingredients containing milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, and soybeans
