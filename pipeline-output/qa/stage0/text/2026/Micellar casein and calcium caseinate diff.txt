Micellar casein is the natural, undenatured form of protein found in milk, obtained via filtration to maintain its micellar structure, which forms a slow-digesting, long-lasting, gel-like structure in the stomach. Micellar casein is best for sustained amino acid release (6-8 hours) for overnight recovery, while calcium caseinate is a chemically treated, faster-digesting, more soluble alternative.

Key Differences:
Production: Micellar casein is made using gentle microfiltration to keep the protein structure intact. Calcium caseinate is created by reacting casein with an alkali (calcium hydroxide).
Digestion Speed: Micellar casein digests very slowly (6–8 hours). Calcium caseinate digests slower than whey but faster than micellar casein (approx. 3–5 hours).
Solubility/Texture: Micellar casein is thicker, less soluble, and forms a gel in the stomach. Calcium caseinate is more soluble in water and mixes better, making it superior for drinks.
Taste: Micellar casein is generally considered to have a better, more natural milk taste.

Which to Choose:
Choose Micellar Casein for: Maximum overnight muscle recovery (anti-catabolic effect) and long-lasting satiety to prevent late-night snacking.
