The most common and clinically studied dose of berberine HCl is:

Standard Daily Dose
500 mg, 2–3 times per day
Total daily intake: 1,000–1,500 mg/day

Because berberine has a relatively short half-life (about 4–6 hours), dividing the dose throughout the day is generally preferred over taking it all at once.

Typical Uses and Doses
Goal Common Dose
General metabolic health 500 mg twice daily (1,000 mg/day)
Blood sugar support 500 mg three times daily (1,500 mg/day)
Weight management 500 mg 2–3 times daily
Lipid/cholesterol support 500 mg 2–3 times daily
PCOS support 500 mg 2–3 times daily
Timing
Usually taken with meals or 15–30 minutes before meals to help improve post-meal glucose control and reduce gastrointestinal discomfort.
Side Effects

The most common side effects are:

Constipation
Diarrhea
Gas/bloating
Stomach discomfort

Starting with 500 mg once daily for 3–7 days and gradually increasing can improve tolerance.

Practical Takeaway

For most adults seeking support for glucose control, body composition, insulin sensitivity, or cholesterol management, 500 mg of berberine HCl taken 2–3 times daily (1,000–1,500 mg/day total) is the dose most consistently used in clinical research and is generally considered the standard effective range.
