For general health, cholesterol reduction, blood sugar control, and digestive regularity, the most commonly studied dose of psyllium husk is:
Typical Effective Doses
General digestive health / regularity
3–5 grams, 1–2 times daily 
Total daily intake: 5–10 grams 
Cholesterol reduction
5–7 grams, twice daily 
Total daily intake: 10–14 grams/day 
Often taken before or with meals 
Blood sugar support
5 grams taken 15–30 minutes before meals 
Total daily intake: 10–15 grams/day 
Weight management / satiety
5–10 grams mixed in water, 15–30 minutes before meals 
Total daily intake: 10–15 grams/day 
Practical Translation
Psyllium husk powders vary slightly by density, but generally:
1 teaspoon ≈ 3–4 grams 
1 tablespoon ≈ 7–10 grams 
Therefore:
A typical maintenance dose is 1 teaspoon twice daily. 
A common therapeutic dose for cholesterol and blood sugar management is 1 tablespoon twice daily. 
Important Considerations
Always take with at least 8–12 oz (240–360 mL) of water. 
Start low (e.g., 3–5 g/day) and increase over 1–2 weeks to minimize gas and bloating. 
Separate from medications and supplements by 1–2 hours, since psyllium can reduce absorption of some compounds. 
Takeaway
For most healthy adults, 5 grams twice daily (10 g/day total) is a practical, evidence-based dose that provides meaningful benefits for digestive health, LDL cholesterol reduction, blood sugar control, and satiety without requiring very large amounts. For those specifically targeting cholesterol lowering, 10–14 grams/day is where many of the strongest clinical benefits have been observed.
