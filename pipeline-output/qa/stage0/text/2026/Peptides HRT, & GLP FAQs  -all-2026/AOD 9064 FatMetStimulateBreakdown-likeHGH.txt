AOD 9604 (Anti-Obesity Drug 9604) is a synthetic peptide fragment of the human growth hormone (hGH). Its mechanism of action primarily targets fat metabolism by stimulating the breakdown of stored fat and preventing the formation of new fat, without the adverse effects on blood sugar associated with full-length hGH. 
Primary Mechanisms of Action
The key ways AOD 9604 works include:
Stimulation of Lipolysis (Fat Breakdown): AOD 9604 actively promotes the breakdown of triglycerides (stored fat) into free fatty acids and glycerol in fat cells (adipose tissue). This process makes the stored fat available for the body to use as energy.
Inhibition of Lipogenesis (Prevention of Fat Formation): The peptide inhibits the process by which the body converts non-fatty foods into body fat, helping to maintain fat reduction over time.
Beta-3 Adrenergic Receptor Interaction: The chronic effects of AOD 9604 on fat metabolism are believed to be partly mediated by enhancing the expression and activity of beta-3 adrenergic receptors (β3-AR) in fat cells, a major receptor involved in lipolysis in rodents.
Increased Energy Expenditure and Fat Oxidation: AOD 9604 increases the overall rate at which the body burns calories and specifically enhances the oxidation (burning) of fat. 
Key Distinctions from Human Growth Hormone (hGH)
AOD 9604 was specifically engineered to harness the fat-reducing properties of hGH while avoiding the unwanted side effects of full hGH therapy. 
No hGH Receptor Binding: Unlike full hGH, AOD 9604 does not bind to the hGH receptor or induce cell proliferation, meaning it does not cause the general growth-promoting effects.
No Impact on Blood Sugar/Insulin: AOD 9604 has no significant adverse effects on blood glucose levels or insulin sensitivity, a major safety concern with hGH treatment.
No IGF-1 Elevation: It does not elevate insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1) levels, further distinguishing its targeted metabolic action. 
In summary, AOD 9604 acts as a targeted metabolic modulator that primarily affects adipose tissue, stimulating fat loss and increasing metabolic rate without disrupting normal hormonal balance or causing the systemic effects of hGH.
