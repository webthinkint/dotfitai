Sermorelin Peptide Overview
Sermorelin is a synthetic peptide analog of growth hormone–releasing hormone (GHRH 1-29). It does not contain growth hormone (GH) itself. Instead, it stimulates the pituitary gland to increase the body’s own pulsatile secretion of GH, which subsequently raises downstream IGF-1 production in the liver and peripheral tissues.
Because it works upstream, sermorelin is generally viewed as a physiologic GH support peptide, rather than a replacement hormone.
How Sermorelin Is Typically Used
1️⃣ Systemic (Non-Site-Specific) Administration
Unlike BPC-157, sermorelin is not used locally at injury sites.
Most commonly discussed uses include:
Age-related decline in GH secretion
Recovery and repair support
Lean body mass preservation
Sleep quality optimization
General “anabolic signaling” support in older adults
Why systemic only:
Sermorelin acts at the pituitary level
Requires intact hypothalamic–pituitary signaling
No local tissue benefit from site-specific injection
Timing Considerations (High-Level)
Sermorelin is most often discussed as being used:
Once daily
In the evening / before sleep
Rationale:
GH secretion naturally peaks during deep sleep
Sermorelin amplifies physiologic pulses rather than overriding them
Lower insulin and glucose levels may improve response

Typical Duration of Use
Short cycles are uncommon. Sermorelin is usually discussed as a longer-term peptide compared to injury-repair peptides.
Commonly referenced durations:
12–16 weeks minimum to assess response
3–6 months is very common
Some protocols extend to 6–12 months with monitoring
Why longer use is typical:
GH/IGF-1 adaptations are gradual
Body composition, sleep, and recovery effects accumulate over time
Pituitary stimulation benefits are not immediate
Commonly Cited Dose Ranges
These are not FDA-approved dosing guidelines—they reflect ranges commonly discussed in clinical practice and educational literature.
Most frequently cited ranges:
200–300 mcg per day (common starting range)
300–500 mcg per day (frequently referenced “full” dose)
Typical patterns described:
Once-daily dosing
Some protocols titrate upward over 1–2 weeks
Higher doses do not always produce greater effects
Important nuance:
Response depends on pituitary capacity
Individuals with severely impaired pituitary function may respond poorly

Key distinctions vs GH injections:
Preserves natural feedback loops
Lower risk of GH suppression
More physiologic hormone rhythms
What It’s Commonly Used For (Educational Context)
Age-related GH decline
Sleep quality and recovery
Lean mass preservation during training or caloric deficit
Adjunct support alongside resistance training
General “recovery capacity” enhancement
Not typically used for:
Acute injury repair
Local tissue healing
Rapid cosmetic body composition changes
Safety & Regulatory Notes (Important)
Sermorelin is not FDA-approved for general therapeutic use
Human data are more robust than many peptides, but still limited
Should only be discussed within medical oversight
Not appropriate for individuals with certain endocrine disorders
Trainers should never prescribe or administer peptides.
