If the primary goal is increasing strength and skeletal muscle mass in a healthy adult who is already training hard, eating enough protein, and recovering properly, I would rank the commonly discussed peptides/compounds approximately as follows:
| Rank | Peptide/Compound | Muscle Gain Potential | Strength Potential | Comments |
| 1 | IGF-1 LR3 | Very High | Very High | Most directly anabolic of the compounds we've discussed. Acts directly on IGF-1 receptors and satellite cells. |
| 2 | Tesamorelin | Moderate | Moderate | Indirectly increases GH and IGF-1. Better known for body composition and visceral fat reduction than pure hypertrophy. |
| 3 | MK-677 (not a peptide) | Moderate | Moderate | Raises GH and IGF-1 continuously. Often produces increases in lean mass, though some is water retention. |
| 4 | Sermorelin | Mild-Moderate | Mild-Moderate | Similar mechanism to Tesamorelin but generally weaker. |
| 5 | BPC-157 | Low direct effect | Low direct effect | Excellent for recovery and injury support, not a muscle-building peptide. |
| 6 | TB-500 | Low direct effect | Low direct effect | Recovery and tissue repair, not primarily anabolic. |
If Your Goal Is Pure Muscle Growth
IGF-1 LR3
Primary Use: Increase skeletal muscle growth, satellite cell activation, muscle recovery, and nutrient partitioning.
Mechanism:
Directly stimulates IGF-1 receptors in muscle tissue. 
Increases satellite cell proliferation and differentiation. 
Enhances protein synthesis. 
May increase glucose and amino acid uptake into muscle. 
Long half-life compared with native IGF-1. 
Among the compounds discussed, IGF-1 LR3 has the strongest direct anabolic effect on skeletal muscle.
If Your Goal Is Strength + Leaner Physique
Tesamorelin
Primary Use: Increase endogenous GH release and reduce visceral fat.
Mechanism:
Potent GHRH analog. 
Increases pulsatile GH secretion. 
Raises circulating IGF-1. 
Improves fat mobilization and body composition. 
Many users report:
Improved recovery 
Better body composition 
Gradual increases in lean mass 
However, Tesamorelin is generally more of a body-composition peptide than a pure hypertrophy peptide.
Combining Tesamorelin + IGF-1 LR3
This is why some physique-focused individuals combine them:
Tesamorelin → raises endogenous GH and systemic IGF-1. 
IGF-1 LR3 → provides a direct anabolic signal at the muscle level. 
The theoretical rationale is:
Improved recovery and fat loss from GH signaling. 
Direct muscle growth signaling from IGF-1 LR3. 
The downside is that this combination produces substantially greater exposure to the GH/IGF axis, so monitoring of:
IGF-1 
Fasting glucose 
HbA1c 
Blood pressure 
Edema/carpal tunnel symptoms 
becomes more important.
Practical Takeaway
For a healthy, resistance-trained adult:
Best for muscle and strength: IGF-1 LR3
Best for improving body composition while supporting muscle: Tesamorelin
Best overall balance of leaner physique, recovery, and gradual muscle gain: Tesamorelin with excellent training and nutrition
Most anabolic combination discussed: Tesamorelin + IGF-1 LR3, though this also carries the greatest uncertainty regarding long-term safety because neither is approved specifically for muscle-building in healthy individuals.
The reality is that for most trained adults consuming ~1.6–2.2 g/kg/day protein and following a progressive resistance-training program, the largest determinant of muscle gain remains training quality and nutrition; peptides may provide incremental benefits on top of that foundation.
