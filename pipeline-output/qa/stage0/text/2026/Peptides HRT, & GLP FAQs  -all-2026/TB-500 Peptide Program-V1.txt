TB-500 (often used with BPC-157 depending on injury grade)
TB-500 is a synthetic fragment of Thymosin Beta-4 (TB4), a naturally occurring protein found throughout the body. It promotes cellular migration and tissue remodeling. Because TB-500 effects appear systemic rather than localized, unlike BPC-157, injection location is generally not injury site specific to determine where healing occur – i.e., TB-500 is typically used as a whole-body recovery peptide rather than a site-specific peptide. If using it for acute injury support, then the duration is generally 4–8 weeks. For significant Tendon/Ligament Injuries, 8–12 weeks may be appropriate, with a tapering in the final 2-4 weeks. TB-500’s longer half-life allows for less daily injections to support efficacy. 
Goal/primary use: support tissue repair, recovery, and reduced inflammation—commonly used for healing injuries or improving mobility including:
• Soft tissue injury support; tendon and ligament recovery; muscle strain recovery
• Mobility and flexibility support; exercise recovery enhancement; General tissue healing
Mechanism*: enhances actin polymerization, increases angiogenesis, and promotes cell migration, accelerating wound repair and reducing fibrosis.
Dose (may vary in syringe volume with methods of reconstitution – follow supplier directions)
First 8 weeks: 2.5 mg twice weekly
Week 9-12: 2.5 mg once weekly
Week 13 off unless maintenance is warranted 
2–5 mg every few weeks is often used as a maintenance
Injection site: abdomen is typically the easiest and most used but thigh, buttocks areas also used but practitioner/user preference since efficacy is not site specific.
Notes
Always complete nutrition programming (including longevity supporting supplementation), sleep, load management, and rehab first – or at a minimum, begin simultaneously when starting peptides.
TP-500 is not FDA-approved for human therapeutic use but commonly used safely
Human data is limited; most mechanistic evidence comes from animal models, but empirical (practical human experience) moderately supports safe positive results when administered and compounded properly.
Because peptides mimic natural substances, when they are metabolized in the body, their constituent AAs can be recycled with little to no stress on organs or metabolism unlike most drugs.
*Purported mechanisms: Increases cellular migration to damaged tissue; promotes angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation); supporting tissue remodeling and repair; regulating inflammation during healing; enhancing actin regulation within cells, which is important for cell movement and repair processes.
