Compared with Injectable Wegovy
Oral Wegovy: approximately $149–299/month for most cash-paying patients. 
Injectable Wegovy: approximately $349/month for self-pay after introductory pricing, although insured patients may pay much less. 
Why This Is Important
This pricing is significant because it lowers one of the biggest barriers to GLP-1 therapy:
No weekly injections. 
Approximately 50–60% less expensive than the injectable version for many self-pay patients. 
Easier to prescribe and likely to increase adoption among people who dislike injections. 
One Caveat
The tablet has stricter administration requirements than the injection:
Take once daily. 
Take first thing in the morning on an empty stomach. 
Swallow with only a small amount of plain water. 
Wait at least 30 minutes before eating, drinking anything else, or taking other medications. 

Given the work we've done together on GLP-1 education for trainers, I also think it's worth watching how this changes the market.
