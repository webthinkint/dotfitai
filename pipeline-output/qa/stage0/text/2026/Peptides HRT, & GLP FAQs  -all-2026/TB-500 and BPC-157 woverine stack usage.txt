In what is commonly referred to as the "Wolverine Stack," BPC-157 and TB-500 are not dosed on the same schedule, even though they are often started at the same time and used synergistically.
The rationale is that they appear to work through somewhat different mechanisms:
BPC-157 = more localized tissue repair signaling, angiogenesis, tendon-to-bone healing, gut repair, and cellular protection. 
TB-500 = more systemic tissue remodeling, cell migration, connective tissue recovery, and mobility support. 
Typical Wolverine Stack Protocol
BPC-157
250-500 mcg daily
Often split: 
250 mcg AM 
250 mcg PM 
Or 500 mcg once daily 
Usually continued for 4-8 weeks 
TB-500
2-2.5 mg twice weekly
Example: 
Monday = 2.5 mg 
Thursday = 2.5 mg 
Total = 5 mg/week 
Usually continued for 4-6 weeks loading 
Followed by: 
2-5 mg every 2-4 weeks maintenance if desired 
Example 6-Week Wolverine Stack
Weeks 1-6
Daily
BPC-157: 500 mcg/day 
Twice Weekly
TB-500: 2.5 mg Monday 
TB-500: 2.5 mg Thursday 
This is probably the most commonly discussed protocol among sports medicine clinics and peptide practitioners.

Site-Specific vs Systemic
One common approach is:
BPC-157
Injected:
Near injured tendon 
Near ligament 
Near joint 
Near muscle injury 
Examples:
Patellar tendon 
Rotator cuff region 
Achilles tendon 
Elbow tendon 
TB-500
Injected:
Abdomen 
Thigh 
Anywhere subcutaneously 
Because TB-500 is generally considered systemic, practitioners usually do not attempt site-specific administration.
Why They Are Combined
The theory is:
BPC-157
Provides:
Local repair signaling 
Angiogenesis 
Tendon-to-bone healing support 
Cytoprotection 
TB-500
Provides:
Cellular migration 
Tissue remodeling 
New blood vessel formation 
Connective tissue recovery 
Together they potentially cover multiple phases of tissue healing.
Practical Duration
For an athlete with a tendon or soft tissue injury:
| Compound | Typical Duration |
| BPC-157 | 4-8 weeks |
| TB-500 | 4-6 week loading phase |
| Combined Stack | 4-8 weeks |
Many practitioners discontinue both once symptoms resolve rather than cycling indefinitely.
Trainer/Educator Takeaway
If discussing the Wolverine Stack in an educational setting, the key point is that BPC-157 is typically administered daily because of its shorter activity profile and localized healing focus, while TB-500 is generally administered only once or twice weekly because of its longer systemic activity. They are started together, but they are not usually injected at the same frequency.
One additional note: despite widespread use in athletic and recovery communities, both compounds remain investigational, human clinical data are limited, and neither is FDA-approved for injury treatment. Therefore they should be viewed as adjuncts to the fundamentals that drive recovery—appropriate rehabilitation, progressive loading, adequate protein intake, sleep, and overall nutrition.
