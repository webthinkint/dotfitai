GUMMIES AS A DIETARY SUPPLEMENT DELIVERY SYSTEM
The Science, Benefits, Limitations & dotFIT Standard
dotFIT POSITION: Gummies can be a convenient, enjoyable and effective delivery system for certain dietary supplements—but the delivery form does not determine efficacy. The right ingredient, at the evidence-based dose, maintained at that dose throughout shelf life, does.
WHY GUMMIES CAN MAKE SENSE
Gummies can improve the consumer experience by being easy to take, portable and highly palatable—potentially improving adherence for people who dislike swallowing pills or using powders. Vitamins, minerals, caffeine, botanical compounds and even sports-performance ingredients can potentially be delivered successfully through gummies when appropriately formulated.
The scientific issue is therefore not whether gummies can work—they can. The question is whether a particular finished gummy product has been demonstrated to work as intended throughout its shelf life.
THE CHALLENGE: A GUMMY IS A COMPLEX DELIVERY MATRIX
Unlike a dry tablet, capsule or powder, gummies contain a water-bearing gel matrix. Their stability can be influenced by:
Water activity • Moisture • pH • Heat • Oxygen • Light • Ingredient interactions • Packaging • Storage temperature • Time
These factors can affect nutrient potency, texture, flavor, microbial stability and ultimately the amount of active ingredient reaching the consumer.
Some vitamins—particularly oxidation-, heat- or moisture-sensitive nutrients—can lose potency during manufacturing and storage. Minerals do not degrade like vitamins, but their chemical form, solubility, taste, interactions and relatively large required doses can make gummy formulation challenging.
This means that how much ingredient goes into the gummy on manufacturing day is not necessarily how much active ingredient remains when the consumer takes it months later.
CREATINE DESERVES SPECIAL ATTENTION
Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched and validated sports-nutrition ingredients available and is extremely stable as a dry powder.
The challenge changes when creatine is placed into a water-containing gummy matrix. Creatine stability is affected by moisture, pH, temperature and time, and under certain conditions creatine can degrade, including conversion to creatinine.
This does not mean an effective creatine gummy cannot be manufactured. Emerging research even demonstrates performance benefits from a verified creatine-monohydrate gummy formulation.
It means a creatine gummy requires greater finished-product validation—not less.
For example, a label stating “5 grams creatine” should not merely mean that 5 grams was originally added. It should mean that the finished serving contains the declared amount and remains within specification throughout its stated shelf life.
The same principle applies to vitamins, minerals, amino acids, energy ingredients and other dietary compounds.
THE dotFIT STANDARD: PROVE WHAT REACHES THE CONSUMER
dotFIT does not select products simply because a delivery format is popular, convenient or tastes good. The science and intended outcome must drive the formulation.
Before putting the dotFIT name on a gummy product, the appropriate validation should include:
✓ Ingredient identity & purity
Verify that the correct raw materials and chemical forms are being used.
✓ Evidence-based dosing
The dose should be based on the scientific literature—not reduced simply to make an ingredient fit into a gummy.
✓ Finished-product potency & uniformity
Confirm that each serving delivers the intended amount after manufacturing.
✓ Stability testing in the actual commercial package
Evaluate the finished product under appropriate real-time and accelerated temperature/humidity conditions.
✓ Shelf-life potency
Demonstrate that active ingredients remain within specification through the stated expiration date—not merely at production.
✓ Degradation testing when appropriate
For ingredients such as creatine, measure both remaining active creatine and relevant degradation markers such as creatinine.
✓ Water activity, moisture, pH & microbiological control
Especially important because gummies are water-containing systems.
✓ Contaminant & quality testing
Apply the same rigorous identity, purity, safety and quality standards expected from other dotFIT products.
THE BOTTOM LINE
dotFIT is not “pro-powder,” “pro-capsule” or “pro-gummy.” We are pro-results.
A gummy can be an excellent delivery system when convenience and taste improve compliance without compromising formulation, evidence-based dosing, potency or stability.
But convenience should never replace science.
THE dotFIT GUMMY STANDARD
If we can't prove what's in it, prove the dose, and prove it remains there through shelf life—we shouldn't put the dotFIT name on it.
