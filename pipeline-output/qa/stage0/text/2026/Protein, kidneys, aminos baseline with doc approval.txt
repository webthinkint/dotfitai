The doctor will make the call on the total protein intake allowed when kidneys are weakened. As long as whey protein is part of the total protein allowed, it would be a proper source. Further AminoFormula may be another way of getting enough protein activity with less nitrogen – just run it by the doc. See more info below for a baseline supplement program and feel free to send to the doctor involved.

More info
The dotFIT Multivitamin and Mineral Formula (MVM) or Alln1 SuperBlendTM (and most other health products as needed) should be fine for this client, but because of the kidney condition, you would want to avoid creatine and too much protein or amino acids, unless the doctor OKs the supplement because the client is not meeting protein needs from whatever specialized diet they may be on. You could have the client show a simple baseline program to the doctor to get approval if desired. I placed both baseline programs below – one with the Alln1 SuperBlendTM and the other with the MVM.
