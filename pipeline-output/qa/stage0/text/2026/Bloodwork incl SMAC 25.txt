| Chemistry/metabolic Panel (SMAC 25) |  |
| Blood chemistry panel, used as a general screening tool by physicians.
Includes:
Amylase, Serum
Comprehensive Metabolic Profile ( includes eGFR ): Albumin, Albumin/Globulin Ratio (calculated), Alkaline Phosphatase, ALT, AST, BUN/Creatinine Ratio (calculated), Calcium, Carbon Dioxide, Chloride, Creatinine with GFR Estimated, Globulin (calculated), Glucose, Potassium, Sodium, Total Bilirubin, Total Protein, Urea Nitrogen
Creatine Kinase (CK), Total, Serum
Gamma Glutamyl Transpeptidase (GGT)
Lactic Acid Dehydrogenase (LD) (LDH)
Lactic Acid, Plasma
Lipid Panel: Cholesterol, total; high-density lipoprotein (HDL) cholesterol; low-density lipoprotein (LDL) cholesterol (calculated); triglycerides; Non-HDL Cholesterol (calculated)
Magnesium, Serum
Phosphate (as Phosphorus)
Uric Acid, Serum (Gout Test)

Patient Instructions: fast for 12 hours preceding collection of specimen. Fasting is defined as no consumption of food or beverage other than water for at least 12 hours before testing. Not be on any intravenous infusion that would affect the acid-base balance. Be in a fasting and resting state (should not exercise). Discuss discontinuing drugs and/or certain dietary supplements before testing. |  |
Add:
Anemia Panel: Iron, TIBC, %Saturation; Ferritin; Vitamin B12
Hematology report
Thyroid Studies: TSH, Free T4, Free T3
Special Chemistry:  %A1C; HS CRP; Homocysteine 
Vitamin D 25-OH
Testosterone/hormone panel (Serum & free testosterone, – if available add DHEA-Sulfate & Estradiol) 
Serum Antigens Panel – i.e., Prostate Specific Antigen
Omega-3 index test if available
ApoB test - people with diabetes, obesity, high triglycerides, or a family history of heart disease, as well as for monitoring the effectiveness of statin therapy. 
Summary
You need 1) Standard chemistry panel (SMAC 25 including GFR); 2) Lipid studies (part of SMAC 25); 3) Anemia panel, 4) Hematology report; 5) Thyroid studies; 6) Tumor markers; 7) Testosterone/hormone tests; 8) Special chemistry including %A1C, High-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hsCRP), Omega-3 index and Vit D.
