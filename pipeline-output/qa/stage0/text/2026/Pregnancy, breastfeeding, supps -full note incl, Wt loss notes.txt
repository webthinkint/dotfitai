Thanks for contacting us. The list of supplements that are commonly used and safe during pregnancy and/or breastfeeding is below.
The weight loss piece needs to be approached carefully with a breastfeeding client. Aggressive calorie restriction can affect milk supply and nutrient density of breast milk. The goal should be gradual, sustainable fat loss — not a hard cut.
A few things that are off the table for now:
- ThermAccel / any stimulant-based thermogenic — not appropriate while breastfeeding, full stop.
- CarbRepel — skip it for now; not the right tool during this phase.
- WeightLoss & LiverSupport — hold off until she's done breastfeeding; better to be conservative here.
The foundational stack (baseline dietary support)— multivitamin, omega-3, calcium, and a clean protein source — is not only safe, it's arguably more important now than at any other time in her life. Her nutrient demands are elevated while breastfeeding, and a calorie-controlled diet makes hitting those daily targets even harder.
Thanks again and have great day!
