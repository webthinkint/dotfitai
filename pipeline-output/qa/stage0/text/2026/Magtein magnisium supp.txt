In 2 grams (2000 mg) of Magtein (Magnesium L-Threonate), you'll find approximately 144 to 145 mg of elemental magnesium, as Magtein is about 7.2% elemental magnesium by weight, with most brands providing around 144-145mg per 2g dose for brain health support. 
Here's the breakdown:
Magtein (Magnesium L-Threonate): A specific compound designed to cross the blood-brain barrier.
Elemental Magnesium: The actual magnesium mineral content.
Calculation: A 2000 mg serving of Magtein typically yields about 144-145 mg of elemental magnesium (2000 mg * ~0.072 = ~144 mg). 
Always check your supplement's label for the exact elemental magnesium amount, as it's the crucial figure for your daily intake. 
 2-gram (2000 mg) serving of a Magtein (magnesium L-threonate) supplement generally provides 144 mg to 147 mg of elemental magnesium. This amount accounts for about 7% to 8% of the total weight of the magnesium L-threonate compound. 
The remaining weight in the 2 grams of magnesium L-threonate is primarily the L-threonate molecule, a water-soluble substance derived from the metabolic breakdown of vitamin C. 
If the product is in capsule or powder form, other inactive ingredients are also present, which vary by manufacturer. Common "other ingredients" found in Magtein supplements include: 
Hypromellose (cellulose capsule)
Rice flour
Stearic acid (vegetable source)
Silicon dioxide
Cellulose
Magnesium stearate 
These ingredients function as fillers, anti-caking agents, or make up the capsule itself, ensuring the product can be manufactured and consumed easily.
