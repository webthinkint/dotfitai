Practical Interpretation for ≥200 lb Athletes
Mechanistic and review data support the rationale that larger athletes may need 5–10 g/day to maintain saturation 10.
Large metaanalyses show creatine works across a wide range of maintenance doses, with some performance signals in >5–8 g/day ranges 1620.
No substantive safety concerns emerge at these doses in healthy athletes 351019.

Bottom line: For a 200+ lb strength/power athlete, 5 g/day is clearly supported, and 5–10 g/day is physiologically reasonable and evidencecompatible when aiming to ensure saturation. Further, emperical data supports higher doses in larger athletes than the 1-size-fits-all or low maintenance dose of conservative science.
