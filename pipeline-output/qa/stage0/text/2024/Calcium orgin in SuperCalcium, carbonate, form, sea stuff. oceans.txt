The calcium carbonate in SuperCalcium is derived from sea minerals and sourced in the USA and Jamaica, depending on availability. It all originates from marine shells/skeleton (see note below).
More Info
Calcium carbonate (CaCO3 – calcium, oxygen and carbon) is one of the most abundant natural compounds on earth (makes up 4% of the earth crust) and because it is the primary constituent of skeletons and shells of different marine organisms, it’s harvested from these sources or from their subsequent sedimentation products. Therefore, the source used in supplements can vary based on availability.
