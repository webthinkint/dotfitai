Basically, you are both correct when choosing between AminoFormula and a Protein powder.
Generally speaking, if you were to only get 2 products and you are an average person, it would be the Multivitamin and Mineral Formula (MVM) and a protein powder to insure you get 1gm/lb/LBM of protein daily. However, if you were an older average adult (>50yrs) then a MVM and AminoFormula (AF) may be more beneficial as long as you are eating enough complete protein (see how I use it below). This is because the AF can overcome an older adult’s natural resistance to protein and exercise better than and intact protein, with the caveat that you still need the total protein intake. The AF will then complement the intact protein (foods) in between meals (or before and after workout) and complement meals with weak protein sources. 
An of course in muscle stacking programs you use both the protein powder and AF. In a meal replacement weight/fat loss program you use the protein as a meal replacement and AF as your pre/post workout supplement. See AminoFormula and Dosing slide below.
