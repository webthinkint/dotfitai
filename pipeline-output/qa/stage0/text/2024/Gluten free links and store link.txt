Q: why did you discontinue the Vegan AminoFormula and why are the calories per servings changing on all the AminoFormulas
A: All AminoFormulas are now environmentally (and vegan) vegan friendly since we incorporated a different leucine source so we could get away from the duck feather extractions the industry commonly uses. Therefore, there was no more need for a separate vegan formula. Further, few people liked the All Natural/Vegan productJ. 
The minor difference in published calories (25, 5, 10) is because of the new (2022) FDA ruling on amino acids and calories, which they just reversed again and therefore we are back to 10cals/svg. NSF approved the calorie amount to be lowered from 25 calories to 10 calories, going forward based on the calorie differences of the individual AAs.
The bottom line is that the calories are insignificant as an energy source no matter how many times they change the labeling laws.
