Fueling bodybuilding workouts adequately with carbohydrates (CHO) is unique to your training phase – I.e., off-season (bulking/pure muscle gain focus) or in season (preparing for competition). Moreover, hypertrophy and strength training do not require the same amount of CHO/LB to optimize a training session when compared to endurance training. In a nutshell after training, for off-season bodybuilders (BB), make sure your CHO intake is ~2:1 CHO/Protein as it should be most of the day. In season obviously begins to slowly reduce CHO intake all the way around, but still having most allowed CHO before and after the workout. I attached a PDF of a lecture I do in for our licensed facilities and certified practitioners that summarizes everything you need to know about optimizing body composition based on the goal (e.g., losing bodyfat while gaining muscle; gaining muscle only; contest prep).
