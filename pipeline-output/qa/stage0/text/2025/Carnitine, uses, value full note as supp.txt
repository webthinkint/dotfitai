Thanks for contacting us. We don’t think much of L-carnitine as a dietary supplement for fitness goals including performance or bodyfat reduction in healthy athletes/exercisers. L-carnitine is used clinically with success in cardiovascular compromised humans (e.g., deficiency states, some cardiovascular/metabolic settings, and nerve/neuropathy contexts). If your goals are fat loss or acute performance, carnitine is not a primary driver. Proper energy balance, protein, creatine, beta-alanine, caffeine, etc., (i.e., evidence-based dietary supplements) and progressive overload, and sleep will washout any benefit of carnitine supplementation. Carnitine can be a nice-to-have for recovery in high dosages (2-4gm/d) and may nudge weight metrics downward over months—especially in higher-BMI clients—as long as the rest of the plan tight.
Hope this helps.
