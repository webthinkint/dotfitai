## Slide 228

Glutamine Complex Product Summary

What is it
Glutamine (GLN-with stabilizing co-factors), which is the most abundant amino acid in the human body, playing vital roles in muscle metabolism, immune system function, gut integrity, and cellular energy. During times of physiological and environmental stress, the body’s natural production cannot meet increased demands leading to rationale to supplement to support overall health and daily recovery.
What does it do
Supplies high dose GLN to help restore levels needed to maintain GLNs many vital roles in muscle metabolism, immune system function, gut integrity, and cellular energy. Supplementation – during times of physical stresses, such as intense exercise, illness, injury, or prolonged caloric restriction when the body’s GLN levels fall up to 50% - helps fill the body’s depleted GLN pool to meet the increased demands and support GLNs indispensable roles in establishing and maintaining health and daily recovery including:
  Reducing  muscle breakdown while supporting  muscle protein synthesis
  Supporting gut health and overall immunity by supplying  and increasing the primary fuel for these cells to improve performance during stress
  Increasing important antioxidant activity to speed recovery throughout the body
  Increasing the activates of the key signaling pathways (e.g., mTOR) critical for muscle growth and repair
Who would use it
Athletes and exercisers under prolonged demanding physical stress, and especially combined with extended periods of energy restriction as in weight/body fat conscious athletes attempting to “make weight” or attain extremely low body fat
Anyone attempting to support:
  The immune system (especially during seasonal high health risk periods)
  Gut health, including supporting intestinal integrity to reduce or protect against excessive gut permeability (AKA “leaky gut”)
  General daily recovery
How to use it
General instructions to support immunity, gut health and overall exercise recovery
  5gms (1scoop) 3 times daily split evenly throughout the day. If exercising take 1-dose before activity, 1-after, and 1-before bed (total 15gm/day)
  Alternatively, working with a Practitioner, they may dose by body weight at ~.1gm/LB/D divided around activities and total wake time

Recover from excessive stresses by enhancing glutamine’s role in  supporting gut health, immunity and exercise-induced gains to play better longer
