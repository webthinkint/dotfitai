What is it
Creatine Monohydrate by dotFIT is a clinically validated, NSF Certified for Sport® supplement for both males and females, designed to increase the body’s natural creatine stores, primarily in muscle and brain tissue. It contains the most research-backed form of creatine—creatine monohydrate—in a pure, stable, and vegan-friendly powder. Each dose delivers a highly absorbable source of immediate energy to support peak physical and cognitive performance, recovery, and long-term health.
What does it do
Creatine Monohydrate fuels the body’s fastest energy system—phosphocreatine—helping regenerate ATP during high-intensity exercise. This translates to stronger workouts, greater training volume, and improved strength, power, and muscle mass. Additionally, it plays a role in enhancing brain energy metabolism, aiding mood, cognition, and possibly even neuroprotection. In aging adults, creatine supports muscle preservation, bone density, and physical function, making it a valuable tool across the lifespan.
Who would use it
This product is ideal for a wide range of male and female users:
Athletes in sports requiring explosive bursts (e.g., football, soccer, sprinting, weightlifting).
Active adults looking to break through training plateaus, improve training motivation, or improve body composition.
Older adults interested in supporting healthy aging, muscle retention, and bone strength.
Vegans/vegetarians, who typically have lower creatine stores due to dietary limitations.
Individuals with high cognitive or physical demands, including students, professionals, and tactical populations

How to Use It
To maximize benefits, users may begin with a loading phase: 0.14 g per pound of body weight per day (e.g., 20g daily for a 150 lb person), divided into four 5g daily doses over 5 days. This is followed by a maintenance phase of 0.045g per pound of body weight[LBM] per day (typically 5–10g daily), split into two doses taken with meals such as with pre & post workout meals or shakes. For those who prefer to skip loading, 5-10g/ daily will saturate muscles within about 30 days. Older adults and those supplementing primarily for health purposes can use a consistent 5g daily dose with a meal or shake.
