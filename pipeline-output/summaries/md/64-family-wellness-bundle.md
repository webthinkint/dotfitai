What Is the Family Wellness Bundle?
The Family Wellness Bundle is a foundational “Health Cabinet” system built around three daily nutrition essentials: a complete multivitamin and mineral formula, FirstString® protein powder, and SuperOmega-3. Together, they help close the most common nutrient gaps found in modern family diets: vitamins and minerals, high-quality protein, and EPA/DHA omega-3 fats. ActiveMV or Women’sMV provides the daily micronutrient foundation, FirstString® helps family members meet protein and recovery needs, and SuperOmega-3 supports heart, brain, immune, joint, vision, and exercise recovery health.
Protein bars and electrolytes can be added as practical support tools: bars for convenient protein and body composition goals, and electrolytes for hydration during heat, heavy sweating, sports, travel, or low-carbohydrate diets.
How Does It Work?
The bundle works by helping the family consistently meet the daily nutrition targets that food alone often misses. The MVM fills vitamin and mineral gaps that affect energy, metabolism, immune function, growth, recovery, and long-term health. FirstString® supplies quality protein, amino acids, and performance carbohydrates to help support muscle repair, growth, glycogen restoration, and active lifestyles. SuperOmega-3 provides EPA and DHA, the essential fats most people under-consume, supporting cell structure, inflammation resolution (anti-inflammatory agent), brain and cardiovascular function, joint comfort, and recovery.
Bars and electrolytes extend the system by making it easier to stay on plan when life gets busy or hydration demands increase.

Who Would Use the Bundle?
This bundle is ideal for active families, especially households with youth athletes, exercising adults, busy parents, and anyone trying to stay healthy, strong, and active long term. Youth ages 12 and older can use ActiveMV with FirstString® and omega-3 support to help growth, development, school focus, sports performance, and recovery. Women ages 18–50 may use Women’sMV unless they are highly active athletes, where ActiveMV may be preferred. Adult men, athletes, and regular exercisers use the bundle to support energy, lean mass, recovery, performance, and body composition. Older active adults can use the same strategy to help preserve strength, mobility, and independence.
Protein bars are best for busy family members needing convenient protein, while electrolytes are most useful for heavy sweaters, tournament athletes, outdoor activity, heat, travel, fasting, or low-carb diets.
How To Use the Bundle
Use the MVM daily with meals based on age, gender, and activity level. Use FirstString® daily to help meet protein needs, especially around exercise: before activity to support energy and reduce muscle breakdown, after activity to support recovery and muscle-building, or between meals as a smoothie or convenient nutrition boost. Use SuperOmega-3 daily when fatty fish intake is low, generally 1–2 soft-gels with meals depending on weekly fish intake.
Add protein bars as needed for between-meal snacks, travel, school, work, tournaments, or body composition support. Use electrolytes as needed in water during or after heavy sweating, intense exercise, heat exposure, travel, or hydration challenges.
