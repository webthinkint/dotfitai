## Slide 59

Over50MV Product Summary

What is it?
Over50MV is dotFIT's daily multivitamin and mineral formula designed specifically for adults over 50. It delivers the essential vitamins and minerals your body still needs as you age — without iron, which research shows can be problematic for some older adults — plus higher amounts of B12, choline, and other nutrients that become harder to absorb or more important as you get older.
What does it do?
Over50MV fills the everyday nutrition gaps that food alone often can't cover. It provides all the known potentially under-consumed vitamins and minerals at corrective doses, designed to bring your daily intake up to recommended levels. Vitamin D and magnesium support bone health, energy, and sleep. Both forms of vitamin K — K1 and K2 — support cardiovascular health and direct calcium where it belongs. Two forms of B12, including methylcobalamin, support brain health and neurological function. Choline, often missing from typical multivitamins, supports brain, liver, and muscle health. And the carefully balanced antioxidants help your body manage the daily oxidative stress of aging. It's everyday foundational support for staying strong, sharp, and active.
Who is it for?
Over50MV is for adults 50 and older who want to maintain energy, brain function, bone health, and overall vitality as they age. Active adults who've graduated from the demands of their younger years but still want comprehensive daily nutrition. Anyone whose diet doesn't consistently include all the food groups that supply these essential nutrients. If you train hard and intensely, you may want to stay on ActiveMV through your mid-60s before transitioning. Otherwise, Over50MV is the right daily foundation from 50 onward.

## Slide 60

How do you use it?
Simple. Take two tablets daily with your favorite beverage after your first meal of the day. Taking it with food helps with absorption and is gentler on your stomach. Splitting your 2-tabs, 1 with an AM Meal and 1 with a PM meal for greater daily utilization. Pair it with a complete diet, and add other dotFIT products like Calcium Complex or Antioxidants if your specific needs call for it.
What makes ours different?
Ours isn't a generic, store-bought senior multivitamin. Many products in this category either keep iron in — which can be a problem for older adults with unknown cardiovascular concerns — or skip the cofactors that make the rest of the formula work. We don't take that approach. Over50MV uses both forms of vitamin K and both forms of B12, including methylcobalamin, the form most effective for brain support. It includes choline, an essential nutrient over 90 percent of Americans fall short on. Magnesium is delivered as magnesium citrate for better absorption. Iron is intentionally left out so you can supplement separately only if a blood test confirms you need it. And it's 3rd party tested and manufactured to pharmaceutical standards exclusively for dotFIT. Every ingredient, every form, every dose is chosen for adults over 50 — not just rebadged from a younger formula.
