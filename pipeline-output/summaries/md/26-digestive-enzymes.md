What Is the Product?
Digestive Enzymes is a dietary supplement designed to support and enhance the body’s natural digestive process. The formula contains a complex of five naturally occurring digestive enzymes—neutral protease, α-amylase, lipase, lactase, and cellulase—derived from microbial fermentation sources. These enzymes assist in the breakdown of proteins, carbohydrates, fats, lactose, and plant fibers so that the body can properly release and absorb the essential nutrients contained in foods. What Does It Do?
Digestive enzyme supplementation helps improve the breakdown of foods into their absorbable components—such as amino acids, glucose, fatty acids, vitamins, and minerals—so they can be effectively utilized by the body. When digestion works efficiently, nutrient extraction and absorption are optimized, supporting overall metabolism, tissue repair, and physiological function. Supplementing enzymes may also help manage common non-clinical digestive issues such as bloating, gas, cramps, lactose intolerance, and occasional gastrointestinal discomfort. Additionally, emerging research suggests that certain enzymes—particularly proteases—may help reduce inflammation and support recovery following strenuous exercise by assisting with tissue repair and regulating inflammatory responses.

## Slide 113

Who Would Use It?
Digestive enzyme supplementation may benefit individuals who want to improve digestive efficiency and nutrient absorption from their diet. It may be particularly useful for people experiencing occasional digestive discomfort such as bloating, gas, or lactose intolerance, or those whose natural enzyme production may be reduced due to aging, lifestyle factors, or dietary changes. Active individuals and athletes may also benefit, as improved nutrient absorption supports recovery and performance, and some enzyme complexes may help reduce exercise-induced muscle soreness. Overall, it is intended for individuals seeking better digestion and improved utilization of the nutrients they consume.
How to Use It
Digestive enzyme supplements are typically taken with meals to assist the body in breaking down the foods being consumed. The recommended use is three capsules daily, taken as one capsule with each of the three main meals, or as directed by a qualified health professional. Taking enzymes alongside food allows the enzymes to participate in the digestive process at the time nutrients are being broken down and absorbed. If the supplement is being used primarily for digestive discomfort and no improvement is noticed after about 30 days of proper use, continued use for that purpose may not be necessary.
