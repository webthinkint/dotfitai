## Slide 266

Establishing a Playspan® = to a Lifespan

Core Message: Creating a long, healthy, and active life depends on three undisputed cornerstones: (1) meeting nutrient recommendations, (2) regular physical activity, and (3) maintaining healthy body composition—always in that order
Without adequate nutrition, activity and healthy weight cannot be sustained, and the body breaks down earlier than necessary, leading to dependence on prescription drugs and the “sick-care” model
The Problem – Silent Hunger & Early BreakdownMost Americans experience “silent hunger”: chronic under-consumption of essential vitamins and minerals. This under-nutrition is not felt in the stomach but manifests as daily under-performance, weight gain, early aging and onset of chronic illnesses such as weakened cardiovascular & respiratory systems, osteoporosis, and dementia
With only ~1 serving of fruit and 1-1.5 servings of vegetables consumed daily—far below the recommended ≥5 servings—nutrient gaps are inevitable
The result: a Playspan® (youthful active years) that averages only about 35 years within a lifespan of only ~77yrs

## Slide 267

The Solution – Fill the Gaps to Stay ActiveA simple, calorically efficient daily dietary support program delivers a complete multivitamin/mineral formula (20 known under-consumed VMs), 500–600 mg of omega-3s (EPA/DHA), and 1 g of protein per pound of lean body mass, plus targeted extra calcium as needed
These nutrients are the “building and rebuilding materials” that keep muscles, brain, skin, and immune systems strong while starving excess body fat. This approach fuels activity and recovery, helping people achieve and sustain a healthy body composition and keep their Playspan® equal to their Lifespan
Compelling NarrativeWe break too early not because of aging itself but because of preventable under-nutrition. By feeding our “silent hunger” with scientifically designed supplementation, we drive activity, maintain independence, and reduce reliance on costly prescription drugs. Prevention is far easier—and cheaper—than cure.
Fitness professionals are perfectly positioned to lead this change and “save the world through fitness” by turning health care into true self-care

## Slide 268

Actionable Takeaways for Everyone
Start with Nutrition: Daily nutrient recommendations are the foundation of lifelong health; exercise and healthy weight follow naturally.
Correct Silent Hunger: Even with the best diet, at least 10 essential nutrients are potentially under-consumed in the U.S.—use a complete multivitamin/mineral formula to close the gap.
Add Essential Omega-3s: Support cardiovascular, immune, and cellular health with 500–600 mg EPA/DHA daily.
Meet Daily Protein Goals: 1 g protein per pound of lean body mass, split into 4–5 servings, ensures optimal muscle recovery and metabolic health.
Personalize & Monitor: Adjust calcium and other support nutrients (e.g., joint, brain, probiotics) as needed based on age, goals, and diet.
Invest in Prevention: Spend a little now on proven supplementation to avoid spending far more later on medications and sick-care
