## Slide 143

FirstString Product Summary (PDSRG)
It’s not just a protein shake—it’s a precision-designed performance system in a shaker cup.

What the Product Is
FirstString™ is a scientifically formulated, rapidly digesting “food-form” performance shake that combines carbohydrates, with a premium 4-stage specialized controlled release protein blend of predominately whey isolate and whey concentrate for fast amino acid release combined with calcium caseinate and micellar casein for sustained release, and low fat in an optimal ratio (~2:1 CHO: protein). It is designed to function as both a training fuel and recovery formula including , meeting NSF Certified for Sport standards and aligning with NCAA nutrition guidelines.
What It Does
FirstString™ delivers targeted nutrient timing support to maximize training outcomes. Pre-workout, it helps fuel glycogen stores and reduce muscle breakdown by delivering essential amino acids (especially leucine). Post-workout, it enhances the muscle protein synthesis (MPS) response and accelerates glycogen replenishment during the critical metabolic window, leading to improved recovery, performance, and long-term gains, while also delivering whey’s natural occurring bio-active health, immune and growth factor compounds.
Who Would Use It - FirstString™ is ideal for:
Strength and power athletes (bodybuilders, powerlifters, sprinters)
Team sport athletes (football, basketball, soccer, etc.)
Short- to mid-distance endurance athletes
Youth athletes needing quality calories for growth/development including using as a delivery system for fruits/veggies – i.e., protein rich smoothies
Anyone needing clean, effective weight gain or performance nutrition support
It’s especially valuable for individuals who train hard and need precise, convenient nutrition to match performance demands.
