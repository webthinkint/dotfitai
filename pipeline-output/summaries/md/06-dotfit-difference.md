## Slide 24

Dietary Supplements – The dotFIT Difference

Why Switch — Shortcomings of Mass-Market Supplements
Price wars → under-formulation. Many mass brands cut doses or use cheaper forms to hit price points; several well-known labels have been called out for under-delivering actives.
Protein “spiking” and label games. Class-action suits documented products inflating “protein” with cheap nitrogen sources; big distributors/brands were implicated.
Frequent quality violations. FDA inspections have cited >50% of facilities for cGMP/quality lapses (purity/testing errors, spiking). Hidden drug ingredients appear regularly on FDA’s health-fraud lists.
Online marketplace inconsistencies. Independent checks show many high-rated products bought online have fake reviews and don’t match labels; multiple items were misbranded, under-potent, or contained prohibited substances.
Retail channel risk. Even major retailers have faced bans/concerns from elite sport bodies—another reminder that consumer channels are not built around practitioner-level controls.
