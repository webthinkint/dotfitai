ThermAccel Product Summary (PDSRG)
Break through plateaus. Burn more. What is the Product?
ThermAccel™ is a scientifically-formulated, multi-target dietary supplement designed to accelerate weight and fat loss by increasing total daily energy expenditure (TDEE), enhancing fat oxidation, and supporting appetite control. It combines proven natural ingredients such as caffeine, EGCG (from green tea), capsaicin, Sinetrol™ polyphenols, Caralluma fimbriata, and L-theanine into a synergistic thermogenic blend. Uniquely crafted to deliver both immediate and sustained energy through two-stage release technology, ThermAccel stands apart from typical stimulant-based formulas by offering a more balanced energy profile—avoiding the fatigue or “crash” often experienced with conventional fat burners.

What Does it Do?
ThermAccel enhances metabolism by stimulating thermogenesis (heat production), promoting lipolysis (fat breakdown), and increasing fat oxidation (burning)—allowing the body to burn more calories at rest and during daily activities. Additionally, it supports appetite regulation through compounds that act on the hypothalamus, gut hormones, and neurotransmitters to reduce hunger and cravings. The formula is specifically designed to help users overcome weight loss plateaus and maintain metabolic momentum, even when traditional methods like diet and exercise begin to stall. By activating brown adipose tissue (BAT), regulating energy partitioning, and enhancing mood and alertness, ThermAccel supports both physical performance and adherence to weight loss goals.

Who Would Use It?
Ideal for individuals seeking a safe yet powerful edge in their weight loss journey—especially those who are already following a sound diet and exercise plan but have reached a plateau. It’s best suited for healthy adults without sensitivity to caffeine or stimulant ingredients who need additional support with energy, metabolism, or appetite control. Personal trainers, fitness enthusiasts, and anyone motivated to reduce body fat while minimizing the hardships of cutting calories or increasing exercise volume would benefit. It is also an option for those seeking a natural alternative to prescription fat-loss medications, without the risks associated with pharmaceuticals.

How to Use It?
The recommended use is to take two (2) tablets at breakfast and two (2) with lunch, accompanied by at least 8 oz of fluids. Individuals sensitive to caffeine should begin with one tablet twice daily to assess tolerance before increasing to the maximum dose of four (4) tablets daily. ThermAccel should not be used with other stimulants or by individuals taking heart medications. Once weight loss goals are achieved and lifestyle changes are firmly in place, ThermAccel can be discontinued.
