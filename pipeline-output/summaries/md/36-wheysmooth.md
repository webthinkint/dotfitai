With 25 grams of complete protein per scoop and only 160 calories, it's the simple way to support muscle, recovery, and overall health — at any stage of life. Available in chocolate, strawberry and vanilla.

WheySmooth fuels what your body builds and rebuilds. Through it’s controlled release proteins It delivers immediate and sustained muscle protein synthesis to help preserve lean muscle during weight loss, speeds recovery after training, and provides the natural health, immune and growth factor bio-actives found only in true whey concentrate. Whether you're chasing performance, managing your weight, or staying strong as you age, this is the protein that does more per scoop.
Who is it for?
WheySmooth is for almost everyone. Active adults and athletes who want to maximize training results. Anyone in a calorie deficit who wants to protect lean muscle while losing fat. Aging adults working to maintain strength and independence. Even busy people who just need a quick, high-quality protein source. If you're not consuming enough complete protein from food to support your fitness goals, WheySmooth fills the gap.

WheySmooth Product Summary (PDSRG)

How do you use it?
Simple. Mix one scoop with water, milk, or your favorite ingredients — fruit, oats, nut butter, whatever fits your goals. Use it before or after workouts to support recovery, between meals to hit your daily protein target, or before bed to support overnight muscle repair. Adjust the serving size to match your needs — one and a half scoops gives you 40 grams of protein in just 240 calories.
What makes ours different?
Ours isn't a generic, store-bought protein powder. WheySmooth is a premium, low-calorie, controlled-release protein system that uses true whey protein concentrate, not just isolate — so you get the natural growth factors, immunoglobulins, and bio-actives that support overall health, not just muscle. It's high in leucine — three grams per scoop — the key amino acid that triggers muscle protein synthesis. Naturally low in lactose, easy to digest, and easy to mix. NSF Certified for Sport, third-party tested for purity, potency, and banned substances. And manufactured to pharmaceutical standards exclusively for dotFIT. That's the level of quality most protein powders simply can't match.
