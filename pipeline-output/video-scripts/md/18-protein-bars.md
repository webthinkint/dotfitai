## Overview

dotBARs and dotWAFERs are dotFIT's grab-and-go protein line — a rotating range of flavors and macro profiles built on one promise: real nutrition you can carry in a pocket. Every one delivers at least 12 grams of protein along with fiber, so it works as a satisfying snack, a smarter way to handle a sweet craving, or a portion-controlled meal substitute when whole food isn't an option. Whatever flavor you reach for, you're getting protein that helps curb hunger — not just empty calories. dotBARs & dotWAFERs — real protein, ready when you are.

## What is it?

The dotBAR and dotWAFER line is a selection of protein-rich bars in a variety of flavors and macro profiles. Some are lighter and lower in sugar; others are richer and more dessert-like; there's a crispy wafer and options built around real nuts. The flavors rotate over time, but the standard behind them doesn't: every bar delivers a minimum of 12 grams of protein and a good source of fiber in a single, portable serving — and many are microwaveable if you want yours warm.

## What does it do?

Protein is the most satisfying of the three macronutrients — it blunts hunger and takes more energy to digest than carbs or fat. That's the idea behind every dotBAR and dotWAFER: a protein-forward, portion-controlled serving that helps you stay full and on track between meals. As a snack, one helps tame a craving without derailing your day; as a meal substitute when you're short on time, it gives you built-in portion control that can support a weight-management plan. The protein also supports muscle maintenance and recovery.

## Who is it for?

It's for anyone who needs real nutrition on the move — busy professionals, parents, travelers, students, and anyone who tends to skip meals or grab vending-machine junk when whole food isn't around. Because the line spans different flavors and macro ratios, there's a fit for different goals: lighter, lower-sugar options when you're watching calories; heartier ones when you want a fuller meal substitute; crispy or nut-based choices when you want variety. Ingredients and allergens differ from bar to bar, so check each label if you have a food allergy.

## How do you use it?

Keep them where you'll actually reach for them — your bag, your desk, the gym, or the car (out of the heat). Have one as a between-meals snack to manage hunger, as a quick pre-workout bite, or in place of a meal when whole food isn't convenient and you want easy portion control. They're microwaveable if you'd like yours warm. As with any meal-substitute approach, use them as part of a balanced overall diet rather than a wholesale replacement for whole foods, and drink plenty of water alongside.

## What makes it different?

Plenty of "protein" bars are candy in disguise — light on protein, heavy on sugar and filler. Every dotBAR and dotWAFER is held to a real standard: a minimum of 12 grams of quality protein and a good source of fiber in every serving, from a nutrition company with more than 20 years behind it. The flavors are refreshed regularly so the lineup stays interesting, but that nutrition floor never drops — which is exactly what you can't count on from a typical grocery-aisle bar.
