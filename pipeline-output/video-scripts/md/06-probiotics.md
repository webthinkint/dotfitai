## Overview

Probiotics delivers 35 billion live cultures from eight strains of the two most-studied beneficial bacteria — Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium — plus a prebiotic blend to feed them. Diet, stress, aging, and antibiotics can throw your gut's bacterial balance off, and since so much of your digestion and immune defense lives in the gut, that balance matters. Taken daily, it helps restore and maintain the good bacteria that keep your gut — and the rest of you — running well. Probiotics — restore the balance your gut depends on.

## What is it?

Probiotics is a once-daily capsule with 35 billion CFUs from eight strains of Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium, the two most-researched families of beneficial gut bacteria. It also includes a prebiotic blend — fiber that feeds those bacteria — making it a complete pro- and prebiotic formula in one. The strains are specially acid-resistant, so they survive stomach acid and reach the gut alive, and they're shelf-stable without refrigeration. It's third-party tested for guaranteed potency through the expiration date printed on the bottle.

## What does it do?

Your gut is home to trillions of bacteria, and keeping the beneficial ones dominant is central to your health: they help you digest food and absorb nutrients, crowd out harmful bacteria, and support your immune system — much of which lives in the gut lining. Everyday factors like diet, stress, aging, and antibiotics can tip that balance the wrong way. Probiotics helps restore and maintain a healthy mix, supporting digestion, nutrient absorption, and immune resilience, while the prebiotic blend nourishes the good bacteria already living there.

## Who is it for?

It's for any adult who wants to support a healthy, balanced gut — especially if diet, age, ongoing stress, or a recent round of antibiotics may have thrown it off. Because the gut is so closely tied to immunity, it's also useful for athletes in prolonged, intense training, who are more prone to the colds and sniffles that heavy training can invite. If you have a compromised immune system or a serious GI condition, check with your doctor first, and if you're on antibiotics, separate the two by a couple of hours.

## How do you use it?

Take one capsule daily, with a meal. It doesn't need refrigeration, so it's easy to keep on the counter or pack when you travel. If you're taking an antibiotic, space your probiotic at least two hours apart, since antibiotics can knock back the live cultures. Building and maintaining a healthy gut population is about consistency, so steady daily use over time matters more than any single dose.

## What makes it different?

Three things set it apart. First, potency you can actually count on: it's guaranteed to deliver 35 billion live cultures through the printed expiration date — not just on the day it was bottled — because each capsule is filled with significantly more to start. Second, the strains are acid-resistant, so they survive the stomach and arrive in the gut alive, with no refrigeration needed. Third, it pairs eight researched strains with a prebiotic blend that feeds them. And it's third-party tested throughout production for verified potency and stability.
