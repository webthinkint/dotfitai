## Overview

Simply put, Brain Health provides targeted brain nutrition designed to maintain youthful levels of key neurological nutrients that naturally decline with age. By supplying scientifically supported compounds involved in brain cell health and protection, mitochondrial energy production, and neurotransmitter activity, the formula helps support mental clarity, cognitive function, and long-term brain health as part of a comprehensive healthy-aging strategy.

## What is it?

Advanced Brain Health is a targeted nutritional formula designed to support brain structure and cognitive function as you age. It combines four well-researched ingredients — phosphatidylserine, acetyl-L-carnitine, alpha-lipoic acid, and vitamin B12 — some of which the body produces naturally but in declining amounts over time combined with the natural age-related decline in absorption from food. It's the brain support layer of the dotFIT longevity program.

## What does it do?

This formula supports the systems that keep your brain sharp. It helps maintain memory, focus, and mental clarity, supports healthy nerve and brain cell function, and supplies antioxidants that protect against age-related oxidative stress. It also supports mood and emotional balance — because how you feel and how you think are deeply connected.

## Who is it for?

Advanced Brain Health is built for adults 45 and older who want to stay mentally sharp and protect long-term cognitive function. Whether you're noticing the first small shifts in memory or focus, or you simply want to be proactive about brain health, this formula is dosed to match what your body needs as it ages.

## How do you use it?

Dosing scales with age. Adults 45 to 55 take one serving — four capsules — with any meal. Ages 56 to 65 take two servings, split between morning and evening meals. Over 65, take three servings — one with breakfast, two with dinner. Pair it with your daily multivitamin, omega-3, and antioxidant for full coverage.
