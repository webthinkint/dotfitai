## Overview

WeightLoss & LiverSupport is a non-stimulant fat-loss aid with a difference: it also supports the organ that runs your metabolism — your liver. Carrying excess body fat can bog the liver down and make it harder to use dietary carbs and fat efficiently. This formula combines choline, milk thistle, NAC, green tea extract, and African mango to support the liver's natural fat-processing and antioxidant defenses while supporting fat metabolism and appetite control. It's built to ease and speed a healthy fat-loss journey alongside diet and exercise. WeightLoss & LiverSupport — support the organ that runs your metabolism.

## What is it?

WeightLoss & LiverSupport is a non-stimulant supplement that pairs body-fat-reduction support with liver support. It brings together five researched ingredients: choline, which helps the liver process and export fat; milk thistle (silymarin) and NAC, which support the liver's antioxidant defenses; green tea extract standardized for EGCG, which supports fat metabolism and antioxidant activity; and African mango (Irvingia gabonensis), which supports appetite control and healthy lipid levels. It's taken three times a day, and it's third-party tested.

## What does it do?

Your liver is the body's main nutrient processor — the first stop for the carbs, fats, and protein you eat. When excess body fat accumulates, it can interfere with how efficiently the liver does that job, which works against your metabolism. This formula supplies nutrients that support the liver's natural ability to process and clear fat (choline) and bolster its antioxidant defenses against the oxidative stress that comes with carrying extra weight (NAC, milk thistle, EGCG). Meanwhile, green tea extract supports fat metabolism and African mango supports appetite control — so the overall effect is to support healthy metabolism and make fat loss a little easier.

## Who is it for?

It's for adults actively working on meaningful body-fat reduction who want non-stimulant support — no caffeine load, no jitters — and who want to support their liver health as part of the process. Because it's stimulant-free, it suits people who are sensitive to or want to avoid stimulant-based fat burners. It can be used on its own or as part of the dotFIT 90-Day Weight Loss Solution (LeanPak90). It's meant to be discontinued once you reach your goal. It isn't for use during pregnancy or nursing.

## How do you use it?

Take one tablet three times daily, spread as evenly as you can through your waking hours — taking each about 30 minutes before a meal is an easy way to space them out and get some appetite support along the way. Use it while you're actively working toward your goal, then discontinue once you've reached it or your habits hold the result on their own. If you have a liver condition, take diabetes medication or anticoagulants, or are pregnant or nursing, check with your doctor before using it.

## What makes it different?

Most fat-loss products are stimulant-driven and work on a single mechanism. This one is completely non-stimulant and works on several fronts at once — supporting the liver's fat-processing and antioxidant defenses while supporting fat metabolism and appetite control. It's positioned honestly: a tool to ease and speed the journey alongside diet and exercise, used until you reach your goal — not a permanent crutch or a magic pill. Ingredient doses are kept within researched, safe ranges (its green tea extract, for instance, sits well under established safety limits).
