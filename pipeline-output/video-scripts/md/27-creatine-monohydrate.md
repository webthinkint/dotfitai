## What is it?

Creatine Monohydrate is one of the most researched and proven supplements in sports nutrition. It's a single-ingredient powder that helps your muscles produce energy faster, train harder, and recover stronger. Available in unflavored and Raspberry Lemonade — both NSF Certified for Sport for athletes who need to know exactly what they're putting in their body.

## What does it do?

Creatine fuels the energy system your body relies on for short, explosive efforts — sprinting, lifting, jumping, anything that demands quick power. By saturating your muscles with creatine, it helps you push harder during training, recover faster between sets, and build more lean muscle over time. Newer research also shows benefits beyond performance — including support for brain function, healthy aging, and bone and muscle preservation as you get older.

## Who is it for?

Creatine is for adults both male and female who train hard and want results. Strength and power athletes — lifters, sprinters, jumpers — see the biggest performance gains. Team sport athletes whose play depends on repeated bursts of speed and power benefit too. Older adults working to preserve muscle, strength, and independence as they age. And anyone serious about training adaptations who wants a supplement backed by hundreds of clinical studies. And by the way, since creatine directly feeds the brain’s immediate energy system, when brain energy levels are stressed/compromised, creatine supplementation has been shown to improve overall brain health and functioning, including positively managing mood.

## How do you use it?

For fastest results, start with a five-day loading phase — one scoop, four times per day, taken with carbohydrate and/or protein-containing meals or shakes. After that, drop to one scoop, twice per day to maintain muscle saturation. On training days, take one dose before your workout and one after. Mix the unflavored into water, juice, or your favorite shake — or grab the Raspberry Lemonade for a refreshing standalone drink. Now, if you weigh less than 175 lbs, you can cut that maintenance dose in half – in other words, 1 scoop per day.
