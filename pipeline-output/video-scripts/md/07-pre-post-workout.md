## Overview

Pre & Post Workout Formula is dotFIT's most versatile protein powder — one formula that does the job of several. With 21 grams of fast- and extended-release whey protein, fast-acting carbs, and only 3 grams of sugar in about 250 calories, its balanced carb-to-protein ratio lets it work as a pre- or post-workout shake, a meal replacement, a weight loss or weight gain supplement depending on how you incorporate it into daily caloric planning, or a simple daily protein boost. Pick it for the job you need — and the taste you'll actually keep reaching for. Pre & Post Workout Formula — one powder, whatever the goal.

## What is it?

Pre & Post Workout Formula is a whey-based protein and carbohydrate powder built for versatility. Each serving delivers 21 grams of a fast- and extended-release protein blend (mostly whey concentrate, with whey isolate and a little casein), 33–35 grams of fast-digesting carbohydrate, and just 3 grams of sugar, in about 240–250 calories. That roughly 1.7-to-1 carb-to-protein ratio is the sweet spot that lets a single powder serve several goals. It mixes easily, tastes great, and is third-party tested.

## What does it do?

Around a workout, your muscles are primed to use protein and carbs — to rebuild tissue and refill energy stores. Pre & Post Workout Formula delivers both in a fast-absorbing form to make the most of that window: the whey blend supplies the amino acids that drive muscle repair and growth, while the quick carbs replenish glycogen and help shuttle those amino acids into muscle. Away from the gym, the same balanced formula works as a convenient meal replacement, a protein top-up to hit your daily target, or extra quality calories when you're trying to gain.

## Who is it for?

It's for anyone who'd rather have one flexible powder than a shelf full of single-purpose ones. Exercisers use it before and after training to support performance and recovery; people managing their weight use it as a portion-controlled meal replacement; those building mass use it for extra quality calories; and anyone short on protein uses it to top up. You can even adjust it — add fruit, oats, or more protein — to fit a specific goal. Because it is a dairy protein, anyone with unusual dairy allergies would choose the dotFIT Plant Protein.

## How do you use it?

Around training, mix it with water 30–40 minutes before your workout, scaling the scoops to your body size, and again right after. As a meal replacement for weight management, use it in place of a meal or two within a calorie-controlled plan. To gain, add servings through the day to meet your calorie and protein targets. It blends smoothly into water or milk, and you can build it up with other foods to dial in exactly the nutrition you want.

## What makes it different?

One formula, four jobs — that's the point. Its balanced carb-to-protein ratio lets it flex from pre-workout fuel to meal replacement to weight-gain shake, where most powders are locked into a single role. It uses a fast- and extended-release whey blend for both immediate and lasting amino acid delivery, keeps sugar to just 3 grams, skips aspartame, and tends not to cause the gas or bloating many protein powders do. And like every dotFIT powder, it's formulated — not spiked — to work safely alongside the rest of your regimen. Third-party tested.
