## How do you use it?

Mix two to three scoops daily with 8 ounces of water. On training days, take one scoop before your workout and one scoop after — you can mix it with your pre and post-workout shake or a meal containing protein or carbohydrate. On non-training days, take one scoop with your morning meal and one with your evening meal. If you're over 150 pounds and chasing maximum performance, add a third scoop with another meal or shake during the day. Spread your doses out — morning, afternoon, evening — to keep your muscles saturated.
