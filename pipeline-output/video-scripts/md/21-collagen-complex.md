Collagen Complex is your 2-in-1 solution for mobility and beauty—from stronger, more comfortable joints to smoother, younger-looking skin. CollagenComplex is a dual-action formula that supports both joint and skin health from the inside out. It's built around BioCell Collagen — a clinically researched matrix of hydrolyzed type II collagen peptides, hyaluronic acid, and chondroitin sulfate — the three building blocks that make up healthy cartilage and youthful skin. One formula. Two visible benefits.

CollagenComplex helps replenish what your body naturally loses with age. For joints, it supports cartilage health, mobility, and comfort — especially helpful for active people whose connective tissues take a beating from training. For skin, it supports collagen content, elasticity, and hydration, helping reduce the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles. It also helps protect connective tissue against the daily wear of aging and environmental stress.

CollagenComplex is built for adults who want to stay mobile, recover faster, and look as good as they feel. Collagen production starts declining in your 30s — about 1 to 1.5% per year — and accelerates from there. Whether you're an athlete protecting your joints, an adult noticing the first signs of aging skin, or someone simply being proactive about long-term health, this formula works on both fronts at once.

Simple. For combined joint and skin support, take one capsule in the morning and one at night, starting around age 35. For skin support alone, one capsule daily is enough. Athletes can take it at any age — two capsules in the morning and two at night during intense training cycles or when recovering from injury. Take it consistently, with or without food.
