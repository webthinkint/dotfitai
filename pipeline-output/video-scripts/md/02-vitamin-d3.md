## What does it do?

Vitamin D plays a role in nearly every system in your body. It supports bone strength, muscle function, immune health, mood, and athletic performance. The science is clear that maintaining healthy vitamin D levels — above 30 nanograms per milliliter — is associated with better long-term health outcomes. For athletes, levels above 40 are associated with better performance. The challenge is that most people can't reach those updated levels through diet, sunlight or even through a multivitamin and mineral formula alone. dotFIT Vitamin D3 helps close that gap consistently and safely.

## Who is it for?

Vitamin D3 is for almost everyone. Vitamin D insufficiency and deficiency are widespread due to limited sun exposure, indoor lifestyles, darker skin pigmentation, aging, and modern diets low in fatty fish or fortified foods. Moreover, older adults' bodies make less vitamin D from sunlight. In people with darker skin, melanin blocks the UVB rays that produce vitamin D. In people with higher body fat, vitamin D gets stored in fat tissue and becomes less available. Anyone who works indoors, lives in northern climates, or wears sunscreen regularly is a candidate for vitamin D supplementation. And almost all athletes who want to maximize performance and recovery.
