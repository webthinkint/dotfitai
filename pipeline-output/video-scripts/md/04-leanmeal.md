“LeanMeal is one of the simplest, most effective tools we use to help clients lose body fat and keep it off. ## What is it?

LeanMeal is dotFIT's high-protein, low-calorie shake mix — built to support weight loss, body fat reduction, and long-term weight management. In just 170 calories, it delivers 21 grams of premium whey protein isolate, 7 grams of fiber, low-glycemic carbohydrates, and a healthy fat blend. It's a complete, balanced meal in a scoop — designed to fit into your day, not replace what you actually want to eat.

## What does it do?

LeanMeal works on every level of healthy weight management. The whey protein isolate protects lean muscle while you lose fat, and supports a higher metabolic burn. The fiber blend — including Fibersol and glucomannan — slows digestion, supports gut health, and adds to lasting fullness. Low-glycemic sustained release carbohydrates deliver steady, even energy without the crash. And it all comes in fewer calories than almost any traditional meal you'd put together. The result is a shake that helps you control calories without feeling hungry, preserves the muscle you've worked to build, and keeps your energy steady throughout the day.

## Who is it for?

LeanMeal is for anyone working toward a healthier body composition. People trying to lose weight or body fat without losing muscle. Busy professionals who skip meals or grab whatever's convenient and want a nutritious option that fits in seconds. Athletes and active adults during cutting phases who need to control calories while protecting performance. People who've reached their goal weight and want a sustainable maintenance strategy. And anyone tired of fad diets and looking for an evidence-based tool that actually works long-term.

## How do you use it?

Simple. Mix one scoop with 8 to 10 ounces of water, milk, or your favorite beverage — or blend it into a smoothie with fruit, vegetables, or extra protein for a full meal. Use it once or twice a day to replace or supplement meals, depending on your calorie goals. It works alongside your traditional meals, not instead of them. For most people, two LeanMeal shakes plus two or three traditional meals each day is a proven strategy for both weight loss and long-term maintenance.
