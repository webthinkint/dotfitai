## Overview

LeanPak90 is dotFIT's complete 90-day weight-loss kit: three fat-loss supplements that each work a different way, plus a personal Weight Loss Planner to guide the whole thing. CarbRepel blocks part of the starch you eat from being absorbed, ThermAccel raises the calories you burn, and WeightLoss & LiverSupport supports healthy fat utilization and appetite control. Together they help you take in less, absorb less, and burn more — all built around a sensible diet and exercise plan, not instead of one. LeanPak90 — three ways to lose, one simple plan.

## What is it?

LeanPak90 is a complete weight-loss kit with a 90-day supply of three dotFIT fat-loss products, each working through a different mechanism: WeightLoss & LiverSupport, a stimulant-free aid that supports healthy fat utilization, liver function, and appetite; CarbRepel, a stimulant-free fiber blend that reduces how much dietary starch you absorb; and ThermAccel, a thermogenic that helps increase the calories you burn. It also includes a Personal Weight Loss Planner that ties the supplements to the eating and activity habits that actually drive results.

## What does it do?

Weight loss comes down to a few levers — eat less, absorb less, burn more — and LeanPak90 covers all of them at once. All three products support appetite control, so you take in fewer calories. CarbRepel's Phase 2® ingredient blocks a portion of the starch in your meals from being absorbed. ThermAccel's thermogenic complex helps raise your daily calorie burn. And WeightLoss & LiverSupport keeps the metabolic engine — your liver — supported so your body uses dietary fat and carbs efficiently. The planner turns all of that into a day-by-day routine.

## Who is it for?

It's for adults starting or restarting a serious weight-loss effort who'd rather follow one structured system than piece products together themselves. It suits people who struggle on more than one front — appetite, carb cravings, and a sluggish calorie burn. One thing to note: ThermAccel contains caffeine, so LeanPak90 isn't the right fit if you need everything stimulant-free, and it isn't for anyone under 18 or those who are pregnant, nursing, or contemplating pregnancy.

## How do you use it?

Follow the Weight Loss Planner, which lays out the routine. In short: take WeightLoss & LiverSupport as one tablet three times daily before meals; CarbRepel as two tablets twice daily before your two largest carb-containing meals; and ThermAccel as two tablets before a morning meal and two before an afternoon meal — but never within several hours of bedtime, since it's stimulant-based, and start with a lower dose if you're sensitive to caffeine. Use all three alongside sensible eating and regular exercise, and discontinue the aids once you've reached your goal.

## What makes it different?

LeanPak90 isn't a single magic pill — it's a complete system that attacks fat loss from three angles (consume less, absorb less, burn more) and hands you a planner to follow, so you're not guessing. It's honest about the role supplements play: tools to ease and speed a diet-and-exercise journey, used until you reach your goal, not forever. Two of the three products are completely stimulant-free, and the one thermogenic is clearly labeled so you stay in control of your caffeine. It’s HSA/FSA eligible and third-party tested. The LeanPak90 system helps you burn more, crave less and get leaner, faster—without giving up your energy or muscle.
