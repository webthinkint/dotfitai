## How do you use it?

Dosing scales to body weight. Mix it with water about 30 minutes before training. Under 150 pounds, take one and a half scoops. 150 to 200 pounds, two scoops. Over 200 pounds, two and a half scoops. Don't exceed three scoops. Avoid other caffeine sources within four hours. Use it on training days only.

**Training Days**

Take as listed by body weight:

- Under 150 lbs – 1.5 scoops ~30 minutes before activity.
- 150-200 lbs – 2 scoops ~30 minutes before activity.
- Over 200 lbs – 2.5 scoops ~30 minutes before activity.
- Do not exceed three (3) scoops (primarily due to caffeine content).

\* If tingling occurs and user is uncomfortable, split total dosage into ½ scoop increments ingesting each ½ scoop no less than ~1.5 hours apart so that the total dosage is consumed before and during the workout, as necessary.

- Example: training at total dosage = 2 scoops. Divide dosage in ½ scoops and take at least 1.5 hours apart with final dose started before workout and may continue to drink during the workout.

**Non-Training Days**

- Take same total dose by weight as described above at any time during the day and at least 5 hours before sleep. May take all at once or split such as, half dose in the morning and remaining half in the afternoon, or split in ½ scoop doses, as described above.

Maintain this protocol for at least 28 days to fully saturate muscle stores.
