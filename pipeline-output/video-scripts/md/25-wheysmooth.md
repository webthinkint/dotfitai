## Overview

WheySmooth™ is more than just another protein powder — it’s a premium, low-calorie, controlled-release protein system designed to support lifelong strength, recovery, and body composition goals. Using a specialized blend of whey concentrate, whey isolate, calcium caseinate, and micellar casein, it delivers both fast and sustained amino acid release to directly support muscle repair, recovery, appetite control, and lean muscle preservation. With 25 grams of complete protein, 3 grams of leucine, naturally low lactose, added calcium and potassium, plus NSF Certified for Sport third-party testing, WheySmooth gives you pharmaceutical-level quality protein that fits almost any lifestyle, age, or fitness goal — all in a delicious, convenient shake.

“WheySmooth™ — Strength, Recovery & Results in Every Scoop.”

## What is it?

WheySmooth is a premium whey protein containing a 4-stage specialized controlled release protein blend of predominately whey isolate and concentrate for fast amino acid release combined with calcium caseinate and micellar casein for sustained release, delivering nature's highest quality protein in a clean, low-calorie powder. With 25 grams of complete protein per scoop and only 160 calories, it's the simple way to support muscle, recovery, and overall health — at any stage of life. Available in chocolate and vanilla.

## What does it do?

WheySmooth fuels what your body builds and rebuilds. Through its controlled-release proteins, it delivers immediate and sustained muscle protein synthesis to help preserve lean muscle during weight loss, speeds recovery after training, and provides the natural health, immune and growth factor bio-actives found only in true whey concentrate. Whether you're chasing performance, managing your weight, or staying strong as you age, this is the protein that does more per scoop.

## Who is it for?

WheySmooth is for almost everyone. Active adults and athletes who want to maximize training results. Anyone in a calorie deficit who wants to protect lean muscle while losing fat. Aging adults working to maintain strength and independence. Even busy people who just need a quick, high-quality protein source. If you're not consuming enough complete protein from food to support your fitness goals, WheySmooth fills the gap.

## How do you use it?

Simple. Mix one scoop with water, milk, or your favorite ingredients — fruit, oats, nut butter, whatever fits your goals. Use it before or after workouts to support recovery, between meals to hit your daily protein target, or before bed to support overnight muscle repair. Adjust the serving size to match your needs — one and a half scoops gives you 40 grams of protein in just 240 calories.

## What makes it different?

Ours isn't a generic, store-bought protein powder. WheySmooth uses true whey protein concentrate, not just isolate — so you get the natural growth factors, immunoglobulins, and bio-actives that support overall health, not just muscle. It's high in leucine — three grams per scoop — the key amino acid that triggers muscle protein synthesis. Naturally low in lactose, easy to digest, and easy to mix. NSF Certified for Sport, third-party tested for purity, potency, and banned substances. And manufactured to pharmaceutical standards exclusively for dotFIT. That's the level of quality most protein powders simply can't match.
