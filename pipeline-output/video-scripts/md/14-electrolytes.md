## Overview

When you sweat, you lose more than water — you lose the electrolytes that keep your muscles contracting, your nerves firing, and your body holding onto fluid. dotFIT Electrolytes replaces them: sodium, potassium, magnesium, calcium, and chloride, plus vitamin C and B12. For long or intense sessions, hot and humid conditions, or heavy sweating, it helps you stay hydrated, hold off fatigue, and recover better than water alone — without loading you up with sugar or megadoses. Electrolytes — replace what you sweat out, perform like you didn't.

## What is it?

dotFIT Electrolytes is a mix-with-water supplement that replaces the minerals your body loses in sweat and uses during exercise. It delivers the full set of key electrolytes — sodium and chloride, which drive fluid balance; potassium, magnesium, and calcium, which support muscle and nerve function — rather than just sodium. It also includes vitamin C, an antioxidant that helps the immune system handle heavy training loads, and vitamin B12, which supports energy metabolism and recovery. It's designed to be used before, during, or after activity, and it's third-party tested.

## What does it do?

Lose more than about 2% of your body weight in sweat and your performance drops — your heart works harder, your core temperature climbs, and endurance falls off. Electrolytes helps you stay ahead of that. By replacing fluid and the minerals lost in sweat, it helps maintain hydration and fluid balance, supports normal muscle contraction and nerve signaling, and reduces exercise-related fatigue compared to water alone. It also helps restore fluid balance afterward for better recovery, and a magnesium-rich electrolyte mix has been shown to cut down on exercise-associated muscle cramps for some people.

## Who is it for?

It's for athletes and active people doing prolonged or high-intensity exercise — especially in heat or humidity, where sweat losses climb fast. Endurance athletes, anyone training longer than an hour or two, regular sauna users, and "salty sweaters" benefit most, as do weight-class athletes managing dehydration carefully. It's worth being honest here: for easy sessions under an hour in mild conditions, plain water is usually enough. Electrolytes earns its place when you're sweating hard, going long, or training in the heat. However, they are also beneficial for people that follow keto or other low-carbohydrate diets, fast regularly, travel frequently, or struggle with hydration, such as aging.

## How do you use it?

Mix it with water and match it to your session. For exercise lasting over an hour or in hot, humid conditions, sip it during your workout to keep fluid and sodium up. Afterward, drink roughly 16–24 ounces of fluid with electrolytes for every pound you lost during training. For ultra-endurance efforts, aim for steady sodium intake across the event and alternate electrolyte fluid with plain water. If you take prescription medications, check with your physician before regular use.

## What makes it different?

A lot of "electrolyte" products are mostly sodium and sugar. dotFIT Electrolytes delivers the full balance your body actually loses — sodium, potassium, magnesium, calcium, and chloride — and is notably magnesium-rich, the mineral most linked to cramp relief in research. Potassium is kept to a sensible amount for safety, and the added vitamin C and B12 support immune resilience and recovery under heavy training. There are no megadoses, and it's formulated to work alongside the rest of your dotFIT regimen so your total intake stays in a safe range. Third-party tested.
