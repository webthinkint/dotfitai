## Overview

DigestiveEnzymes is a five-enzyme complex that supports your body's own digestion — helping break down the protein, carbs, fats, dairy sugar, and fiber in your meals. Natural enzyme production can dip with age, diet changes, and lifestyle, and when it does, food doesn't break down as cleanly — which can mean bloating, gas, or the sense you're not getting everything out of what you eat. Taken with meals, it tops up what your body already makes, to support smoother digestion and better nutrient absorption. DigestiveEnzymes — help your body get more from every meal.

## What is it?

DigestiveEnzymes is a capsule that delivers DigeZyme®, a five-enzyme blend from Sabinsa: neutral protease for protein, α-amylase for carbohydrates, lipase for fats, lactase for the sugar in dairy, and cellulase for fiber. The enzymes are microbe-derived through fermentation, which makes them vegan-friendly, gluten-free, and unusually stable — they keep working across the wide range of acidity in your digestive tract. They're made to Food Chemicals Codex standards and third-party tested. You take one with each main meal.

## What does it do?

Your body makes enzymes to break food down into the nutrients it can actually absorb — amino acids, vitamins, minerals, glucose, fats. But natural production can fall short with age, stress, medications, or foods your system isn't used to, and under-digested food can leave you with minor bloating, gas, or cramping. DigestiveEnzymes adds to your own supply across all the major food groups — including the dairy sugar lactose and the fiber your body can't break down on its own — to support more comfortable digestion and help you get more out of what you eat. There's also some evidence the protease may help ease exercise-induced muscle soreness.

## Who is it for?

It's for anyone dealing with occasional, minor digestive discomfort — bloating, gas, or cramping after meals — or who finds certain foods, like dairy, harder to digest than they used to. Because natural enzyme output tends to decline with age, it can be especially useful for older adults and for anyone whose diet or routine has recently changed. Exercisers may notice a recovery bonus from the protease. It isn't for use during pregnancy or nursing, and if you take blood thinners or certain antibiotics, check with your doctor first.

## How do you use it?

Take three capsules a day — one with each of your three main meals — so the enzymes are present when food arrives and digestion is underway. They're built to stay active throughout your digestive tract, so taking them with food is the thing that matters. One honest note: if you're using it purely for digestive comfort and don't notice a difference after about 30 days of consistent use, there may be no reason to keep taking it for that particular goal.

## What makes it different?

Many enzyme products give you the basic three — protease, amylase, and lipase. DigestiveEnzymes adds two more: lactase, for the sugar in dairy, and cellulase, for the fiber your body can't break down on its own — so it covers more of what you actually eat. The enzymes are microbe-derived, which means they work at lower doses and stay active across the full pH range of your gut, where many animal-based enzymes can't. They're vegan-friendly, gluten-free, made to Food Chemicals Codex standards, third-party tested, and formulated to fit alongside the rest of your dotFIT regimen.
