## What does it do?

Calcium is the mineral your skeleton is mostly built from, and your body will pull it out of your bones to keep blood levels steady when your diet runs short. Supplying enough each day helps protect that reserve. But calcium doesn't act alone: vitamin D3 helps you absorb it, magnesium supports its regulation, and vitamin K2 acts as a "chaperone" that helps direct calcium into bone rather than soft tissue. Together, these nutrients support bone strength and the maintenance of bone density across your lifespan.

## Who is it for?

It's for anyone whose diet falls short of the daily calcium target — which is most people, especially those who've cut back on dairy or aren't consuming calcium-fortified foods. Bone mass builds until about age 30 and slowly declines after, so it matters across every life stage: younger adults building toward peak bone mass, and older adults supporting what they've got. Because it's NSF Certified for Sport, drug-tested athletes can use it with confidence. If your diet already meets the calcium target of ~1200mg/day and you take a complete multivitamin, you shouldn’t need it.

## How do you use it?

Take it with a meal — calcium absorbs best alongside food. Women generally take one or two tablets daily; if taking two, split them between a morning and evening meal. Men typically need only one tablet a day when their diet falls short. Pair it with a dotFIT multivitamin — the formula is built to complement, not duplicate, the bone nutrients your multivitamin already provides, keeping your total intake in a safe range. Space it apart from a separate iron supplement, and check with a healthcare professional if you take prescription medications.
