## Overview

FirstString™ is a complete performance nutrition solution that removes the guesswork from fueling and recovery. By delivering the right nutrients at the right time in the right ratio, it helps athletes train harder, recover faster, and build more muscle—while also supporting total daily nutrition. It’s not just a protein shake—it’s a precision-designed performance system in a shaker cup. “Fuel the Work. Build the Result.”

## What is it?

FirstString is a complete pre and post-workout fuel built for serious athletes. With 21 grams of fast-digesting whey protein, 45 grams of clean carbohydrates, and only 3 grams of sugar per two-scoop serving, it delivers the exact ratio of fuel and recovery your body needs around training. It's NCAA approved and NSF Certified for Sport — so drug-tested athletes can use it with confidence.

## What does it do?

FirstString fuels training and accelerates recovery. The fast-acting carbohydrates top off your glycogen stores so you can train harder and longer. The high-quality whey protein — rich in leucine and other essential amino acids — triggers muscle protein synthesis right when your body is most receptive to it. Together, they hit the so-called metabolic window — the 90-minute period after exercise when your muscles are primed to absorb nutrients and rebuild. Used pre and post-workout, FirstString helps you make every session count.

## How do you use it?

Dosing scales to body size. Take 30 to 40 minutes before activity, then repeat the same dose immediately after. Athletes 100 to 150 pounds use one and a half scoops. 151 to 220 pounds, two scoops. 221 to 300 pounds, three scoops. It also works as a meal replacement, weight gain supplement, or smoothie base — blend it with fruits, milk, or vegetables to round out a young athlete's daily nutrition.
