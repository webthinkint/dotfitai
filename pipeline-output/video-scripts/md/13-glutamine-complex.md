## Overview

GlutamineComplex supplies free-form L-glutamine — the most abundant amino acid in your body — with stabilizing cofactors, for the times when hard training, strict dieting, or illness drain your natural supply faster than you can replace it. When glutamine runs low, your muscle, gut, and immune system all compete for what's left. Replenishing it helps defend muscle from stress-induced breakdown, supports gut integrity and immune function, and aids recovery between demanding sessions. 3rd party tested and NSF Certified for Sport. GlutamineComplex — recovery, immune, and muscle support for when you're pushing hardest.

## What is it?

GlutamineComplex is an easy-to-mix powder that delivers free-form L-glutamine along with chromium and magnesium chelate cofactors. Glutamine is the most abundant amino acid in your body and is considered "conditionally essential" — meaning you normally make enough, but under heavy stress like intense training, calorie restriction, or illness, demand outpaces what your body can produce. That's when an outside source helps. It mixes cleanly into water, a sports drink, or a shake, and it's 3rd party tested and NSF Certified for Sport.

## What does it do?

Under sustained physical stress, your glutamine stores get depleted. In fact, body and muscle glutamine levels fall by up to 50% during stress — and because your immune cells, gut lining, and muscle all rely on it, something has to give. Supplementing replenishes that pool, so those systems aren't forced to compete or break down. It helps protect skeletal muscle from having to release its amino acids and reduces the breakdown that hard training and dieting drive. Additionally, supplementing supports the integrity of the gut (where much of the immune system lives), and fuels the immune cells that get taxed by heavy training or illness — one reason athletes use it to cut down on the sniffles that come with hard training blocks. The net effect is better and faster recovery.

## Who is it for?

It's for athletes and exercisers under prolonged, demanding training stress — especially when that's combined with dieting, like fighters and physique competitors making weight or getting very lean, and ultra-endurance athletes. It's also for anyone trying to support their immune system through a heavy training block, protect gut health, and recover better between sessions, or to blunt the effects of overtraining. If you have a kidney or liver condition, are pregnant or nursing, or take prescription medications, check with a healthcare professional before using it. It’s good for anyone during flu season to help mount a proper immune response.

## How do you use it?

It's a flexible powder — add it to water, a sports drink, or a protein shake. For general immune, gut, and recovery support, take 5 grams (one scoop) three times a day: one dose before exercise, one after, and one before bed, for 15 grams total. Highly active or heavily stressed athletes can scale up to about 0.1 gram per pound of body weight, split across the day with half roughly 40 minutes pre-workout. It's best cycled to your most intense training periods, but can be used to support overall health year-round.
