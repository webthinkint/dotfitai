## Overview

CarbRepel is a non-stimulant carb-control aid for people whose weight struggle is really a carb struggle. Its clinically studied active — Phase 2® white kidney bean extract — inhibits the enzyme that digests starch, so a portion of the starch calories you eat pass through rather than being absorbed. Taken before carb-containing meals, it can ease the calorie math of dieting while you keep up sensible eating and regular exercise. No stimulants, no jitters, no effect on your nervous system. CarbRepel — let the carbs you love work a little less against you.

## What is it?

CarbRepel is a non-stimulant weight-management supplement built around Phase 2®, a clinically studied extract of the white kidney bean (Phaseolus vulgaris). Phase 2® is an alpha-amylase inhibitor — it interferes with the enzyme that breaks starch down for absorption. It's rounded out with citrus pectin, a fruit fiber, and pomegranate leaf extract. You take it shortly before your largest starch-containing meals. It's third-party tested and made to GMP standards in an NSF-certified facility.

## What does it do?

When you eat starchy carbohydrates, an enzyme called alpha-amylase breaks them down so your body can absorb them. Phase 2® partially blocks that enzyme, so some of that starch passes through undigested instead of being absorbed as calories. The FDA has accepted two claims for the ingredient: that it may reduce the enzymatic digestion of dietary starches, and that it may assist in weight control when used alongside a sensible diet and exercise program. The Citrus Pectin (CP) is a natural fiber found in many fruits and used in this product to help in the delivery of the active ingredient Phaseolus vulgaris (Phase 2).

## Who is it for?

It's for people actively working toward fat loss who tend to crave or overeat carbohydrates and want help blunting the impact of starchy meals. But also for anyone consuming carbohydrates as part of their diet to allow the carb intake but reduce the calories. Because it's non-stimulant, it suits anyone who wants a fat-loss aid without caffeine or any effect on the central nervous system. It can also be used occasionally — on a day when carb intake runs high — to help keep weight steady, or as part of a structured plan like LeanPak90. It isn't for use during pregnancy or nursing.

## How do you use it?

Take two tablets, twice daily, about 30 minutes before your two largest carbohydrate-containing meals, with at least 8 ounces of fluid. Timing matters: it works on the starch in the meal you're about to eat, so take it before, not after. Use it while you're actively pursuing your goal, then taper off once your eating habits and lifestyle hold the result on their own. If you take diabetes medication, monitor your blood sugar closely and check with your doctor, since the ingredient can affect glucose response.

## What makes it different?

CarbRepel is built on Phase 2®, one of the most-studied carbohydrate-control ingredients available, with FDA-accepted structure/function claims behind it. Just as important is what it isn't: it's completely non-stimulant, so there's no caffeine, no jitters, and no effect on your nervous system — a real contrast to the many fat-loss products that simply load up on stimulants. It's positioned honestly as a tool that works with a sensible diet and exercise, not a substitute for them. Third-party tested and made to GMP standards in an NSF-certified facility.
