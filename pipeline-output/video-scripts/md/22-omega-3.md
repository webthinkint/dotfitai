## What is it?

Omega3 is a high-quality fish oil supplement that delivers the two essential fatty acids your body needs but can't make enough on its own — EPA and DHA. Sourced from sustainably wild-caught Alaska Pollock and concentrated in a clinically meaningful dose, it's the simple, mercury-free way to fill a gap that almost everyone has.

## What does it do?

EPA and DHA are foundational. They support heart health, brain function, vision, and healthy inflammation response — the systems that quietly run everything else. They're built into the structure of your cells, especially in the brain and eyes, and they help your body manage triglycerides, support circulation, and recover from daily wear and exercise. Most importantly, they manage inflammation so it can work for you, not age you.

## Who is it for?

Omega3 is for almost everyone. Most Americans fall well below the recommended intake of EPA and DHA — even people who eat fish a few times a week. If you're not eating two to four servings of fatty fish weekly, you're likely under-supplied. That includes active adults, athletes, pregnant or nursing women, kids, and aging adults. If you have a fish allergy or follow a vegan diet, talk to your provider about plant-based alternatives.

## How do you use it?

Simple. Take one to two soft gels with any meal. One per day if you eat fatty fish occasionally. Two per day if fish isn't part of your regular diet. Take it consistently — these are nutrients your body uses every single day.
