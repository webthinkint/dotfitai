## What does it do?

Women'sMV fills the everyday nutrition gaps that food alone often can't cover. It supports energy, immune function, bone strength, skin, hair and nails, brain health, and overall vitality. Iron is included at the higher levels women need to support healthy blood and energy, especially during menstruation. Folate and choline support reproductive health and are critical during early pregnancy — often before pregnancy is even known. Both forms of vitamin K and both forms of B12 — including methylcobalamin — support bone, heart, and brain health. Vitamin D is dosed higher than most multivitamins, in line with the levels research now associates with better long-term health. And the formula is built to work synergistically with the rest of the dotFIT lineup, so adding products like Calcium Complex or Omega3 keeps everything in the safe and effective range.

Women'sMV is for women ages 18 to 50 who want a daily nutritional foundation. Women whose diets don't always include the variety of food groups needed to meet recommended levels. Simple. Take one tablet daily with your favorite beverage immediately after your first food meal of the day. Taking it with food helps absorption and is gentler on the stomach. Pair it with a complete diet, and add other dotFIT products like Calcium Complex, Omega3, or Antioxidants if your specific goals call for it.
