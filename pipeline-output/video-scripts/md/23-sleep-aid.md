Sleep Aid helps you relax before bed, fall asleep faster, and stay asleep longer. The dual-release melatonin works with your body's natural circadian rhythm — releasing some right away to help you drift off, then more through the night to keep you there. 5-HTP supports calming serotonin levels, while lemon balm, hops, and magnesium add natural relaxation support. Together, they help reduce wakefulness, support REM sleep, and ease the effects of jet lag, shift work, or just a stressful day.

Sleep Aid is built for adults who have trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking up feeling rested.
