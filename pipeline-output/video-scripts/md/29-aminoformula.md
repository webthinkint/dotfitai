AminoFormula™ is a clinically backed, fast-acting muscle protein synthesis (MPS) stimulator that goes beyond traditional protein supplements. Within extremely low calories, it contains 9 essential amino acids (EAAs)—including high-dose BCAAs—formulated to directly trigger muscle building signals at the molecular level (e.g., mTOR activation), helping you build and preserve lean mass while supporting metabolism, recovery, performance and desired body composition. It's NSF Certified for Sport — trusted by athletes who need to know what's in every scoop.

## What does it do?

AminoFormula goes straight to work where intact protein can't. Because the amino acids are isolated and free-form, they reach your muscles fast — creating the ideal environment for muscle protein synthesis. It triggers and supports muscle building, accelerates recovery, helps preserve lean muscle during calorie restriction, and pushes your training adaptations beyond what protein alone can deliver. All in just a handful of calories per serving.

AminoFormula is for athletes and active adults who want more from every workout. Strength and physique athletes chasing maximum muscle gains. Anyone in a calorie deficit — dieters, weight-class athletes, people on intermittent fasting or keto — who needs to protect lean muscle without adding calories. Aging adults working to slow age-related muscle loss and stay strong and independent. And anyone who wants a low-nitrogen, easy-on-the-stomach option that supports muscle without a heavy protein load. It is also commonly used by anyone to fortify low protein meals such as typical breakfast food and plant-based meals and diets. Additionally, non-exercisers use it regularly to stave off the natural loss of muscle due to aging and/or inactivity.

## How do you use it?

On training days if ≤150 lbs: ½ scoop before + ½ scoop after workout; 150–200 lbs: ¾ scoop before + ¾ scoop after; >200 lbs: 1 scoop before + 1 scoop after. Or simply mix both doses in one drink and start ingesting 10 min before the activity and consume at a pace so it’s finished at end of activity. Mix it with water, juice, your pre-workout drink, or stir it into your favorite shake.
