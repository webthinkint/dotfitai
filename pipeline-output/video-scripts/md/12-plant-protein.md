## Overview

PlantProtein is a vegan protein built to perform like whey. Its pea-isolate base is fortified with BCAAs, glutamine, and seed proteins — chia, cranberry, and sacha inchi — to closely match the amino acid profile that drives muscle building, all at just 130 calories for 21 grams of protein. A built-in digestive enzyme blend means none of the gas or bloating plant proteins are known for. It's gluten-free, soy-free, dairy-free, non-GMO, and sweetened naturally with stevia. ## What is it?

PlantProtein is a plant-based protein powder built around pea protein isolate and fortified with BCAAs, glutamine, and small amounts of chia, cranberry, and sacha inchi seed proteins. Together they closely mimic the amino acid profile of whey — the benchmark for protein quality — in a fully vegan formula. Each serving delivers 21 grams of protein in just 130 calories, with under a gram of sugar. It's hypoallergenic, gluten-free, dairy-free, soy-free, and non-GMO, with a digestive enzyme blend built in, and with no artificial sweeteners or flavors. Third-party tested.

## What does it do?

Protein is what your body uses to build and preserve lean muscle, and the amino acids that drive that process — especially the BCAAs and leucine — are exactly what most plant proteins are short on. PlantProtein is fortified to match whey's profile, so it supports muscle protein synthesis comparably to whey, which research has shown directly. It helps you hit your daily protein target on few calories, and its enzyme blend (bromelain and alpha-galactosidase) helps you digest plant protein comfortably — without the gas and bloating that are common with other plant powders.

## Who is it for?

It's for anyone who wants a plant-based protein — vegans and vegetarians, people with dairy allergies, and anyone avoiding animal protein for health, environmental, religious, or personal reasons. Because it's high in protein and low in calories, it's well suited to weight- and fat-conscious athletes and to anyone dieting who wants to protect muscle while cutting calories. It's also valuable for older adults, whose bodies become more resistant to building muscle with age, and for anyone simply struggling to hit their protein target from food alone.

## How do you use it?

Mix one scoop with water or your favorite liquid and stir or blend. Use it before or after workouts, or anytime you need to top up your daily protein. Because it's low in calories, it fits cleanly into a weight-control plan — and since it's a powder, you can build it into a fuller meal by adding fruit, greens, or other ingredients to hit your overall nutrition targets for the day.

## What makes it different?

Most plant proteins, compared to animal proteins (especially whey protein), fall short on the amino acids that actually drive muscle building. PlantProtein is fortified to close that gap — matching whey's profile and carrying even more BCAAs per gram. It's pea-based with no soy, and the built-in enzyme blend means you skip the gas and bloating other plant proteins are notorious for. It's gluten-free, dairy-free, non-GMO, under a gram of sugar, and naturally flavored and sweetened — third-party tested and made to GMP standards in an NSF-certified facility exclusively for dotFIT.
