## Overview

ThermAccel is dotFIT's maximum-strength thermogenic — a multi-ingredient fat-loss aid that works on weight loss from several angles at once. It combines a thermogenic blend (caffeine, green tea EGCG, and capsaicin) to raise your calorie burn, Sinetrol citrus polyphenols to support fat oxidation, and Caralluma fimbriata to help curb appetite — with L-theanine to keep the energy smooth and crash-free. Taken alongside diet and exercise, it's built to help you push past plateaus without having to add more work. ThermAccel — break the plateau, skip the crash.

## What is it?

ThermAccel is a stimulant-based thermogenic supplement for weight and fat loss, built on several working ingredients. A thermogenic blend of caffeine, green tea extract (EGCG), and capsaicin from cayenne raises your metabolism and calorie burn; Sinetrol, a non-stimulant citrus polyphenol complex, supports fat oxidation through a different pathway; Caralluma fimbriata supports appetite control; and L-theanine smooths out the caffeine to soften jitters and the end-of-day crash. A two-stage release spreads the effect through the day.

## What does it do?

Losing fat gets harder as you go — your metabolism adapts and slows, so the same diet and exercise stop producing the same results (the dreaded plateau). ThermAccel works against that by raising the calories you burn — through thermogenesis, your body giving off more heat, and increased fat oxidation — and by helping curb appetite, so continuing to lose doesn't always mean eating less or training more. Additionally, not only does your body naturally burn more calories, ThermAccel also increases your energy levels, so you want to do more daily activities. Each ingredient targets a different part of the equation: burn more, oxidize more fat, feel less hungry. The L-theanine keeps the stimulant effect balanced rather than jittery.

## Who is it for?

It's for adults pursuing fat loss who want a serious, multi-pronged push — especially to break through a plateau — and who tolerate stimulants well. If you've maxed out the diet and exercise you can realistically sustain and need help over the hump, this is the most aggressive option in dotFIT's weight-loss lineup. Because it's stimulant-based, it isn't for anyone under 18, pregnant or nursing, sensitive to caffeine, or managing conditions like high blood pressure, heart issues, anxiety, or thyroid disease. Check with your doctor if you take any medications.

## How do you use it?

The maximum dose is four tablets a day — two with breakfast and two with lunch, each with at least 8 ounces of fluid. If you're sensitive to caffeine, start with one tablet and build up. Don't take it within several hours of bedtime or within four hours of any other stimulant, including coffee. Use it while you're actively working toward your goal, alongside sensible diet and exercise, then discontinue once you've reached it — it's meant to ease the journey, not to be permanent.

## What makes it different?

Most fat burners are just caffeine in a capsule. ThermAccel works on several fronts at once — thermogenesis, fat oxidation, and appetite — through ingredients that each act a different way, which is the whole point of the formula. Just as notable is the L-theanine, included to balance the stimulant so you get steadier energy without the jitters and end-of-day burnout common to other thermogenics. And it's positioned honestly: a tool to accelerate and ease a diet-and-exercise plan until you reach your goal. It's third-party tested and made to GMP standards in an NSF-certified facility.
