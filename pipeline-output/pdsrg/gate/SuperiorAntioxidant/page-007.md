Lutein and Zeaxanthin in Eye Health
L and Z as structural and functional components of the vison system, are predominately active in the macula of the
retina but also found in the lens of the eye along with their oxidized metabolites.134,153 There is a 2:1 lutein: zeaxanthin
ratio in the peripheral area of the retina, but zeaxanthin becomes the dominant pigment in the macular area.154 At the
center of the macula, total Z content is both zeaxanthin and meso-zeaxanthin in a 1:1 ratio.154 The macula is
responsible for central vision and acuity. Both L and Z serve as blue light and near-ultraviolet radiation filters,
protecting underlying ocular tissues from damage.134 L and Z absorb 40-90% of incident blue light, thus protecting the
retina from damage related to light.134,155,156 Like all carotenoids, L and Z function as powerful antioxidants and anti-
