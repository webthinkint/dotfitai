The Protein Digestibility Corrected
Amino Acid Score (PDCAAS) is a
composite score indicator of protein
quality used to determine the
ability of protein to meet the body’s AA
requirements.
It factors the protein’s EAA composition
and digestibility.
A given dietary protein cannot fully
meet the body’s EAA requirements
when its PDCAAS is less than 100%.
