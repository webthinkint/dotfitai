## 5. Food Alone is Not Enough (p. 6)

Even with a good diet, micronutrient shortfalls are inevitable. Relying on food alone is unrealistic and proven ineffective in Western societies. Protein is Priority #1 in Macro Planning At least 1g per lb of lean body mass/day, divided over 4–5 meals. Protein fuels muscle, bone, and recovery while helping control body composition. Omega-3s, Calcium, and extra Vitamin D are Critical Baseline Additions Omega-3s: 500–600mg/day EPA+DHA (or ~4 servings fatty fish/week). Calcium: ~1000–1200mg/day from diet + supplements as needed. Vitamin D: Ensure serum 25(OH)D >30ng/mL, ideally 40–70ng/mL in athletes. These nutrients are repeatedly deficient and essential for lifelong resilience.
