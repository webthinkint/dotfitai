# Member FAQs

## Account and password

**I forgot my password. How do I recover it?**

Click the person icon at the top right of the dotFIT website and select “Forgot Password.” Enter your email address and check your inbox for a reset link. Check spam or junk if it does not arrive.

**How do I change my password?**

Open the menu (☰), then go to My Account \> My Info. Update your password there.

**I never received my welcome email. What should I do?**

Check spam or junk. If it is missing, ask the Trainer or facility that created your account to verify your email address, or email support@dotfit.com.

## Ordering and shipping

**What is the best way to order?**

Build Your Program to see products recommended in Your Dietary Support on the Summary Overview, or shop directly in the Store by category. Add items to your cart and follow checkout prompts. You can select recurring delivery during checkout.

**When is standard shipping free?**

One-time orders of \$80 or more and recurring orders of \$20 or more qualify for free standard shipping, according to the supplied shipping policy. Review the checkout total and shipping terms before placing your order.

**How does the Free Shipping Plan work?**

The supplied plan offers free shipping on every order with no minimum in the continental United States for \$6.95 per month or \$69.95 per year. Review current terms before enrolling.

## Recurring orders and account profiles

**How do I change a recurring order?**

Log in and go to My Account \> Manage Recurring Orders. You can adjust quantities, shipping frequency or dates, and pause, reactivate, or cancel an order. Save changes before the order processes.

**How do I manage my Free Shipping Plan?**

Go to My Account \> Manage Subscriptions. Make and save changes before the subscription processes.

**How do I update my billing or shipping profile?**

Go to My Account \> My Info, scroll to your existing billing or shipping profiles, edit the appropriate profile, and save.

**Will account changes affect an order that has already processed?**

Changes should be made before processing. If an order has already processed, contact support@dotfit.com as soon as possible for help.

## Programs and progress

**How do I build my program?**

Start Build Your Program and answer the intake questions about your goals. Your Summary Overview then brings together your progress, nutrition, workouts, and dietary support.

**Can I change my goal?**

On the Summary Overview, select the blue \+ button, then New Program. Your earlier information is saved as you update your goal.

**Can I use dotFIT without a Trainer?**

Yes. You can build and use your own program. A Trainer can also help manage and customize it if you are working with one.

**How do I connect a fitness app or device?**

Use the device sync option in your program to connect supported services such as Fitbit or Apple Health. Device data generally syncs at midnight; use Device Sync on the Summary page to request an earlier sync.

**How do I log food?**

Use quick logging, favorites, custom foods or meals, reference menus, or a custom menu.

**How do I know which supplements are right for me?**

Build your program and review Dietary Support on your Summary page. The intake considers your stated goals and health information. Ask your healthcare professional about medical questions or potential interactions.

**How do I track workouts and measurements?**

Use the Workout Portal to find routines and the measurement tools in your program to record weight, body fat percentage, and circumference.

**Where can I find recipes, articles, and tutorials?**

Open Learn from the desktop navigation or mobile menu and use Search. Member Onboarding also offers program walkthroughs.

**Where can I get help?**

Email support@dotfit.com or visit the Member Onboarding page linked below.

# Trainer FAQs

**How do I add a Member?**

Sign in to your Trainer account, open the menu, select dotFIT Users, and choose \+ Add New Member. Enter their information and save. The Member should receive a welcome email; ask them to check spam or junk.

**Can I create my own program?**

Yes. Your Trainer account includes personal program tools as well as client management tools. Creating your own program is optional.

**Where do I manage a Member’s program?**

Open the Member from your Trainer dashboard. You can manage program elements and recommendations, customize menus and workouts, and use the available client tools.

**Can I help a Member choose supplements?**

Yes. Review their Dietary Support recommendations and use Trainer tools to manage recommendations and supplement tracking. Review the Member’s information first and refer medical questions to their healthcare professional.

**Can I place an order for a Member through the website?**

Create their Member profile and help identify products. For purchases through the dotFIT website, the Member logs in and places their own order.

**Where are certification, tutorials, and reports?**

Use Getting Started for certification and product resources, and Trainer Tutorials for program tasks, reports, and commission guidance. Trainer reporting covers your own Members.

# Administrator FAQs

**What is the difference between an Org Admin and a Club Admin?**

An Org Admin oversees accounts and reporting across the organization. A Club Admin manages Trainers and Members, wholesale ordering, and admin reports for their assigned club.

**How do I add a Trainer or Member?**

Open the menu and select dotFIT Users. Choose \+ Add New Trainer or \+ Add New Member, enter the account details, verify club and Trainer assignments when applicable, and save.

**How do I add another Club Admin?**

Open the menu, select Administration, and choose \+ Add New Location Admin. Enter their information, confirm the correct club, and save.

**Should an Admin also have a Trainer account?**

Yes, if they want personal program access or Trainer certification. Create a separate Trainer account. dotFIT’s account guidance says linked Admin and Trainer accounts use the same email address and password but different usernames.

**How do I switch between linked accounts?**

Open the menu and use the downward arrow to choose the Admin or Trainer account.

**Can I reassign a Member to another Trainer?**

Admin account management supports reassignment. Check the Member’s club and selected Trainer before saving.

**Who can view reports or place wholesale orders?**

Org Admins can access organization reporting and wholesale ordering. Club Admins can access reporting and wholesale ordering for their club. Trainers see reports that pertain to their clients.

**Who can help with an account or permission problem?**

Confirm that you are signed into the intended role and club, then email support@dotfit.com with the username, club, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords.

# Returns and Refunds

**Can I exchange a product purchased on dotFIT.com?**

Yes. Request an exchange for a product of equal or lesser value within 30 days of delivery.

**Can I get a refund for an online product purchase?**

An unopened product may qualify for a refund of its purchase price, minus shipping and handling. Return it in excellent condition with your dotFIT order number. Clearance items marked “all sales final” are excluded.

**How do I mail a return?**

Pack the item securely, preferably in its original packaging, and include the packing list. Without it, include the order number, order date, billing name, and address. Ship prepaid to dotFIT Returns, 900 Wilmeth Road, McKinney, TX 75069\. Keep your tracking number; C.O.D. shipments are not accepted.

**When will I receive my refund?**

Expect a refund to the original payment method within 30 days after it receives the return. If a manufacturer or dotFIT shipping error caused the return, we will refund shipping costs.

**What if my product arrived damaged or defective?**

Contact dotFIT Customer Service within 30 days of delivery. Have your order number, item number, email address, and phone number ready. For extensive package damage, call 877.436.8348 for instructions.

**Can dotFIT refund a product I bought at a club or another retailer?**

No. Contact the retailer where you purchased it and ask about its return policy.

**Can I get a refund for an online subscription?**

No. Online service subscriptions are nonrefundable. When you cancel an eligible monthly, quarterly, or annual subscription, it runs through the current billing cycle without a prorated refund. Prepaid subscriptions cannot be canceled. The return policy lists My Account \> Orders & Subscriptions \> Cancel; account menus may also show Manage Subscriptions.

**Can I return a wholesale order?**

Unopened wholesale products may be returned within 30 days of the order date if unused, in excellent condition, and accompanied by paperwork. The refund excludes a 10% restocking fee and shipping and handling. Final-sale clearance items are excluded.

**Are completed certifications refundable?**

No. dotFIT does not refund completed certifications.

# The dotFIT Difference

**What makes dotFIT products different?**

Registered Dietitians and nutrition scientists choose ingredients, forms, amounts, and directions using published third-party research. We emphasize transparent labels, ingredient testing, and manufacturing in regularly audited NSF Certified facilities.

**Are dotFIT products third-party tested?**

All its products are third-party tested and the majority carry NSF Certified for Sport certification. Check the individual product for its specific certification.

**Does dotFIT use proprietary blends?**

We do not hide ingredient amounts behind secret proprietary blends. Our practitioner reference guide shares the research behind product ingredients.

**Should I check with a healthcare professional before using supplements?**

Yes. We advise consulting a physician before starting a supplement, nutrition, or exercise program, especially if you take medication. Recommendations should account for your individual health information.

# Athlete Sponsorship

**Who can apply for dotFIT athlete sponsorship?**

We seek athletes who actively compete, train consistently, maintain an authentic social presence, and align with its mission. Selection is limited; applying does not guarantee sponsorship.

**What does a sponsored athlete do?**

Sponsored athletes may represent dotFIT at events, use its products in training, share authentic content, and maintain a professional presence.

**What support might a sponsored athlete receive?**

Depending on the sponsorship level and needs, support may include monthly products, access to dotFIT nutrition experts, and opportunities to appear in dotFIT content.

**How do I apply?**

Complete the online Athlete Sponsorship form. Be ready to share contact details, athletic accomplishments, why you want sponsorship, social or website links, and a photo. Read the form terms before submitting; the application itself is not a sponsorship agreement.

**I am a dotFIT Trainer. Is athlete sponsorship the right program?**

dotFIT points Trainers and coaches to its Fit Pro Rewards program, which is separate from athlete sponsorship.

# Certification

**What does dotFIT Certification cover?**

It teaches fitness professionals to use dotFIT tools for research-based nutrition programs, responsible supplement guidance, behavior change, and client progress tracking. The platform programs are designed by Registered Dietitians.

**How much is certification?**

The certification page lists a \$99.95 purchase with immediate platform access. Check the page for the current price when enrolling.

**Can I earn continuing education credit?**

The page lists 1.6 CEUs for NASM and AFAA, 16 for PTAG, 1.0 for NFPT, and 4.5 Level 1 CPEUs for Registered Dietitians upon completion. Verify current acceptance with your credentialing organization.

**Does certification let me give medical nutrition advice?**

Follow the rules for your profession and state. dotFIT specifically advises checking your state’s nutrition programming regulations.

# Licensed Club Partnerships**

**Who is the licensed partner program for?**

It is designed for clubs and fitness businesses that want to offer dotFIT nutrition programs and products to their members and clients.

**What does a club license include?**

The traditional monthly license option describes unlimited client and member nutrition and supplement plans, staff training and certifications, staff product discounts, rewards eligibility, online sales profit share, and wholesale pricing. Confirm the terms for your selected agreement.

**Is there a minimum wholesale order?**

The partnership page says there is no wholesale order minimum and lists free shipping on wholesale orders greater than \$300 under the traditional monthly license option.

**Can staff earn commissions or discounts?**

The page lists a 40% product discount for eligible team members while employed by the partner. It describes Trainer commission as an optional club-owner incentive, commonly 10% of monthly sales; ask your club which incentives it offers.

**How does a club get started?**

Choose a license option and email berlinda@dotfit.com. dotFIT then sends a setup questionnaire and license agreement. After the signed agreement is returned, the club receives its unique URL and welcome instructions. A welcome webinar can be scheduled.
