# dotFIT supplement program guide

How dotFIT builds a supplement program. It is taken from dotFIT's product summaries and teaching deck (`summaries_teaching.pptx`, 2026), which is dotFIT's current, legal-approved guidance. The deck's product sections are in `pipeline-output/summaries/md/`, and the slide numbers below point into them. Part numbers are in brackets. Flavors of the same product share one entry.

Two parts are **carried over from the previous guide**, because the deck says nothing on them: the screening rules for medical conditions (§2), and the nutrient arithmetic in the overlap check (§9). Both are marked where they appear.

---

## 1. Rules for every program

1. **Foundation first.** A long, healthy, active life rests on three cornerstones, in this order: meeting nutrient needs, regular activity, and a healthy body composition. Every program starts with the foundation (§3). Then add products for the goal (§4–§7), then extras as needs, activity and budget allow. Nobody has to take everything. Start with the essentials and build from there. (Slides 9, 36, 90, 266.)
2. **Everyone gets one complete multivitamin/mineral.** Use one of the multis in §3.1, or Alln1 SuperBlend. **Never both.**
3. **Protein: at least 1 g per lb of lean body mass per day**, spread over 4–5 meals. Food comes first, and a dotFIT protein closes the gap (§3.4). Youth aim for about 1 g per lb of body weight (§7).
4. **Omega-3 depends on fatty-fish intake. Calcium depends on dietary calcium. Vitamin D depends on a blood test.** None of the three is automatic.
5. **Fat-loss aids are temporary.** Stop WeightLoss & LiverSupport, CarbRepel and ThermAccel once the goal is reached. The foundation continues. (Slide 201.)
6. **Stimulants.** ThermAccel and NO7 PreWorkout contain caffeine. Neither is for caffeine-sensitive people. ThermAccel is never combined with another stimulant. (Slides 172, 180.)
7. **Under 18:** use only the youth program (§7).

## 2. Screening (carried over from the previous guide)

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|

| Pregnant, nursing, or trying to conceive | dotFIT has **no prenatal product**. Refer them to a physician. The deck says not to use CollagenComplex or CarbRepel in pregnancy or while nursing, and ThermAccel is not for pregnant or nursing women (slides 97, 184, 180). |

### 3.2 Omega-3: if fatty fish is short (slides 79–81)

- **Omega-3 Fish Oil** [1020] (SuperOmega-3). Take it with a meal.
  - 1 softgel a day if they eat more than 8 but less than 16 oz of fatty fish a week.
  - 2 a day if they eat 8 oz or less.
  - None needed at 16 oz or more a week, unless it is for athletic recovery.
- **Athletes: up to 4 a day** for recovery, to reach an omega-3 index of 8% or more.
- There is no dotFIT vegan omega-3. Vegans and people with fish allergies can use an algae-based product.

### 3.3 Calcium and vitamin D: as needed (slides 67–77)

- **Calcium Complex** [1004] (Super Calcium) is only for when food doesn't reach about 1,000–1,200 mg a day.
  - Fewer than 1 serving of calcium-rich food a day: 2 tablets, split across meals.
  - About 1.5–2.5 servings: 1 tablet with a meal.
  - For reference: 1 cup of milk or 1.5 oz of cheese is about 300 mg. A serving of WheySmooth has about 350 mg, and 2 servings of First String have about 400 mg.
- **Vitamin D-3** [1018]:
  - The target is a blood level (25(OH)D) of at least 30 ng/mL, ideally 40–70 ng/mL, with athletes toward the top.
  - Take it daily, on top of the multi: typically 1,000–2,000 IU (1–2 softgels).
  - Athletes, overweight people, or people with low levels may need up to 4,000 IU a day.
  - Everyone adds 1–2 softgels in high-risk periods, such as flu season or intense training. Athletes add 1–2 year-round.
  - Take it with food and adjust to the blood test.

| Level | Who | Products |

**On GLP-1 drugs** (slides 55, 260, 263):
- The foundation and high protein are essential to limit lean-mass loss. Use LeanMeal, SuperBlend or Active MV, and a dotFIT protein, alongside resistance training.
- The drug itself is the doctor's decision.

## 6. Health, longevity and aging

### 6.1 The five youth-preserving levels (slides 89, 128–129)

| Level | Pill version | SuperBlend version |
|---|---|---|
| 1 – Essentials | Multi (§3.1) + Calcium Complex as needed + Omega-3; Vitamin D-3 as needed | SuperBlend + Calcium Complex as needed + Omega-3; Vitamin D-3 as needed |
| 2 – Living Stronger | + **Antioxidant** [1000] | (already in SuperBlend) |
| 3 – Stronger & Healthier | + **Probiotics** [1017] | (already in SuperBlend) |
| 4 – Looking, Moving & Staying Younger | + **CollagenComplex** [1003] | + CollagenComplex |
| 5 – Thinking Better Longer | + **Brain Health** [1001] | + CollagenComplex + Brain Health |

Extras, when needed:
- **Digestive Enzymes** [1019], for digestion and stomach comfort
- **SleepAid** [1048], for sleep quality and duration

Protein to target applies at every level.

### 6.2 Seniors, 50+ (slides 7–13)

1. **Foundation:** Over 50 MV or SuperBlend, protein (All Natural WheySmooth), omega-3, and calcium and vitamin D as needed.
2. **Health + Performance:** add **Creatine Monohydrate**, about 5 g a day.
3. **Longevity / Lifelong Performance:** add **AminoFormula** (older adults and non-exercisers: see §6.4). The deck's text for this level also names joint and brain support, which means CollagenComplex and Brain Health from §6.1.

### 6.3 Brain and mental health (slides 119–125)

- **Level 1 – Essentials:**
  - A multi or SuperBlend, plus extra Vitamin D-3 to about 40–70 ng/mL.
  - Omega-3: about 1,000 mg EPA+DHA a day for general health, or 1,500–3,000 mg for brain, mood and performance. Each softgel has 600 mg.
- **Level 2 – Next Level:**
  - Add Creatine Monohydrate, about 5 g a day.
  - From about age 45, add **Brain Health** [1001]: 4 tablets a day at 45–55, 8 a day from 56. Take with meals, split morning and evening.

### 6.5 Beyond nutrition (slides 261–264)

- Spend at least 6 months on nutrition and fitness before considering any "anti-aging" therapy.
- Hormone therapy, peptides and GLP-1 drugs are medical decisions for a physician.
- The popular anti-aging ingredients (NMN, NAD+, resveratrol, urolithin A and similar) lack strong evidence in well-nourished people. dotFIT sells none of them.

## 7. Youth (slides 237–254)

**Level 1 – Essentials (ages about 5–17):**
- **Multi:**
  - Under 12: dotFIT has no multi. The deck recommends the ChildLife children's multivitamin, which is **not a dotFIT product**.
  - 12–17: **Active MV** [1005], 1 tablet a day with a meal, or **Alln1 SuperBlend** [8001/8016], 1 scoop.
- **First String** [1371/1372]: daily, to reach about 1 g of protein per lb of body weight. On workout days, 25–35 g of protein 30–40 min before and again right after. Children who avoid animal protein use **Plant Protein** [1300/1301].
- **Omega-3 Fish Oil** [1020]: if they eat fatty fish fewer than 2 times a week (fried fish doesn't count). 1–2 a day, as in §3.2.
- **Calcium Complex** [1004]: only if food and shakes don't reach about 1,000–1,200 mg a day.

**Level 2 – Advanced Athlete (post-pubescent, typically 16+, with a parent's approval):**
- Level 1, plus **Creatine Monohydrate** [1200/1227] daily with meals or shakes.

**Nothing else under 18.** No pre-workouts, fat-loss products, glutamine, or the adult add-ons. Youth also should avoid energy drinks.

## 9. Overlap check: run it on every finished program

The combination rules come from the deck. The nutrient amounts are **carried over from the previous guide**, which took them from the product labels.

| Combination | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| SuperBlend + another multi | Two multis | Drop one |
| SuperBlend + Antioxidant or Probiotics | SuperBlend already contains both | Leave them out of the SuperBlend version (§6.1). Add them only as a deliberate extra. |
| More than one creatine source (Creatine Monohydrate, Creatine Complex 2.5 g a scoop, NO7 PreWorkout 2.5 g a scoop) | The deck allows combining them at the competitor level, but creatine adds up | Count the total creatine and state it |
| NO7 PreWorkout + ThermAccel | Both contain caffeine, and ThermAccel is not used with other stimulants | Never together |
| **Magnesium** from SuperBlend (160 mg) + Calcium Complex (125 mg a tablet) + Active MV (100 mg a tablet) + GlutamineComplex (34 mg a scoop) | Supplemental magnesium is capped at **350 mg a day** | Add it up |
| Glutamine from Creatine Complex (3.5 g a scoop) + Plant Protein (3.5 g) + GlutamineComplex (5 g a scoop) | Adds up | Count it toward about 20 g a day |
| Iron from Women's MV (10 mg) + SuperBlend (8 mg) + Plant Protein (5 mg a scoop) + Active MV (5 mg a tablet) | Total iron should stay near 18 mg a day | Count it. No standalone iron without a blood test. |
| Vitamin D from the multi + Calcium Complex + Vitamin D-3 | Dose to the blood test | Adjust to the 25(OH)D result |
| A vegan program | Fish oil, CollagenComplex and Vitamin D-3 use gelatin. Whey products and LeanMeal contain milk. | Use SuperBlend, Plant Protein and the non-gelatin capsules and tablets |

## 10. Needs dotFIT doesn't cover

Say so plainly instead of substituting a product.

- Prenatal multi
- A multi for children under 12. The deck points to ChildLife, which is not dotFIT.
- Standalone iron, only with a blood test
- Vegan (algae) omega-3
- Hormone therapy, peptides, and weight-loss drugs, which are physician territory
